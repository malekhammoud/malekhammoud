# Sanity vs Hygraph: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Sanity wins for AI-powered content workflows and real-time collaboration, while Hygraph excels at enterprise-grade content modeling and multi-brand management.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Sanity** | **Hygraph** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free | $0 |

| **Core Strength** | Real-time collaboration | Multi-brand management |

## Deep Dive: Sanity

Sanity is a fully customizable, code-first headless CMS built for modern content operations. Its real-time collaborative editing and AI-powered content tools make it ideal for teams that need dynamic, interactive content creation. The Content Lake and Live CDN provide a scalable, real-time content delivery system, while the App SDK allows developers to rapidly build custom content applications. Sanity's modular architecture and powerful APIs make it a top choice for teams that prioritize flexibility and real-time collaboration.

### Standout Features of Sanity
- **Sanity Studio**: Fully customizable headless CMS with real-time collaboration
- **Content Agent**: AI-powered content operations for automated workflows
- **Live CDN**: Simple, scalable, real-time content delivery for global audiences

## Deep Dive: Hygraph

Hygraph is an AI-powered headless CMS designed for enterprise-grade content management. Its robust content architecture and multi-brand support make it ideal for large organizations managing complex content ecosystems. Hygraph's App Marketplace and extensive dev tooling provide flexibility for developers, while its SSO integration and audit logs ensure enterprise-grade security. The platform's worldwide CDN ensures fast, reliable content delivery, making it a strong choice for teams that need scalability and control.

### Standout Features of Hygraph
- **Content Architecture**: Flexible content modeling for complex data structures
- **App Marketplace**: Extensive ecosystem of integrations and extensions
- **Worldwide CDN**: Global content delivery for fast, reliable performance

## The Final Verdict

Choose Sanity if you need AI-powered content workflows, real-time collaboration, and a highly customizable CMS for dynamic content creation.

Choose Hygraph if you require enterprise-grade content modeling, multi-brand management, and robust security for large-scale content operations.
