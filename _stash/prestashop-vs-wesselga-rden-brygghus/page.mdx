## Deep Dive: PrestaShop
PrestaShop is a battle-tested open-source e-commerce platform built for merchants who demand full control over their online store. Its modular architecture supports thousands of extensions, allowing developers to customize everything from checkout flows to inventory management. The platform scales from small shops to enterprise-level operations handling millions in revenue, with dedicated support and extensive documentation for complex deployments.

### Standout Features of PrestaShop
- **Full Data Ownership**: Complete control over customer data, transactions, and store configuration
- **Modular Architecture**: Thousands of modules and themes for unlimited customization possibilities
- **Scalable Infrastructure**: Built to handle growth from hundreds to millions in annual revenue
- **Developer-Friendly**: Open-source codebase with REST API for custom integrations
