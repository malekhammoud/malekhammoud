# Contentful vs Sanity: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Contentful wins for enterprise-scale personalization and AI-driven analytics; Sanity dominates for customizability and real-time collaborative authoring.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Contentful** | **Sanity** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $15/month | Free |
| **Best For** | Enterprise personalization | Custom content platforms |

## Deep Dive: Contentful

Contentful positions itself as a Digital Experience Platform (DXP) with AI-driven analytics baked into the workflow. Its architecture centers on structured content delivery with built-in personalization and experimentation capabilities. The platform excels at orchestrating multi-channel experiences through its visual assembly tools and extensive app ecosystem. Contentful's agentic analytics provide real-time insights directly in the content workflow, making it ideal for teams that need data-driven content optimization at scale.

The platform targets marketing teams and enterprises that require sophisticated audience segmentation and A/B testing capabilities. Contentful's strength lies in its ability to launch and scale rich sites faster than traditional CMS approaches while eliminating busywork through AI automation. Its partnerships and enterprise solutions make it particularly attractive for organizations with complex digital experience requirements and significant content personalization needs.

### Standout Features of Contentful
- **Agentic Analytics**: AI-powered insights delivered directly in your workflow
- **Visual Assembly**: Drag-and-drop experience building at scale
- **AI Automation**: Eliminate repetitive content tasks with built-in AI

## Deep Dive: Sanity

Sanity takes a fundamentally different approach with its fully customizable all-code backend built around real-time collaboration. The platform's Content Lake serves as a content-optimized database designed for AI, automations, and applications. Sanity Studio provides a headless CMS experience that developers can fully customize, while Canvas offers AI-assisted, free-form writing capabilities. The Live CDN ensures simple, scalable, real-time content delivery.

Sanity targets developers and teams that need maximum flexibility in their content architecture. Its strength lies in collaborative authoring workflows and the ability to build custom content applications using the App SDK. The platform's event-driven Compute system and Agent Actions provide built-in, content-aware AI capabilities that integrate seamlessly with the content workflow. This makes Sanity particularly powerful for teams building custom content platforms or requiring sophisticated real-time collaboration features.

### Standout Features of Sanity
- **Real-time Collaboration**: Multi-user editing with instant synchronization
- **Customizable Studio**: Fully hackable headless CMS interface
- **Content Lake**: Optimized database for AI and application workloads

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Contentful if...**
- You need enterprise-grade personalization and A/B testing
- Your team requires AI-driven analytics integrated into workflows
- You're building multi-channel digital experiences at scale

**Choose Sanity if...**
- You need maximum customization and developer flexibility
- Real-time collaborative authoring is critical
- You're building custom content applications or AI-powered platforms
