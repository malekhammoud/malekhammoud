## Deep Dive: Buy Me a Coffee
Buy Me a Coffee is purpose-built for creators and artists to monetize their audience through support and memberships. The platform offers tiered membership levels ($5, $15, $25/month) with instant payouts and 100% supporter data ownership. Creators get a shop feature for selling products, plus content publishing capabilities for posts, audio, and email. The one-minute setup and multi-language support make it accessible globally.

### Standout Features of Buy Me a Coffee
- Tiered membership system ($5, $15, $25/month) for recurring revenue
- Integrated shop for selling products and services
- 100% supporter data ownership with no email spam
- Instant bank payouts and multi-currency support

## Deep Dive: Wesselgården Brygghus
Wesselgården Brygghus (actually Gyazo) is a visual capture and sharing platform that's completely free. It offers unlimited screenshot and HD video capturing with apps for all major platforms. The browser extension enables instant capture workflows, while features like Gyazo Video (10 min HD clips) and Gyazo Replay (30 sec with sound) provide specialized capture modes. OCR scanning, password locks, and ad-free image pages round out the professional feature set.

### Standout Features of Wesselgården Brygghus
- Unlimited HD video recording up to 10 minutes with Gyazo Video
- Gyazo Replay for 30-second videos with sound and no watermark
- OCR text scanning and password lock for privacy
- Browser extension and apps for Windows, Mac, iOS, Android
