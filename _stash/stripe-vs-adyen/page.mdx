## Deep Dive: Stripe
Stripe is a developer-first financial services platform built for rapid deployment and flexible monetization. Its unified API handles everything from simple card payments to complex usage-based billing, tax automation, and revenue recognition. With built-in Sigma analytics, Data Pipeline, and Financial Accounts, Stripe empowers businesses to build, scale, and optimize their payment infrastructure without vendor lock-in on core functionality.

The platform excels at serving startups, SaaS companies, and mid-market businesses that need quick time-to-market and modular feature adoption. Stripe Connect enables marketplace and platform models, while Issuing and Identity services extend beyond traditional payment processing into embedded finance and fraud prevention.

### Standout Features of Stripe
- **Usage-based billing**: Dynamic pricing models for API calls, subscriptions, and metered usage
- **Stripe Sigma**: SQL-based analytics for custom reporting and real-time insights
- **Global Payouts**: Multi-currency disbursements with localized payment methods and compliance

## Deep Dive: Adyen
Adyen is an enterprise-grade payment infrastructure designed for global scale and operational resilience. Built on a single, unified platform, it processes payments across all channels with 99.999% historical uptime and built-in optimizations for conversion, fraud reduction, and cost efficiency. Its one-API approach supports omnichannel commerce, from in-store terminals to mobile apps and e-commerce.

The platform targets enterprise merchants, multinational retailers, and high-volume transaction processors that require payment orchestration, local acquiring, and unified reporting across markets. Adyen's data-driven approach provides granular insights into payment performance, while its direct bank connections eliminate intermediaries for lower costs at scale.

### Standout Features of Adyen
- **99.999% uptime**: Enterprise reliability with built-in redundancy and failover
- **Payment orchestration**: Unified processing across 150+ payment methods and 200+ markets
- **Conversion optimization**: Machine learning-powered routing to maximize approval rates

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Stripe if:**
- You're a startup or growing SaaS company needing rapid integration
- Flexible billing models and modular feature adoption are priorities
- You want built-in analytics and financial services beyond payments

**Choose Adyen if:**
- You're an enterprise processing millions in monthly transactions
- Global payment orchestration and local acquiring are critical
- You need enterprise-grade reliability and direct bank connections
