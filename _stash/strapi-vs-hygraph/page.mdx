## Deep Dive: Strapi
Strapi is the leading open-source headless CMS that gives developers complete control over their content architecture. Built for teams that want to self-host or leverage Strapi Cloud, it offers full API customization, plugin marketplace, and Strapi AI for automated content operations. The platform shines when you need deep customization, on-premise deployment, or want to avoid vendor lock-in.

Developers love Strapi's self-hosted flexibility, allowing deployment on any infrastructure from Vercel to bare metal servers. The Enterprise Edition adds advanced security, dedicated support, and custom SLA options. With Strapi Market, teams can extend functionality through a growing ecosystem of plugins and integrations.

### Standout Features of Strapi
- **Open-source core**: Full control over codebase with MIT license
- **Self-hosting**: Deploy anywhere from cloud to on-premise
- **Strapi AI**: Automated content operations and smart suggestions
- **Enterprise-grade**: Advanced security, SSO, and compliance features

## Deep Dive: Hygraph
Hygraph is an AI-powered headless CMS designed for enterprise teams that need to manage complex content at scale. With built-in AI & Automation, worldwide CDN, and enterprise-grade security, it's built for organizations that prioritize content velocity and team collaboration. The platform excels at handling content of any complexity while maintaining performance and reliability.

Hygraph's App Marketplace and dev tooling make it easy to integrate with existing workflows, while roles & permissions and SSO integration provide enterprise-grade access control. The audit logs and security features ensure compliance, and the worldwide CDN guarantees fast content delivery globally.

### Standout Features of Hygraph
- **AI & Automation**: Built-in content intelligence and workflow automation
- **Worldwide CDN**: Global content delivery with edge caching
- **Enterprise security**: Audit logs, SSO, and granular permissions
- **Content architecture**: Advanced modeling for complex content structures

## The Final Verdict
Choose Strapi if you need open-source flexibility, want to self-host, or require deep customization options. It's perfect for startups and teams that want control over their tech stack and avoid vendor lock-in.

Choose Hygraph if you're an enterprise team that needs AI-powered content workflows, global scalability, and enterprise-grade security out of the box. It's ideal for organizations that prioritize content velocity and want a managed solution with built-in compliance.
