## Deep Dive: Ecwid
Ecwid operates as a headless e-commerce API-first platform, designed to embed seamlessly into existing websites, blogs, and social channels. Its architecture supports REST API endpoints for real-time inventory sync, order management, and dynamic storefront rendering. Ideal for SMBs and agencies needing multi-channel selling without rebuilding their site, Ecwid excels in POS integration and app marketplace extensibility.

### Standout Features of Ecwid
- **Multi-platform API**: Sell natively on Instagram, Facebook, Amazon, and eBay via unified API
- **POS Integration**: Real-time inventory sync between online and brick-and-mortar via Clover, Square, and Poynt
- **App Market**: 100+ integrations for shipping, accounting, CRM, and marketing automation

## Deep Dive: Digistore24
Digistore24 is a conversion-first e-commerce engine built for digital product vendors and affiliate marketers. Its architecture centers on high-converting, multilingual order forms with built-in split-testing, S2S postback tracking, and advanced affiliate attribution. The platform handles VAT/tax compliance across 30+ countries, making it ideal for EU-based digital sellers and high-volume affiliate campaigns.

### Standout Features of Digistore24
- **Multi-step order forms**: Drag-and-drop funnel builder with one-click upsells and order bumps
- **Affiliate marketplace**: 100,000+ active affiliates with real-time commission tracking
- **Compliance automation**: Auto-calculates and remits VAT/MOSS taxes across Europe

## The Final Verdict
Choose Ecwid if you need an embeddable storefront that integrates with your existing site and sells across multiple platforms without rebuilding.

Choose Digistore24 if you're selling digital products and need high-converting funnels, affiliate management, and automated tax compliance out of the box.
