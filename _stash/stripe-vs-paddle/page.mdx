# Stripe vs Paddle: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Stripe wins for maximum flexibility and global reach; Paddle wins for effortless tax compliance and SaaS-specific billing.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Stripe** | **Paddle** |

## Deep Dive: Stripe

Stripe is a financial services platform designed for developers who need maximum control over their payment infrastructure. Its modular architecture allows businesses to build custom billing models, connect to global payout networks, and integrate advanced features like Stripe Sigma for analytics or Stripe Connect for marketplace payments. With support for 135+ currencies and 50+ countries, Stripe excels at handling complex, high-volume payment flows. The platform's API-first design makes it ideal for businesses that want to own their entire payment stack and scale globally without switching providers.

### Standout Features of Stripe
- **Payments & Billing**: Build custom billing models with recurring, one-time, or usage-based pricing
- **Global Payouts & Connect**: Handle marketplace payments and global payouts with built-in compliance
- **Financial Accounts & Issuing**: Create virtual cards and bank accounts for your customers directly through Stripe

## Deep Dive: Paddle

Paddle is a Merchant of Record solution built specifically for SaaS and app businesses that want to eliminate payment complexity. Instead of just processing payments, Paddle handles the entire transaction lifecycle—including tax collection, invoicing, and fraud prevention—on your behalf. This means you don't need to worry about VAT, GST, or sales tax compliance in multiple countries. Paddle's checkout is optimized for conversions with localization in 20+ languages and supports flexible subscription management, making it perfect for SaaS companies that want to focus on product rather than payment infrastructure.

### Standout Features of Paddle
- **Merchant of Record**: Paddle becomes the seller of record, handling all tax compliance and liability
- **SaaS-Optimized Checkout**: High-converting, localized checkout flows designed specifically for subscription businesses
- **Fraud Prevention**: Built-in fraud detection and chargeback protection without additional setup

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Stripe if...**
- You need maximum flexibility and want to build custom payment flows
- You're operating in multiple countries and need granular control over pricing and payouts
- You have the engineering resources to handle tax compliance yourself

**Choose Paddle if...**
- You want to eliminate tax compliance headaches entirely
- You're a SaaS startup that wants to launch quickly without payment infrastructure complexity
- You prefer a "set it and forget it" approach to billing and subscriptions
