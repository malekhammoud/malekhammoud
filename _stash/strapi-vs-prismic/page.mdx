## Deep Dive: Strapi
Strapi is a developer-centric, open-source headless CMS that gives teams complete control over their content infrastructure. Built with Node.js and customizable APIs, it allows developers to create, manage, and distribute content across any platform while maintaining full ownership of the codebase. The self-hosted option and plugin ecosystem make it ideal for teams with technical resources who need flexibility beyond what SaaS solutions offer.

### Standout Features of Strapi
- **Open-source architecture**: Self-host or use Strapi Cloud with full codebase access
- **Strapi AI**: AI-powered content generation and workflow automation
- **Enterprise Edition**: Advanced security, SSO, and dedicated support for large organizations

## Deep Dive: Prismic
Prismic is a marketer-focused headless CMS and visual page builder that combines content management with landing page creation. Its slice-based architecture and drag-and-drop interface allow non-technical teams to build, edit, and publish pages without developer assistance. With built-in localization, SEO controls, and one-click sync, Prismic streamlines the entire content workflow from creation to deployment.

### Standout Features of Prismic
- **Visual Page Builder**: Real-time editing with live preview and drag-and-drop functionality
- **Slice Variations**: Reusable content components with multiple layout options
- **Automatic TypeScript**: Auto-generated type definitions for seamless integration

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Strapi if:**
- You need complete control over your CMS infrastructure
- Your team has strong technical resources for customization
- You want open-source flexibility with self-hosting options

**Choose Prismic if:**
- You're building landing pages that marketers need to edit
- Your team lacks technical resources for content management
- You want visual editing without involving developers
