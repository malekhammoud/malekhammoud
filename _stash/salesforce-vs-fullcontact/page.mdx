## Deep Dive: Salesforce
Salesforce operates as an AI-driven CRM platform with advanced pricing capabilities built on a unified data model. The platform integrates pricing management, execution, and design tools into a cohesive ecosystem that supports complex enterprise workflows. Its architecture enables seamless connections with quoting, order capture, and other CRM components through standardized APIs and data synchronization protocols.

The platform targets large enterprises and organizations requiring sophisticated pricing strategies, volume discounts, and tiered pricing models. Salesforce's pricing waterfall feature provides transparent discount application, while its pricing rules engine supports bundle discounts and volume-based pricing structures. The unified pricing platform ensures compliance and accuracy across all pricing operations.

### Standout Features of Salesforce
- **Pricing Designer**: Creates pricebooks and entries with drag-and-drop interface
- **Pricing Rules Engine**: Implements volume, tiered, and bundle discounts automatically
- **Pricing Waterfall**: Provides transparent visibility into discount application logic

## Deep Dive: FullContact
FullContact focuses on B2B lead identification through deterministic identity resolution and cross-device tracking. The platform identifies website visitors in real-time, mapping customer journeys and providing professional contact information for high-intent leads. Its architecture leverages browsing analytics and device recognition to build comprehensive visitor profiles.

The tool targets B2B companies looking to convert anonymous website traffic into actionable sales leads. FullContact's real-time alerts notify sales teams when target accounts visit their site, enabling immediate outreach during the evaluation phase. The platform's identity resolution capabilities ensure accurate visitor matching across multiple touchpoints and devices.

### Standout Features of FullContact
- **Real-time Visitor Identification**: Detects and identifies website visitors instantly
- **Cross-device Recognition**: Tracks users across multiple devices and sessions
- **Professional Contact Information**: Provides verified business contact details for identified leads

**Choose Salesforce if:**
