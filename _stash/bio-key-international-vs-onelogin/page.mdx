## Deep Dive: BIO-key International
BIO-key PortalGuard is built around identity-bound biometrics, offering over 15 MFA options including fingerprint, facial, and iris recognition. Its architecture supports on-premises, cloud, or hybrid deployments, making it ideal for organizations requiring hardware-backed authentication. The platform excels in high-security environments where passwordless access and identity assurance are critical, such as government, healthcare, and finance.

### Standout Features of BIO-key International
- **Identity-Bound Biometrics**: Permanent biometric templates tied to user identity, not devices
- **15+ MFA Options**: Including passkeys, FIDO2, and mobile push for flexible authentication
- **Self-Service Password Reset**: Reduces help desk burden while maintaining security

## Deep Dive: OneLogin
OneLogin is a cloud-native IAM platform designed for enterprise workforce identity management. Its extensible architecture supports human and machine identities across customers, partners, and employees. The platform's strength lies in its comprehensive SSO capabilities, advanced directory services, and AI-driven security features like Vigilance AI for threat detection. OneLogin scales effortlessly for large organizations with complex identity ecosystems.

### Standout Features of OneLogin
- **SmartFactor Authentication**: AI-powered risk-based authentication
- **Vigilance AI**: Continuous threat monitoring and anomaly detection
- **Identity Lifecycle Management**: Automated provisioning/deprovisioning for workforce agility
