# Write.as vs OnTheGoSystems: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Write.as wins for solo writers and minimalist bloggers; OnTheGoSystems dominates for multilingual WordPress sites.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Write.as** | **OnTheGoSystems** |
| :--- | :--- | --- |
| **Starting Price** | $6 per month | €39 |
| **Best For** | Minimalist blogging | Multilingual WordPress |
| **Core Strength** | Distraction-free writing | WordPress translation |

## Deep Dive: Write.as
Write.as is a minimalist blogging platform built for writers who value simplicity and focus. Its architecture strips away complex content management features, offering a clean interface for publishing text-based content. With support for custom domains, ActivityPub integration, and command-line publishing, it caters to technical writers and indie creators who want full control without bloat. The platform emphasizes privacy with password-protected and private blogs, making it ideal for personal or sensitive content.

### Standout Features of Write.as
- **ActivityPub integration**: Publish directly to the Fediverse, reaching decentralized audiences.
- **Command-line publishing**: Write and publish via terminal for developers who prefer keyboard-driven workflows.
- **Full design customization**: Tailor your blog’s appearance without being locked into rigid templates.

## Deep Dive: OnTheGoSystems
OnTheGoSystems powers WPML, a WordPress plugin designed for building and managing multilingual websites. Its architecture integrates deeply with WordPress, supporting translation of pages, posts, custom types, taxonomies, menus, and theme texts. With compatibility across all WordPress themes and plugins, it’s a robust solution for businesses and developers managing global content. Features like AI translation with human accuracy and professional translation service integration make it scalable for enterprise use.

### Standout Features of OnTheGoSystems
- **Multilingual SEO capabilities**: Optimize translated content for search engines in multiple languages.
- **AI translation with human accuracy**: Combine machine efficiency with human oversight for high-quality translations.
- **Flexible translation management**: Assign, review, and manage translations across teams with granular control.

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Write.as if...**
- You’re a solo writer or blogger who values simplicity and distraction-free publishing.
- You need a minimalist platform with technical features like ActivityPub and CLI publishing.
- Privacy and clean design are top priorities.

**Choose OnTheGoSystems if...**
- You’re running a multilingual WordPress site for a business or enterprise.
- You need deep integration with WordPress and robust translation management tools.
- SEO and scalability for global audiences are critical to your strategy.
