## Deep Dive: Salesforce
Salesforce delivers a unified AI, data, and Customer 360 platform built for Agentic Enterprises where humans and AI agents collaborate seamlessly. Its pricing architecture enables sophisticated price management, execution, and waterfall transparency across quoting, order capture, and CPQ workflows. The platform excels at complex pricing strategies including volume discounts, tiered pricing, and bundle configurations through its Pricing Designer and Rules engine.

### Standout Features of Salesforce
- Unified pricing platform with consistent, compliant execution
- Pricing Designer for dynamic pricebook creation
- Transparent pricing waterfall for audit-ready discount tracking

## Deep Dive: Freshworks
Freshworks provides enterprise-grade, AI-assisted service software designed for exceptional customer and employee experiences at SMB scale. The platform bundles multichannel support, Freddy AI capabilities, and comprehensive ITSM features including incident/problem/change management, service catalog, and SLA management. Freshworks delivers advanced ticket routing, automation, and analytics dashboards within an accessible pricing model.

### Standout Features of Freshworks
- Freddy AI for intelligent ticket classification and response
- Comprehensive ITSM suite with change and problem management
- Multichannel support with unified analytics dashboards

## The Final Verdict
Choose Salesforce if you need enterprise-scale AI CRM with sophisticated pricing management, unified data platforms, and Agentic Enterprise capabilities.

Choose Freshworks if you're an SMB seeking affordable, feature-rich service management with AI automation, ITSM workflows, and multichannel support.
