# Freshworks vs FullContact: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Freshworks dominates for enterprise service management, while FullContact excels at real-time lead identification and visitor intelligence.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Freshworks** | **FullContact** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $19/agent/month, billed annually | $99 |
| **Best For** | Enterprise service management | Lead identification & visitor intelligence |

## Deep Dive: Freshworks
Freshworks delivers enterprise-grade service software built for scale, featuring Freddy AI for intelligent automation and ticket routing. The platform's architecture supports multichannel support with integrated knowledge bases, SLAs, and service catalogs. Its analytics dashboards and custom reporting provide deep operational insights, while change management and problem management workflows ensure enterprise compliance. The modular design allows teams to deploy specific capabilities without over-provisioning.

Freshworks targets mid-market to enterprise organizations requiring comprehensive service management. The platform's strength lies in its unified approach to customer and employee experience, combining incident management, automation, and AI assistance in a single interface. With advanced ticket routing and SLA management, teams can maintain service level agreements while scaling operations across departments.

### Standout Features of Freshworks
- **Freddy AI**: Machine learning-powered automation for ticket classification and routing
- **Service Catalog**: Self-service portal for employee requests and knowledge base integration
- **Advanced Analytics**: Real-time dashboards with custom reporting and trend analysis

## Deep Dive: FullContact
FullContact's Acumen Lead Identification platform specializes in real-time visitor intelligence through deterministic identity resolution. The architecture maps customer journeys across devices, providing businesses with professional contact information and browsing analytics. Real-time alerts notify sales teams when high-intent visitors engage with specific content, while cross-device recognition ensures accurate lead scoring. The platform's identity resolution engine creates persistent customer profiles from fragmented data sources.

FullContact serves B2B organizations focused on capturing inbound demand and accelerating sales cycles. Its core strength lies in identifying anonymous website visitors and providing actionable intelligence at the moment of evaluation. By combining browsing analytics with professional contact data, teams can prioritize outreach to prospects demonstrating active interest in their solutions.

### Standout Features of FullContact
- **Deterministic Identity Resolution**: Accurate visitor identification across multiple devices and sessions
- **Real-Time Journey Mapping**: Track prospect behavior from first touch to conversion
- **Professional Contact Enrichment**: Access verified business contact information for identified visitors

## The Final Verdict
Choose Freshworks if you need comprehensive service management with AI automation, multi-department workflows, and enterprise-grade compliance features. The platform excels at scaling customer support operations while maintaining service quality through intelligent routing and analytics.

Choose FullContact if your primary goal is identifying and converting anonymous website visitors into sales opportunities. The platform's real-time intelligence and contact enrichment capabilities make it ideal for B2B organizations focused on inbound lead generation and sales acceleration.
