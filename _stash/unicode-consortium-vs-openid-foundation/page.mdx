## Deep Dive: Unicode Consortium
The Unicode Consortium maintains the Unicode Standard, the foundational character encoding system that enables global text representation across platforms. Their tiered membership structure ($50,000 Corporate down to $35 Student) funds the development and maintenance of this critical infrastructure. Voting members directly influence the evolution of character sets, emoji approval, and script encoding decisions that affect billions of devices worldwide.

### Standout Features of Unicode Consortium
- **Universal Character Set**: Governs over 150 scripts and 150,000+ characters
- **Technical Work Participation**: Direct involvement in Unicode Technical Committees
- **Tax-Deductible Support**: 501(c)(3) status makes contributions tax-deductible in the US

## Deep Dive: OpenID Foundation
The OpenID Foundation oversees the development and promotion of open identity standards that power modern authentication systems. While specific pricing isn't publicly listed, their governance model brings together industry leaders to maintain protocols like OpenID Connect, OAuth 2.0, and FIDO2. These standards form the backbone of secure, decentralized identity verification across web and mobile applications.

### Standout Features of OpenID Foundation
- **Open Identity Standards**: Maintains protocols used by billions of authentication requests daily
- **Industry Collaboration**: Brings together tech giants, financial institutions, and government agencies
- **Security Focus**: Develops FIDO2 and other cutting-edge authentication mechanisms

## The Final Verdict
Choose Unicode Consortium if you need to influence global character encoding standards, require voting rights in technical decisions, or want tax-deductible support for universal text representation.

Choose OpenID Foundation if you're building authentication systems, need to participate in identity protocol governance, or want to ensure compatibility with modern security standards.
