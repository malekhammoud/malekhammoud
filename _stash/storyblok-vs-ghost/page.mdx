# Storyblok vs Ghost: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** **Storyblok wins for enterprise-grade headless CMS needs with visual editing, while Ghost dominates for content-first publishers and subscription businesses.**

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Storyblok** | **Ghost** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free | $9/month |
| **Best For** | Visual content architecture | Subscription publishing |
| **Core Strength** | Visual editor + API | Built-in memberships |

## Deep Dive: Storyblok

Storyblok is a headless CMS built for developers and content teams who need visual control over their content architecture. Its visual editor provides real-time preview across all devices, while the API-first approach delivers content via GraphQL or REST to any frontend framework. With 100GB monthly traffic and 100k AI credits on the free tier, it's designed for teams scaling digital experiences across multiple channels. The component-based content modeling system makes it ideal for complex, multi-brand websites requiring structured content delivery.

### Standout Features of Storyblok
- **Visual Editor**: Real-time preview with inline editing across all devices
- **Component System**: Reusable content blocks with nested components
- **Multi-channel Delivery**: API-first architecture serving web, mobile, and IoT
- **Rich Integrations**: Native Shopify, Algolia, and marketing tool connectors
- **Collaboration Tools**: Role-based permissions and workflow management

## Deep Dive: Ghost

Ghost is a purpose-built publishing platform for professional content creators and subscription businesses. Unlike traditional CMS options, it comes with built-in membership management, native newsletter functionality, and integrated analytics. The platform handles everything from content creation to payment processing, making it a complete solution for digital publishers. With its markdown-based editor and dynamic cards system, Ghost prioritizes content creation speed while maintaining professional output quality.

### Standout Features of Ghost
- **Membership System**: Native subscriptions with tiered access control
- **Built-in Newsletters**: Email campaigns integrated with content publishing
- **Dynamic Cards**: Interactive content elements (forms, galleries, embeds)
- **Native Analytics**: Real-time performance metrics and audience insights
- **SEO Optimized**: Automatic sitemap generation and structured data

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Storyblok if:**
- You need visual content modeling for complex websites
- Your team requires API-first content delivery to multiple channels
- You're building multi-brand or enterprise digital experiences
- You want component-based reusable content structures

**Choose Ghost if:**
- You're running a subscription-based publication or membership site
- You need integrated newsletter and payment processing
- You want a complete publishing platform without third-party tools
- Your focus is content creation speed with built-in monetization
