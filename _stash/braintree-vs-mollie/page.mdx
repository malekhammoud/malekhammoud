# Braintree vs Mollie: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Braintree wins for enterprise-scale global payments; Mollie excels for European SMBs needing simplicity.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Braintree** | **Mollie** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | 1.9% + 0.30 EUR | €24.95 |
| **Best For** | Global enterprise payments | European SMB simplicity |
| **Core Strength** | Multi-currency & PayPal integration | Developer-friendly European focus |

## Deep Dive: Braintree
Braintree, backed by PayPal, delivers enterprise-grade payment processing with global reach. Its architecture supports 50+ currencies and native PayPal/Venmo integration, making it ideal for businesses scaling internationally. The platform's PCI compliance and adaptive fraud management provide enterprise-level security without the overhead. Braintree's mobile SDKs and comprehensive APIs enable seamless integration across web, iOS, and Android platforms, while its recurring billing system handles complex subscription models with add-ons and discounts.

### Standout Features of Braintree
- **Global Currency Support**: Process payments in 50+ currencies with real-time conversion
- **PayPal Ecosystem Integration**: Native support for PayPal, Venmo, and PayPal Credit
- **Advanced Fraud Tools**: Adaptive fraud management with customizable rules and risk scoring
- **Mobile-First SDKs**: Native iOS, Android, and React Native SDKs for frictionless mobile payments

## Deep Dive: Mollie
Mollie focuses on European markets with a developer-centric approach and transparent pricing. Its architecture emphasizes simplicity and speed, offering optimized checkout flows and fast funding options. The platform's RESTful API and comprehensive documentation make integration straightforward for developers. Mollie's strength lies in its European payment method coverage (SEPA, iDEAL, Bancontact) and local acquiring, which reduces cross-border fees for European businesses. The €24.95 base pricing provides predictable costs for SMBs, while the platform handles everything from online payments to in-person transactions through its iOS app.

### Standout Features of Mollie
- **European Payment Methods**: Native support for SEPA, iDEAL, Bancontact, and other regional methods
- **Transparent Pricing**: Fixed €24.95 base + per-transaction fees with no hidden costs
- **Fast Funding**: Next-day payouts available for eligible merchants
- **Developer-First API**: Comprehensive documentation with sandbox environments and webhook testing

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Braintree if:**
- You need global payment processing across multiple currencies
- PayPal/Venmo integration is critical to your business model
- You're processing high-volume enterprise transactions
- You require advanced fraud prevention and PCI compliance

**Choose Mollie if:**
- Your business primarily serves European markets
- You want predictable, transparent pricing without volume commitments
- You need regional payment methods like iDEAL or SEPA
- You're an SMB prioritizing developer experience and fast payouts
