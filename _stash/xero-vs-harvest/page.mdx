# Xero vs Harvest: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Xero dominates for comprehensive accounting automation, while Harvest excels at lightweight time tracking and client billing.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Xero** | **Harvest** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $3.75 USD per month | Free |
| **Best For** | Full-stack accounting | Time tracking & billing |
| **Core Strength** | Automated financial workflows | Simple time-to-invoice pipeline |

## Deep Dive: Xero
Xero is a full-featured accounting platform built for small businesses that need real-time financial visibility. Its architecture connects directly to bank feeds, automates invoicing, and syncs with third-party apps via a robust API. The platform handles everything from payroll to inventory, making it a one-stop financial operations hub. Mobile access and Syft-powered analytics add flexibility for on-the-go decision-making.

### Standout Features of Xero
- **Bank Connections**: Automatic transaction imports with reconciliation
- **Inventory Management**: Built-in stock tracking with cost of goods sold
- **Payroll Integration**: Native payroll processing for multi-employee teams

## Deep Dive: Harvest
Harvest is a streamlined time tracking and invoicing tool designed for professional service teams. Its architecture focuses on simplicity—track hours, generate invoices, and monitor project profitability without the overhead of full accounting software. SAML-based SSO and activity logs make it enterprise-friendly, while budget monitoring helps teams stay on target. The free tier makes it accessible for freelancers and small teams.

### Standout Features of Harvest
- **Time Tracking**: Clean, intuitive interface for logging hours
- **Budget Monitoring**: Real-time budget vs. actual tracking
- **Profitability Reporting**: Project-level margin insights

## The Final Verdict
Choose Xero if you need comprehensive accounting automation, bank reconciliation, and inventory management in one platform.

Choose Harvest if you want simple time tracking, client billing, and profitability insights without the complexity of full accounting software.
