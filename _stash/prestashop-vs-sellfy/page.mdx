## Deep Dive: PrestaShop
PrestaShop is a self-hosted, open-source e-commerce platform built for businesses that demand complete control over their online store. With thousands of modules and themes, it offers unmatched flexibility to scale from hundreds to millions in revenue. Its architecture supports full data ownership, making it ideal for enterprises that need to integrate custom APIs, third-party systems, or handle complex workflows. The platform’s modular design allows developers to extend functionality without limitations, while its dedicated support team ensures reliability for high-growth operations.

### Standout Features of PrestaShop
- **Full Data Ownership**: Host your store on your own infrastructure with complete control over data and compliance.
- **Modular Architecture**: Thousands of modules and themes for endless customization and scalability.
- **Enterprise Scalability**: Designed to handle high traffic and revenue growth without performance bottlenecks.

## Deep Dive: Sellfy
Sellfy is a SaaS-based e-commerce platform tailored for creators, artists, and small businesses who need a fast, no-code solution to sell digital products, subscriptions, and physical goods. Its intuitive store builder allows users to launch a fully functional storefront in minutes, with built-in marketing tools, automated workflows, and secure payment integrations. Sellfy’s focus on simplicity and speed makes it perfect for creators who prioritize ease of use over deep customization. The platform’s subscription model includes unlimited products, email marketing, and 24/7 support, ensuring a hassle-free experience.

### Standout Features of Sellfy
- **Code-Free Store Builder**: Launch a store in minutes with customizable templates and no technical skills required.
- **Multi-Product Support**: Sell digital downloads, print-on-demand products, subscriptions, and physical goods in one place.
- **Built-In Marketing Tools**: Automated marketing, email campaigns, and secure payments to drive sales effortlessly.

## The Final Verdict
- **Choose PrestaShop if**: You need a fully customizable, self-hosted platform for enterprise-scale e-commerce with complex integrations and data ownership.
- **Choose Sellfy if**: You’re a creator or small business looking for a quick, no-code solution to sell digital products, subscriptions, or physical goods without technical overhead.
