# Sanity vs Ghost: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Sanity wins for developers building custom AI-powered content systems; Ghost wins for content creators launching subscription newsletters.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Sanity** | **Ghost** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free | $9/month |
| **Best For** | Custom AI content ops | Subscription publishing |
| **Core Strength** | Programmable content lake | Built-in monetization |

## Deep Dive: Sanity
Sanity is a fully customizable, code-first backend built for AI, automations, and applications that need to author and render content at scale. Its Content Lake database and Sanity Studio provide developers with a structured, real-time content API that can be shaped to any data model. With features like Content Agent, Canvas AI writing, and Compute event handlers, Sanity is engineered for teams building intelligent content workflows and dynamic applications.

The platform excels when you need full control over content architecture and want to integrate AI directly into your content operations. Its App SDK enables rapid development of custom content apps, while the Live CDN ensures global, real-time content delivery. Sanity is ideal for development teams, agencies, and enterprises that prioritize flexibility, scalability, and programmatic content management.

### Standout Features of Sanity
- **Content Agent**: Built-in AI for automating content operations and workflows
- **Canvas**: AI-assisted, free-form writing environment with real-time collaboration
- **Compute**: Event-driven functions that trigger on content changes for powerful automations

## Deep Dive: Ghost
Ghost is a publishing platform designed for professional creators who want to build a business around their content. It combines a simple site builder with powerful creator tools, enabling you to publish articles, send newsletters, and manage paid memberships—all from a single dashboard. With native analytics, rich media support, and integrations with popular apps, Ghost streamlines the workflow for independent publishers and small teams.

The platform's strength lies in its all-in-one approach to content monetization. Built-in newsletters, membership management, and promotional tools make it easy to launch and grow a subscription-based audience without needing separate services. Ghost is perfect for bloggers, journalists, and content entrepreneurs who want a straightforward path to publishing and monetization without heavy technical overhead.

### Standout Features of Ghost
- **Built-in Newsletters**: Send email campaigns directly to your subscribers
- **Membership Management**: Handle paid subscriptions and content access controls
- **Rich Media & Dynamic Cards**: Embed interactive content and multimedia directly in posts

## The Final Verdict
Choose **Sanity** if you're a developer building custom AI-powered content systems, need programmable content workflows, or want full control over your content architecture and delivery.

Choose **Ghost** if you're a creator launching a subscription newsletter, need built-in monetization tools, or want an all-in-one platform for publishing and audience growth without technical complexity.
