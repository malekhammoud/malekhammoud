## Deep Dive: Jivo
JivoChat delivers a unified customer service platform with 15+ communication channels integrated into a single dashboard. Its architecture supports real-time omnichannel engagement through web chat, mobile SDK, social messengers, and telephony. The platform's lightweight design makes it ideal for SMBs seeking comprehensive customer communication without infrastructure overhead.

Jivo's strength lies in its accessibility and breadth of integrations. The free tier includes core features like live chat, WhatsApp Business, and basic CRM functionality. The platform's API enables custom integrations, while pre-built connectors for Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, and Viber provide immediate multi-channel presence. The AI agent and assistant features enhance automation capabilities for growing teams.

### Standout Features of Jivo
- **WhatsApp Business Integration** - Direct customer engagement through the world's most popular messaging platform
- **Multi-channel Dashboard** - Unified interface for managing conversations across 15+ communication channels
- **Mobile SDK** - Native app integration for customer support within mobile applications

## Deep Dive: Dialpad
Dialpad leverages AI-powered cloud architecture to transform contact center operations with advanced call analytics and workforce optimization. The platform's agentic AI provides real-time transcription, sentiment analysis, and coaching during live calls. Its distributed system architecture supports enterprise-scale operations with global redundancy and compliance features.

Dialpad's core advantage is its sophisticated call management capabilities. Features like call whisper, barging, and takeover enable supervisors to guide agents in real-time. The automatic call distribution and interactive voice response systems optimize call routing efficiency. Workforce management tools provide analytics for staffing optimization and performance tracking across large teams.

### Standout Features of Dialpad
- **AI-Powered Sentiment Analysis** - Real-time emotional intelligence during customer interactions
- **Call Whisper & Barge** - Supervisor coaching capabilities without customer awareness
- **Automatic Call Distribution** - Intelligent routing based on agent skills and availability

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Jivo if...**
- You're an SMB needing omnichannel customer support at no cost
- You require integration with social media and messaging platforms
- You want a simple, unified dashboard for multiple communication channels

**Choose Dialpad if...**
