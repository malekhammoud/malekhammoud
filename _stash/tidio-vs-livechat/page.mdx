# Tidio vs LiveChat: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Tidio wins for AI-powered automation with Lyro, while LiveChat dominates with enterprise-grade features and unlimited users.

## Deep Dive: Tidio
Tidio is an AI-first customer service platform built for SMBs seeking automation without complexity. Its Lyro AI Agent handles up to 67% of inquiries autonomously, reducing support team workload while maintaining conversational quality. The platform integrates live chat, helpdesk, and multichannel support into a unified interface, making it ideal for e-commerce stores and small teams that need smart automation without enterprise overhead.

The architecture centers around Flows for visual workflow automation and a shared inbox that consolidates conversations from multiple channels. With native integrations to Shopify and WordPress, Tidio excels at e-commerce customer support where AI can handle repetitive order tracking and FAQ responses, freeing human agents for complex issues.

### Standout Features of Tidio
- **Lyro AI Agent**: Handles 67% of customer inquiries autonomously with contextual understanding
- **Visual Flows**: Drag-and-drop automation builder for complex conversation routing
- **E-commerce Native**: Shopify integration with order tracking and cart recovery automation

## Deep Dive: LiveChat
LiveChat is enterprise-grade customer service software designed for B2B SaaS companies and large e-commerce operations. The platform offers unlimited users and chat history, making it scalable for growing teams. Its comprehensive feature set includes advanced reporting, agent performance tracking, SMS integration, and Apple Messages for Business, providing a complete customer communication solution.

The architecture supports proactive engagement with canned responses, chat summaries, and sentiment analysis. LiveChat's staffing prediction and work scheduler optimize team efficiency, while on-demand reporting provides deep insights into customer interactions. The platform's unlimited campaigns and full widget customization make it suitable for complex multi-brand or multi-region deployments.

### Standout Features of LiveChat
- **Unlimited Users**: Scale your support team without per-seat licensing costs
- **Enterprise Analytics**: Advanced reporting with agent performance metrics and staffing prediction
- **Multi-Channel Integration**: SMS, Apple Messages, and proactive chat capabilities

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Tidio if:**
- You need AI automation to handle repetitive customer inquiries
- Your team is small to medium-sized with limited budget
- You're running an e-commerce store on Shopify or WordPress

**Choose LiveChat if:**
- You require unlimited users and enterprise-level scalability
- Advanced reporting and analytics are critical for your operations
- You need comprehensive multi-channel support including SMS and Apple Messages
