# Zoho vs FullContact: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Zoho wins for SMBs needing custom CRM workflows; FullContact dominates for enterprise lead identification and visitor tracking.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Zoho** | **FullContact** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $5 | $99 |
| **Best For** | Custom CRM workflows | Visitor lead identification |
| **Core Strength** | Modular, scalable platform | Real-time visitor analytics |

## Deep Dive: Zoho

Zoho Creator delivers a low-code platform engineered for rapid application development and deployment. Its tiered architecture scales from 10 modules with 10 fields each in Starter to 200 modules with 300 fields per module in Scale, supporting complex data models without code rewrites. Workflow rules max at 2,500 per org with 2,000 active concurrently, enabling sophisticated automation pipelines. Integration hooks into Zoho Analytics, Zoho Campaigns, and Mail 360 provide native data flow without middleware. Dashboards are unlimited from Growth tier up, and custom reports scale from 100 to unlimited, giving teams real-time visibility into KPIs. Email notifications throttle from 5 per action in Starter to 500 per user/day in Scale, balancing notification volume with deliverability.

### Standout Features of Zoho
- **Modular Architecture:** 10→25→200 modules across tiers for flexible data modeling
- **Workflow Engine:** 2,500 rules/org with granular per-action email limits
- **Native Integrations:** Direct connectors to Zoho Analytics, Campaigns, and Mail 360

## Deep Dive: FullContact

FullContact Acumen specializes in real-time visitor identification and lead enrichment at the point of engagement. Its deterministic identity resolution maps anonymous site traffic to professional contact profiles, surfacing high-intent leads the moment they evaluate products. Cross-device recognition stitches user journeys across sessions, while browsing analytics provide granular behavioral data. Real-time alerts notify sales teams instantly when target accounts hit the site, enabling immediate outreach. Professional contact information enrichment adds firmographic and demographic data without manual research, accelerating lead qualification and routing.

### Standout Features of FullContact
- **Visitor Identification:** Maps anonymous traffic to verified professional profiles
- **Cross-Device Journey Tracking:** Unified user behavior across sessions and devices
- **Real-Time Alerts:** Instant notifications for high-intent account activity

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Zoho if:**
- You need a customizable CRM with complex workflow automation
- Budget constraints require a low-cost, scalable platform
- Native integrations to Zoho ecosystem are critical

**Choose FullContact if:**
- Real-time visitor identification and lead enrichment are priority
- You operate in B2B with high-value account targeting
- Cross-device journey tracking and behavioral analytics drive sales strategy
