# WorkOS vs Ory: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** WorkOS wins for rapid enterprise integration with generous free tier; Ory excels for full-stack IAM flexibility and granular control.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **WorkOS** | **Ory** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free for the first 1 million active users; $2500/month for each additional million. | $770/year |
| **Best For** | Enterprise SSO integration | Modular IAM deployment |
| **Core Strength** | Fast enterprise readiness | Customizable security & compliance |

## Deep Dive: WorkOS

WorkOS is engineered for developers who need to bolt enterprise-grade authentication onto existing apps without rebuilding their auth stack. Its architecture is API-first, with pre-built integrations for SSO providers like Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace, plus Directory Sync for automated user provisioning. The free tier up to 1M MAU is unmatched for startups targeting enterprise customers, and its Admin Portal and Audit Logs make compliance reporting painless. Ideal for B2B SaaS targeting mid-market and enterprise clients who demand SSO.

### Standout Features of WorkOS
- **Enterprise SSO**: Native support for SAML, OAuth2, and OIDC with minimal config.
- **Directory Sync**: One-way sync from HR systems to keep user data fresh.
- **Audit Logs**: Immutable logs for compliance and security monitoring.

## Deep Dive: Ory

Ory takes a modular, Kubernetes-native approach to IAM, letting teams pick and choose components like OAuth2/OIDC provider, permissions APIs, and self-service UI. Its architecture supports multi-region deployments with data residency, making it a strong fit for global enterprises with strict compliance needs. Ory's pricing is usage-based and predictable, and its Bring Your Own UI and Themeable Self-Service Pages give full control over the user experience. Best for organizations that need fine-grained permissions, machine-to-machine auth, and compliance certifications baked in.

### Standout Features of Ory
- **Fine-Grained Permissions API**: Attribute-based access control (ABAC) for complex policies.
- **Multi-Region Deployments**: Data residency compliance across global regions.
- **Bring Your Own UI**: Full customization of auth flows without vendor lock-in.

## The Final Verdict

Choose **WorkOS** if:
- You need to add SSO to an existing app fast
- Your target customers are enterprises demanding compliance
- You want a generous free tier for scaling

Choose **Ory** if:
- You require modular, self-hosted IAM components
- You need multi-region data residency and ABAC
- You want full control over UI and compliance posture
