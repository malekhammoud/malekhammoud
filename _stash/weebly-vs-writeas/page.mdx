## Deep Dive: Weebly
Weebly is a full-stack website builder engineered for entrepreneurs and small businesses needing an all-in-one solution. Its drag-and-drop editor, built-in eCommerce stack, and integrated marketing tools make it a powerhouse for creating professional websites without code. With real-time analytics, inventory management, and payment processing baked in, Weebly handles the entire lifecycle from design to sales.

The platform's architecture supports customizable templates, responsive design, and SEO optimization out of the box. Ideal for users who want to launch a store, blog, or portfolio quickly while maintaining control over branding and customer experience.

### Standout Features of Weebly
- **eCommerce Suite**: Integrated payment processing, inventory management, and shipping tools
- **Marketing Automation**: Email campaigns, coupon creation, and customer review management
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time site stats and visitor insights

## Deep Dive: Write.as
Write.as is a purpose-built, minimalist blogging platform designed for writers who value simplicity and privacy. Stripped of bloated CMS features, it focuses on fast, distraction-free publishing with markdown support and clean typography. The platform's API-first approach enables command-line publishing, email publishing, and deep integration with external tools.

Write.as emphasizes user privacy with password-protected and private blogs, while ActivityPub integration allows seamless cross-posting to the fediverse. Its lightweight architecture makes it ideal for technical writers, journalists, and creators who want to publish without the overhead of traditional CMSs.

### Standout Features of Write.as
- **Privacy-First**: Password-protected and private blog options
- **API & CLI Publishing**: Command-line and email publishing for developers
- **Fediverse Integration**: ActivityPub support for decentralized sharing

## The Final Verdict
Choose **Weebly** if:
- You need a full-featured website with eCommerce capabilities
- You want drag-and-drop design without coding
- You require built-in marketing and analytics tools

Choose **Write.as** if:
- You prioritize distraction-free writing and publishing
- You value privacy and minimalist design
- You need API access for automation and integrations
