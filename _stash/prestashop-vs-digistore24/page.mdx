## Deep Dive: PrestaShop
PrestaShop is a self-hosted, open-source e-commerce platform built for merchants who want complete control over their store's architecture, data, and branding. It offers thousands of modules and themes, enabling deep customization for any business model. The platform scales from small shops to enterprise-level operations with millions in revenue, backed by a dedicated support team and extensive documentation. Ideal for developers and businesses wanting full ownership and no vendor lock-in.

### Standout Features of PrestaShop
- **Full data ownership**: No third-party hosting means you control every byte of customer and transaction data.
- **Modular architecture**: Thousands of add-ons let you extend functionality without rebuilding.
- **Scalable infrastructure**: Built to handle high-volume sales and complex product catalogs.

## Deep Dive: Digistore24
Digistore24 is a cloud-based e-commerce and affiliate marketing platform tailored for digital product creators and vendors who want instant access to a global affiliate network. It bundles payment processing, tax compliance, and affiliate management into one automated system, eliminating the need for separate tools. With features like one-click upsells, split-testing, and multi-currency support, it's engineered for high-conversion funnels and rapid scaling through partnerships.

### Standout Features of Digistore24
- **Built-in affiliate marketplace**: Tap into thousands of affiliates without recruiting.
- **Automated tax & compliance**: Handles VAT, billing, and legal updates automatically.
- **Advanced funnel tools**: One-click upsells, bump offers, and countdown timers maximize average order value.

## The Final Verdict
**Choose PrestaShop if...**
- You need full control over store code and data.
- You want to avoid recurring platform fees.
- You have technical resources to manage hosting and updates.

**Choose Digistore24 if...**
- You prioritize affiliate-driven growth over storefront customization.
- You sell digital products and want built-in compliance.
- You prefer an all-in-one platform with minimal setup.
