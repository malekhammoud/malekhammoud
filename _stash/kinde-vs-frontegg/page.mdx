# Kinde vs Frontegg: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Choose Kinde for all-in-one SaaS building tools with billing; choose Frontegg for free, enterprise-grade CIAM with unlimited orgs.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Kinde** | **Frontegg** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $25 USD per month | $0/month |

## Deep Dive: Kinde

Kinde positions itself as a comprehensive developer platform that goes beyond authentication to provide a complete SaaS building toolkit. Its architecture integrates auth, billing, feature flags, and user management into a unified dashboard, making it ideal for startups that want to ship quickly without piecing together multiple services. The platform shines with its page designer for custom login flows, billing management for monetization, and lead generation tools that help convert visitors into paying customers. Kinde's strength lies in its opinionated approach—everything works together out of the box, reducing integration complexity.

The platform targets early-stage SaaS companies that need to validate their business model quickly. With built-in billing and feature management, teams can experiment with pricing tiers and roll out new functionality without additional infrastructure. Kinde's $25/month starting price includes everything needed to launch, making it a predictable cost structure for growing teams. However, this all-in-one approach means you're locked into Kinde's ecosystem for both auth and monetization.

### Standout Features of Kinde
- **Page Designer**: Drag-and-drop interface for custom login and signup pages without coding
- **Billing Management**: Native subscription handling with multiple pricing tiers and payment processing
- **Feature Flags**: Built-in experimentation and rollout controls for new functionality

## Deep Dive: Frontegg

Frontegg takes a focused approach to CIAM, providing enterprise-grade identity management with a generous free tier that includes unlimited organizations. Its architecture emphasizes security and scalability, featuring bot detection, advanced fraud protection, and tenant-level role management. The platform excels at handling complex B2B scenarios with multiple organizations, each with their own user bases and permission structures. Frontegg's in-app roles component allows developers to embed authorization directly into their applications' UI.

The platform targets mid-market to enterprise SaaS companies that need robust identity infrastructure without the cost barrier. Frontegg's free tier includes core features like SSO, MFA, and social logins, making it accessible for teams to start building immediately. The unlimited organizations feature is particularly valuable for multi-tenant applications, and the advanced fraud protection helps prevent account takeover and abuse. However, billing and feature management capabilities are not included, requiring integration with separate services.

### Standout Features of Frontegg
- **Unlimited Organizations**: Free tier supports unlimited tenant organizations without additional cost
- **Advanced Fraud Protection**: Bot detection and security measures to prevent account abuse
- **Tenant-Level Roles**: Granular permission management across multiple organizational tenants

## The Final Verdict

Choose Kinde if you want an all-in-one platform that combines authentication, billing, and feature management in a single predictable price. It's perfect for startups that need to launch quickly and monetize their product without managing multiple vendors.

Choose Frontegg if you need enterprise-grade CIAM with unlimited organizations and advanced security features at no cost. It's ideal for B2B SaaS companies that already have billing solutions and need robust identity management for complex multi-tenant architectures.
