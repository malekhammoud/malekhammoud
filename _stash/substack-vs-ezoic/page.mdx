## Deep Dive: Substack
Substack's architecture centers on creator-first ownership, giving writers complete control over their intellectual property, mailing lists, and subscriber payments. The platform's growth network drives over 50% of new free subscriptions and 25% of paid subscriptions through internal recommendations and referrals. With no editorial gatekeepers, creators maintain full autonomy over their content and monetization strategy.

The platform excels for independent writers, journalists, and thought leaders who prioritize direct audience relationships over ad revenue. Substack's infrastructure handles everything from payment processing to email delivery, allowing creators to focus purely on content creation while benefiting from built-in discovery mechanisms.

### Standout Features of Substack
- **IP Ownership**: Complete control over content and subscriber data
- **Growth Network**: Built-in discovery through recommendations and referrals
- **Direct Monetization**: No platform cuts on subscriber payments

## Deep Dive: Ezoic
Ezoic's platform operates as a comprehensive ad monetization ecosystem, integrating with major ad networks, Google AdExchange, and direct ad sales teams. The system leverages AI-driven bid floor optimization and supply-path optimization to maximize revenue while using first-party data for audience targeting. The platform's architecture supports rewarded ads and brand ad deals, creating multiple revenue streams for publishers.

The tool is engineered for established websites and publishers focused on maximizing ad revenue through intelligent ad placement and management. Ezoic's infrastructure handles everything from ad-ID providers to direct advertiser relationships, making it ideal for content-heavy sites with significant traffic volume.

### Standout Features of Ezoic
- **AI Monetization**: Automated bid floor and supply-path optimization
- **Network Integration**: Access to major ad networks and AdExchange
- **Direct Sales**: Dedicated team for brand ad deals and partnerships

## The Final Verdict
Choose Substack if you're an independent creator prioritizing audience ownership and direct relationships over ad revenue, especially if you're starting a newsletter or building a subscriber base from scratch.

Choose Ezoic if you're an established publisher with significant traffic looking to maximize ad revenue through intelligent optimization and access to premium advertising networks.
