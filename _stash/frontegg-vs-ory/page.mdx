# Frontegg vs Ory: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Choose Frontegg for cost-effective, developer-friendly CIAM with unlimited organizations; choose Ory for enterprise-grade security and compliance with premium support.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Frontegg** | **Ory** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $0/month | $770/year |
| **Best For** | Startups & SMBs | Enterprise & Regulated Industries |
| **Core Strength** | Unlimited Organizations | Enterprise Security & Compliance |

## Deep Dive: Frontegg
Frontegg delivers a low-code CIAM platform that scales with your SaaS application, offering a free tier that includes unlimited organizations—a standout feature for multi-tenant applications. Its architecture emphasizes developer productivity with pre-built UI components and seamless integration capabilities, making it ideal for teams that want to ship authentication quickly without sacrificing customization. The platform's tenant-level role management and feature flags enable sophisticated access control patterns out of the box.

### Standout Features of Frontegg
- **Unlimited Organizations**: No per-tenant fees, perfect for multi-tenant SaaS architectures
- **Feature Flags Integration**: Built-in feature flag system for progressive rollout and A/B testing
- **User Impersonation**: Admin capability to impersonate users for support and debugging

## Deep Dive: Ory
Ory provides a modular, enterprise-grade IAM platform with a comprehensive security-first approach, including advanced protection against credential stuffing, brute force attacks, and suspicious IP throttling. Its architecture supports multi-region deployments with data residency options and includes certifications like ISO and SOC2, making it suitable for regulated industries. The platform offers concierge onboarding and premium support with SLAs, ensuring enterprise customers receive white-glove service throughout implementation.

### Standout Features of Ory
- **Enterprise Security Stack**: Built-in protection against advanced threats including DoS and credential stuffing
- **Multi-Region Deployments**: Data residency compliance with GDPR-compliant EU storage options
- **99.99% Uptime SLA**: Enterprise-grade reliability with premium support and concierge onboarding

## The Final Verdict
Choose Frontegg if you're a startup or growing SaaS company that needs unlimited organizations without per-tenant costs, values developer productivity with pre-built components, and wants a free tier to get started.

Choose Ory if you're an enterprise or regulated industry that requires comprehensive security certifications, needs multi-region deployments with data residency, and values premium support with guaranteed uptime SLAs.
