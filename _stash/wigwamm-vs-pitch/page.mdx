## Deep Dive: Wigwamm
Wigwamm (Speaker Deck) is a lightweight, developer-friendly platform built for one purpose: converting PDFs into beautiful, embeddable web presentations. Its architecture is minimal—upload a PDF, get a shareable URL and embed code. Ideal for developers, educators, and content creators who need a no-fuss way to distribute slide decks online without building custom hosting.

### Standout Features of Wigwamm
- **100 uploads** with 150MB per file limit on free tier
- **Embeddable decks** with customizable embed code for any website
- **Pro features** include password protection, private URLs, and scheduled publishing

## Deep Dive: Pitch
Pitch is a modern, AI-powered presentation platform designed for teams that need to collaborate on branded, high-impact slide decks. Its architecture supports real-time multiplayer editing, advanced animations, and a robust brand asset library. Pitch is built for marketing, sales, and design teams who want to stay on brand while leveraging AI to speed up deck creation.

### Standout Features of Pitch
- **AI-powered design** with branded templates and custom fonts
- **Collaboration tools** including slide statuses, assignees, and video recordings
- **Cross-platform apps** for iOS and Android with presentation analytics

## The Final Verdict
Choose Wigwamm if you need a free, simple way to share PDFs as embeddable presentations without bells and whistles.

Choose Pitch if you're a team that needs collaborative, branded presentations with AI design assistance and advanced analytics.
