# Zoho vs Jobber: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Zoho wins for complex workflows and scalability; Jobber dominates for field service businesses needing all-in-one operations.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Zoho** | **Jobber** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Deep Dive: Zoho
Zoho Creator is a low-code platform that enables rapid custom application development with granular control over modules, fields, and workflows. Its tiered architecture scales from 10 modules with 10 fields each in Starter to 200 modules with 300 fields in Scale, making it ideal for businesses needing bespoke CRM solutions. The platform supports unlimited dashboards and custom reports in Growth and Scale tiers, plus robust workflow rules (2,500 org-wide) and email notifications up to 500/day per user. Deep integrations with Zoho Analytics, Campaigns, and Mail 360 create an ecosystem for data-driven operations.

### Standout Features of Zoho
- **Modular Architecture**: 10-200 modules with 10-300 fields each for precise data modeling
- **Workflow Engine**: 2,500 org-wide rules with 2,000 active limits for automation
- **Integration Ecosystem**: Native connectivity with Zoho's suite for analytics and marketing

## Deep Dive: Jobber
Jobber is purpose-built for home and commercial service businesses, offering an all-in-one platform that handles quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and payments in a single interface. Its drag-and-drop calendar, client hub, and online booking streamline field operations, while features like Jobber Capital and AI tools add financial and operational intelligence. The platform excels at client communication with built-in marketing tools and receptionist services, making it a complete operations hub for service professionals who need to manage jobs from lead to payment.

### Standout Features of Jobber
- **Field Service Focus**: Scheduling, invoicing, and client management in one app
- **Client Hub**: Self-service portal for customers to view quotes, invoices, and book services
- **Payment Processing**: Integrated credit card processing with Jobber Capital financing

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Zoho if:**
- You need custom CRM applications beyond standard templates
- Your business requires complex workflows and deep integrations
- You want to scale from basic to enterprise-grade functionality

**Choose Jobber if:**
- You run a field service, home service, or contracting business
- You need all operations (quoting, scheduling, payments) in one platform
- You want client self-service and online booking capabilities
