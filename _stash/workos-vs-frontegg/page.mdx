# WorkOS vs Frontegg: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** WorkOS wins for enterprise-scale SSO and directory sync; Frontegg wins for low-code, multi-tenant SaaS with unlimited orgs.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **WorkOS** | **Frontegg** |

| **Starting Price** | Free for the first 1 million active users; $2500/month for each additional million. | $0/month |

## Deep Dive: WorkOS
WorkOS is a developer-first platform built for B2B SaaS targeting enterprise customers. Its architecture is optimized for Single Sign-On (SAML, OIDC), Directory Sync (SCIM), and enterprise-grade compliance (SOC 2, GDPR). WorkOS excels at reducing enterprise sales friction by providing pre-built components for audit logging, RBAC, and fine-grained authorization. Ideal for startups and scale-ups needing to onboard enterprise clients quickly without building complex auth infrastructure from scratch.

### Standout Features of WorkOS
- **Directory Sync (SCIM)**: Automate user provisioning/deprovisioning across HR systems and directories.
- **Audit Logs**: Immutable, searchable logs for compliance and security audits.
- **Fine-Grained Authorization**: Attribute-based access control (ABAC) for complex enterprise permissions.

## Deep Dive: Frontegg
Frontegg is a low-code CIAM platform designed for multi-tenant SaaS applications. Its architecture centers on tenant isolation, in-app role management, and rapid deployment without heavy dev overhead. Frontegg provides a fully customizable login box, tenant-level RBAC, and feature flags—making it ideal for SaaS companies that need to scale customer identity management without sacrificing flexibility. The platform supports unlimited organizations, making it a strong fit for B2C2B and marketplace models.

### Standout Features of Frontegg
- **Unlimited Organizations**: Scale to any number of tenants without extra cost.
- **In-App Roles Component**: Fully customizable UI for managing tenant roles without leaving your app.
- **Feature Flags**: Enable/disable features per user or tenant for targeted rollouts.

## The Final Verdict
Choose **WorkOS** if:
- You need enterprise SSO (SAML/OIDC) and Directory Sync (SCIM)
- Compliance and audit logging are critical
- Your target customers are large enterprises

Choose **Frontegg** if:
- You're building a multi-tenant SaaS with unlimited orgs
- You want low-code tenant isolation and in-app role management
- You need feature flags integrated with identity
