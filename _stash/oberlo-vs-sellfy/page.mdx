## Deep Dive: Oberlo
Oberlo is a dropshipping powerhouse that eliminates inventory management by connecting merchants directly to suppliers. Its architecture focuses on seamless product sourcing, automated order fulfillment, and real-time inventory syncing. The platform's business tools—including profit margin calculators and traffic analytics—help entrepreneurs optimize their operations without technical overhead.

### Standout Features of Oberlo
- **Profit Margin Calculator**: Real-time pricing optimization for maximum margins
- **Business Name Generator**: AI-powered branding assistance
- **Traffic Calculator**: Data-driven audience targeting insights

## Deep Dive: Sellfy
Sellfy is a creator-centric e-commerce platform built for rapid store deployment without coding. Its architecture supports unlimited digital products, print-on-demand fulfillment, and subscription management through a unified dashboard. The platform's core strength lies in its marketing automation, customizable templates, and 24/7 support infrastructure.

### Standout Features of Sellfy
- **Unlimited Products**: No SKU restrictions or tiered limits
- **Automated Marketing**: Email sequences and promotional campaigns
- **Secure Payments**: PCI-compliant processing with multiple gateways

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Oberlo if:**
- You need automated dropshipping supply chain management
- Your business model focuses on physical products from suppliers
- You want integrated profit optimization tools

**Choose Sellfy if:**
- You sell digital products, subscriptions, or print-on-demand items
- You need unlimited product listings without volume restrictions
- You want built-in marketing automation and customizable storefronts
