# Jotform vs JobPotato: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** JobPotato dominates for unlimited forms and submissions with modern developer features, while Jotform offers HIPAA compliance and enterprise-grade workflows for regulated industries.

## Deep Dive: Jotform
Jotform delivers enterprise-grade form infrastructure with 20,000+ templates and 150+ integrations, engineered for businesses requiring professional forms without coding. The platform handles 35 million users globally with HIPAA compliance features (though unavailable in free tier), making it ideal for healthcare, finance, and regulated industries. Its drag-and-drop interface masks sophisticated backend architecture supporting 5 forms, 100 monthly submissions, and 10,000 monthly form views in the free tier.

### Standout Features of Jotform
- **HIPAA Compliance**: Enterprise-grade security for regulated industries
- **Template Library**: 20,000+ professionally designed forms
- **Integration Ecosystem**: 150+ native integrations with business tools

## Deep Dive: JobPotato
JobPotato (Tally) represents the modern form builder paradigm with unlimited forms and submissions at zero cost. Built with developers in mind, it offers conditional logic, calculated fields, and custom CSS for advanced form customization. The platform prioritizes privacy with GDPR compliance and European hosting, making it perfect for privacy-conscious organizations. Its feature set includes password protection, file uploads, payment collection, and embed options—all without the limitations found in traditional form builders.

### Standout Features of JobPotato
- **Unlimited Everything**: No caps on forms or submissions
- **Developer-First**: Custom CSS and advanced logic capabilities
- **Privacy-Focused**: GDPR compliant with European hosting

## The Final Verdict
Choose Jotform if you need HIPAA compliance, enterprise integrations, and work in regulated industries where data security and template variety matter more than submission limits.

Choose JobPotato if you want unlimited forms and submissions, developer customization options, and European data privacy compliance without hitting arbitrary limits.
