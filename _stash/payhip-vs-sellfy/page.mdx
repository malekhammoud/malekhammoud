# Payhip vs Sellfy: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Payhip wins for budget-conscious creators needing simplicity; Sellfy excels for scalable creators needing advanced marketing and print-on-demand.

## Deep Dive: Payhip
Payhip is a lightweight, all-in-one digital storefront engineered for creators who want to launch fast without technical overhead. Its architecture is minimal yet robust, supporting digital downloads, courses, coaching, memberships, and even physical products through a unified interface. With built-in VAT & tax handling and integrated email marketing, Payhip eliminates the need for third-party tools. Its payment gateway integrations are straightforward, making it ideal for solo creators or small teams who prioritize speed and cost-efficiency over advanced customization.

The platform’s core audience includes authors, educators, and independent creators who need a no-fuss solution to monetize digital content. Payhip’s strength lies in its simplicity—no coding required, no complex setup, and a free tier to test the waters. However, its feature set is intentionally lean, focusing on essentials rather than advanced marketing automation or scalability.

### Standout Features of Payhip
- **VAT & Tax Automation**: Built-in compliance for global sales.
- **Coaching & Memberships**: Monetize recurring services alongside digital products.
- **Email Marketing Integration**: Native tools to nurture leads and drive sales.

## Deep Dive: Sellfy
Sellfy is a feature-rich, code-free e-commerce platform designed for creators who need scalability and advanced marketing tools. Its architecture supports digital products, subscriptions, physical goods, and print-on-demand, making it a versatile choice for creators with diverse revenue streams. Sellfy’s customizable store templates and automated marketing features (like upsells and discounts) are tailored for creators who want to grow their brand without hiring developers.

The platform’s core audience includes creators, influencers, and small businesses looking to scale their online presence. Sellfy’s standout feature is its print-on-demand integration, which allows creators to sell custom merchandise without inventory. With 24/7 support and secure payment processing, Sellfy is built for creators who prioritize reliability and advanced functionality over cost.

### Standout Features of Sellfy
- **Print-on-Demand Integration**: Sell custom merchandise without inventory.
- **Automated Marketing**: Upsells, discounts, and email campaigns to boost sales.
- **24/7 Support**: Reliable assistance for scaling businesses.

## The Final Verdict
Choose Payhip if:
- You’re a budget-conscious creator needing a simple, no-fuss digital storefront.
- You prioritize affordability and ease of use over advanced features.
- You sell digital products, courses, or memberships without print-on-demand needs.

Choose Sellfy if:
- You need advanced marketing tools and scalability for growing your brand.
- You want to sell print-on-demand merchandise alongside digital products.
- You’re willing to pay a premium for 24/7 support and robust functionality.
