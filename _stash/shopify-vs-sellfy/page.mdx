# Shopify vs Sellfy: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Shopify wins for enterprise-grade, multichannel e-commerce; Sellfy wins for creators selling digital products.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Shopify** | **Sellfy** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $40.00 | $22/mo |
| **Best For** | Enterprise e-commerce | Digital creators |
| **Core Strength** | Multichannel sales & POS | Digital product sales |

## Deep Dive: Shopify
Shopify is a comprehensive commerce platform built for scalability, offering a full-stack e-commerce solution with POS integration, multichannel selling, and enterprise-grade automation. Its architecture supports complex inventory management, international sales, and custom API development, making it ideal for businesses scaling from startup to enterprise. With Shopify Flow and the Fulfillment Network, it provides end-to-end automation and logistics.

### Standout Features of Shopify
- **Shopify POS**: Seamless in-person sales integration with hardware support
- **Shopify Flow**: No-code automation for complex business workflows
- **Shopify Fulfillment Network**: Automated warehousing and shipping solutions

## Deep Dive: Sellfy
Sellfy is a streamlined, code-free platform designed for creators and small businesses focused on digital product sales. Its architecture prioritizes simplicity, offering unlimited products, automated marketing tools, and secure payment processing without the complexity of traditional e-commerce platforms. The platform excels at quick store setup and integrates email marketing directly into the sales workflow.

### Standout Features of Sellfy
- **Digital-first**: Optimized for selling digital downloads and subscriptions
- **Print-on-demand**: Built-in POD integration for physical product creators
- **Unlimited products**: No caps on product listings in any plan

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Shopify if...**
- You need multichannel sales (online, in-person, social)
- You're building a complex e-commerce business
- You require advanced automation and POS integration

**Choose Sellfy if...**
- You primarily sell digital products or subscriptions
- You want a simple, code-free setup
- You're a creator or small business with basic e-commerce needs
