# Zoho vs Freshworks: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Zoho wins for budget-conscious teams needing custom workflows; Freshworks dominates for enterprise-grade AI-powered customer service.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Zoho** | **Freshworks** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $5 | $19/agent/month, billed annually |
| **Best For** | Custom app development | AI-powered service desks |
| **Core Strength** | Low-code flexibility | Enterprise automation |

## Deep Dive: Zoho

Zoho Creator is a low-code platform built for rapid application development, offering modular scalability from 10 to 200 modules depending on tier. Its architecture supports 10-300 custom fields per module, unlimited dashboards on Growth/Scale plans, and robust workflow automation with 2,500 org-level rules. Integration with Zoho's ecosystem (Analytics, Campaigns, Mail 360) makes it ideal for businesses wanting to extend beyond CRM into analytics and marketing automation. The platform's API limits (2,000 active workflows, 200-500 emails/day) suit SMBs and mid-market teams prioritizing customization over enterprise-scale operations.

### Standout Features of Zoho
- **Modular Architecture:** Scale from 10 to 200 modules as your business grows
- **Workflow Automation:** 2,500 org-level rules with granular email notification controls
- **Ecosystem Integration:** Native connectivity with Zoho Analytics, Campaigns, and Mail 360

## Deep Dive: Freshworks

Freshworks delivers enterprise-grade service software with AI-powered automation through Freddy AI, designed for large organizations managing complex customer and employee experiences. Its architecture includes multichannel support, advanced ticket routing, and comprehensive IT service management (ITSM) capabilities including incident, problem, and change management. The platform excels at SLA management, knowledge base creation, and AI-assisted workflows that reduce manual intervention. With service catalog and automation features built for scale, Freshworks targets enterprises needing robust, AI-enhanced customer service infrastructure.

### Standout Features of Freshworks
- **Freddy AI Capabilities:** Intelligent automation and predictive assistance
- **ITSM Framework:** Complete incident, problem, and change management suite
- **SLA Management:** Automated service level agreement tracking and enforcement

## The Final Verdict

Choose Zoho if:
- You need custom application development beyond basic CRM
- Budget constraints require starting at $5/month
- Workflow automation and Zoho ecosystem integration are priorities

Choose Freshworks if:
- You require enterprise-grade AI-powered customer service
- IT service management (ITSM) capabilities are essential
- Your team needs advanced automation and Freddy AI features
