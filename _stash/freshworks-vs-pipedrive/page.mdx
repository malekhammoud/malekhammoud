# Freshworks vs Pipedrive: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Freshworks dominates enterprise IT service management, while Pipedrive crushes SMB sales pipeline optimization.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Freshworks** | **Pipedrive** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $19/agent/month, billed annually | $14.90 per user per month (Lite plan) |
| **Best For** | Enterprise IT service management | SMB sales pipeline optimization |
| **Core Strength** | AI-powered service desk automation | Sales activity tracking & pipeline visibility |

## Deep Dive: Freshworks
Freshworks is a comprehensive IT service management platform built for enterprise environments. Its architecture centers around Freddy AI, providing intelligent ticket routing, predictive analytics, and automated incident resolution. The platform excels at handling complex service workflows with built-in ITIL capabilities including problem management, change management, and service catalog management. Freshworks targets medium to large organizations that need robust SLA management and multi-channel support across web, email, phone, and chat.

### Standout Features of Freshworks
- Freddy AI for intelligent ticket routing and predictive support
- Advanced analytics dashboards with custom reporting capabilities
- Comprehensive ITIL framework with incident, problem, and change management
- Service catalog and SLA management for enterprise-level service delivery

## Deep Dive: Pipedrive
Pipedrive is a sales-focused CRM engineered for SMBs that need visual pipeline management and activity-based selling. Its architecture prioritizes deal tracking with customizable pipelines, AI-powered sales insights, and automated follow-up sequences. The platform integrates seamlessly with 500+ tools and provides real-time sales analytics through its AI Sales Assistant. Pipedrive targets small to medium businesses focused on lead generation, deal management, and closing sales efficiently through activity-driven workflows.

### Standout Features of Pipedrive
- Visual sales pipeline with customizable stages and deal tracking
- AI Sales Assistant for deal prioritization and revenue forecasting
- Two-way email sync with automated follow-up capabilities
- Mobile CRM with real-time sales insights and activity reminders

## The Final Verdict
Choose Freshworks if you need enterprise-grade IT service management with AI-powered automation, complex workflow handling, and comprehensive ITIL capabilities for medium to large organizations.

Choose Pipedrive if you're an SMB focused on sales pipeline optimization, visual deal tracking, and activity-based selling with seamless integrations and mobile accessibility.
