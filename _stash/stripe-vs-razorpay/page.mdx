## Deep Dive: Stripe
Stripe is a full-stack financial services platform engineered for businesses scaling globally. Its architecture supports complex billing models, tax automation, and revenue recognition out of the box. With Stripe Sigma and Data Pipeline, teams get granular analytics and real-time data export. The platform's Connect and Issuing products enable marketplace and embedded finance use cases, while Global Payouts and Financial Accounts make it a one-stop shop for multi-country operations. Stripe's API-first design and extensive SDKs make it ideal for developers building high-volume, multi-currency payment systems.

### Standout Features of Stripe
- **Revenue Recognition**: Automated ASC 606/IFRS 15 compliance for SaaS financials
- **Stripe Sigma**: SQL-based analytics on raw payment data
- **Global Payouts**: Multi-currency, cross-border payouts with local bank integration

## Deep Dive: Razorpay
Razorpay is purpose-built for the Indian market, offering 100+ local payment methods and deep integration with India's UPI, IMPS, and Bharat QR ecosystems. Its automated payroll and compliance tools simplify statutory requirements for Indian businesses, while the Smart Dashboard provides real-time insights tailored to local tax and regulatory frameworks. Razorpay's Instant Settlements and Dynamic Pricing give startups faster access to funds and flexible fee structures. The platform's focus on seamless integration and quick onboarding makes it a favorite for early-stage companies scaling within India.

### Standout Features of Razorpay
- **100+ Payment Methods**: UPI, cards, wallets, and netbanking in one integration
- **Automated Payroll & Compliances**: End-to-end payroll with statutory compliance
- **Instant Settlements**: Real-time fund access for improved cash flow

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Stripe if...**
- You're building a global, multi-currency business
- You need advanced financial products (Issuing, Connect, Revenue Recognition)
- You require deep analytics and custom billing models

**Choose Razorpay if...**
- Your business is primarily based in India
- You need fast, compliant payouts with local payment methods
- You want an all-in-one platform for payments and payroll
