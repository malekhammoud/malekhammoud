# Mailgun vs Loops: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Mailgun wins for developer-centric transactional email with advanced analytics, while Loops excels at simple, unified email campaigns with drag-and-drop simplicity.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Mailgun** | **Loops** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $0/month | Free |
| **Best For** | Developer-focused email delivery | All-in-one email campaigns |
| **Core Strength** | Advanced analytics & deliverability | Simple interface & journey automation |

## Deep Dive: Mailgun

Mailgun is a battle-tested email delivery platform built for developers who need precise control over transactional email infrastructure. Its API-first approach, comprehensive analytics, and advanced deliverability features make it the go-to choice for engineering teams sending high-volume transactional messages. The platform offers granular tracking, spam trap monitoring, and dedicated IP options that give technical teams the tools they need to optimize inbox placement and monitor performance at scale.

### Standout Features of Mailgun
- **Email API & SMTP**: Dual delivery methods for maximum flexibility
- **Advanced Analytics**: Real-time tracking, open rates, click tracking, and detailed delivery reports
- **Deliverability Tools**: Inbox placement testing, spam trap monitoring, and blocklist monitoring to maximize deliverability

## Deep Dive: Loops

Loops takes a different approach by unifying product, marketing, and transactional email into a single, streamlined interface. Designed for teams that want simplicity without sacrificing power, Loops offers an intuitive drag-and-drop email builder that feels like writing a document. The platform handles all DNS configuration automatically and provides intelligent queueing to optimize deliverability, making it ideal for teams that want to send all types of email without managing complex infrastructure.

### Standout Features of Loops
- **Document-style Email Builder**: Craft emails like you're writing a doc with real-time collaboration
- **Automated Journeys**: Build sophisticated customer journeys without coding
- **Complete DNS Handling**: Automatic configuration and management of all email authentication records

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Mailgun if:**
- You need advanced analytics and deliverability monitoring
- Your team requires granular control over email infrastructure
- You're sending high-volume transactional email and need dedicated IPs

**Choose Loops if:**
- You want to manage all email types (transactional, marketing, product) in one place
- Your team prefers a simple, visual email builder over API complexity
- You need automated customer journeys without engineering resources
