## Deep Dive: Payhip
Payhip is a lightweight, serverless e-commerce platform built for digital creators who need instant storefronts without technical overhead. Its architecture supports instant file delivery, integrated payment processing, and VAT/tax automation—perfect for creators selling courses, memberships, or digital downloads. The platform's modular design allows you to add physical products, coaching, or email marketing without leaving the ecosystem.

### Standout Features of Payhip
- **Instant Digital Delivery**: Automated file delivery post-purchase with no manual intervention
- **VAT & Tax Automation**: Built-in compliance for EU VAT, US sales tax, and international tax rules
- **Email Marketing Integration**: Native email capture and automation tools for customer nurturing

## Deep Dive: Oberlo
Oberlo is a dropshipping ecosystem that connects entrepreneurs to suppliers through Shopify integration. Its architecture focuses on product sourcing, inventory automation, and order fulfillment—eliminating the need to handle physical stock. The platform provides business tools like profit margin calculators and traffic estimators, along with educational resources to help users scale dropshipping operations efficiently.

### Standout Features of Oberlo
- **Supplier Integration**: Direct connection to AliExpress and other supplier networks for product sourcing
- **Automated Order Fulfillment**: One-click order processing that handles shipping and tracking automatically
- **Business Tools Suite**: Profit calculators, name generators, and traffic estimators for data-driven decisions

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Payhip if:**
- You're selling digital products, courses, or memberships
- You need instant setup without technical complexity
- You want built-in tax compliance and payment processing

**Choose Oberlo if:**
- You're building a physical product store without inventory
- You need supplier connections and automated fulfillment
- You want dropshipping-specific business tools and resources
