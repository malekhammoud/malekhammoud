# New Relic vs BetterStack: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** New Relic dominates enterprise observability with comprehensive APM, while BetterStack wins for SMBs needing affordable incident management and AI-powered post-mortems.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **New Relic** | **BetterStack** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free tier available with 100 GB data ingest per month. | $29 |
| **Best For** | Enterprise observability | SMB incident management |
| **Core Strength** | Full-stack monitoring | AI-powered incident response |

## Deep Dive: New Relic
New Relic delivers enterprise-grade observability with its intelligent platform designed for the AI era. The platform excels at full-stack monitoring, offering everything from APM and infrastructure monitoring to advanced features like Cloud Cost Intelligence and Engagement Intelligence. Its Transaction 360 provides deep visibility into application performance, while the Security RX module helps teams maintain compliance and security standards. The free tier with 100 GB data ingest makes it accessible for teams starting their observability journey.

New Relic's architecture is built for scale, supporting distributed tracing, Kubernetes monitoring, and serverless environments. The platform's AI capabilities, including New Relic AI for anomaly detection and automated insights, make it ideal for organizations with complex, microservices-based architectures. With over 500 integrations and support for custom dashboards, New Relic adapts to virtually any tech stack.

### Standout Features of New Relic
- **Transaction 360**: Deep application performance insights with business context
- **Cloud Cost Intelligence**: Track and optimize cloud spending across services
- **Security RX**: Integrated security monitoring and vulnerability detection

## Deep Dive: BetterStack
BetterStack positions itself as an AI-powered SRE platform focused on incident management and response. With 7,000+ customers and a 60-day money-back guarantee, it offers a compelling value proposition at $29/month. The platform combines traditional observability features (logs, metrics, traces) with incident management tools, creating a unified solution for teams that prioritize rapid incident resolution over comprehensive monitoring.

BetterStack's AI capabilities shine in its post-mortem generation and anomaly detection. The platform integrates seamlessly with Slack and MS Teams, making it ideal for teams that operate primarily through chat. Features like unlimited phone call alerts and customizable status pages with custom CSS/JavaScript give teams flexibility in how they communicate incidents to stakeholders.

### Standout Features of BetterStack
- **AI Post-Mortems**: Automatically generated incident analysis and root cause identification
- **Unlimited Phone Alerts**: Ensure critical incidents reach on-call engineers immediately
- **Custom Status Pages**: Fully branded, customizable status pages with custom CSS/JavaScript

## The Final Verdict
Choose New Relic if you need comprehensive enterprise observability, complex microservices monitoring, and advanced analytics across your entire stack. Its free tier and scalable pricing make it suitable for teams of all sizes, but it truly excels in large, distributed environments.

Choose BetterStack if you're a small to medium business focused on incident response and on-call management. Its AI-powered features, affordable pricing, and strong incident management capabilities make it ideal for teams that need to resolve issues quickly without the overhead of a full enterprise observability platform.
