## Deep Dive: Substack
Substack is a vertically integrated publishing platform designed for independent creators who want full ownership of their content and audience. Its architecture centers on a subscription-based model where writers retain 100% IP rights, mailing lists, and subscriber payments. The platform's growth network drives 50% of new free subscriptions and 25% of paid subscriptions through internal recommendations and referrals, reducing the creator's marketing burden. With no editorial gatekeepers, Substack empowers writers to build direct relationships with readers while monetizing through paid newsletters.

### Standout Features of Substack
- **IP Ownership**: Creators retain full rights to content, mailing lists, and subscriber payments
- **Growth Network**: Built-in recommendation system drives organic audience acquisition
- **Editorial Freedom**: No gatekeepers means complete creative control over content

## Deep Dive: Core - Connecting Repositories
Core is a not-for-profit academic infrastructure service that aggregates and indexes scholarly literature at massive scale. Hosted by The Open University and supported by CORE Members, it provides access to 252 million papers through a comprehensive bibliographic database. The platform's architecture includes APIs and datasets for raw data access, content discovery tools for research navigation, and a dashboard for content exposure management. Core also offers OAI identifiers for unique repository record identification and bespoke consultancy services for tailored academic solutions.

### Standout Features of Core - Connecting Repositories
- **Massive Scale**: 252 million papers from global open access repositories
- **API Access**: Raw data availability through programmatic interfaces
- **Repository Management**: OAI identifiers and dashboard tools for content organization

## The Final Verdict
Choose Substack if you're an independent writer, journalist, or content creator looking to build a direct subscriber relationship and monetize your expertise through newsletters.

Choose Core - Connecting Repositories if you're a researcher, academic institution, or organization needing comprehensive access to scholarly literature and repository management tools for academic publishing workflows.
