# ElevenLabs vs Help Scout: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** **ElevenLabs wins for AI-powered voice automation at zero cost, while Help Scout dominates for human-centric team support with proven workflows.**

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **ElevenLabs** | **Help Scout** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $0 | $25 per user/mo |
| **Best For** | AI voice automation | Team collaboration |
| **Core Strength** | Multilingual TTS/Voice cloning | Shared inbox + knowledge base |

## Deep Dive: ElevenLabs
ElevenLabs delivers a cutting-edge AI voice generation platform built on advanced neural TTS models, supporting over 70 languages with voice cloning capabilities. The architecture leverages deep learning for natural prosody and emotional inflection, making it ideal for automated customer interactions. Its API-first design enables seamless integration into existing support stacks, while the 24/7 AI answering service handles high-volume queries without human intervention. The platform's zero-cost entry point makes it particularly attractive for startups and SMBs looking to automate voice-based support at scale.

### Standout Features of ElevenLabs
- **Voice Cloning**: Create custom voice profiles that match your brand's tone and personality
- **Multilingual TTS**: Generate natural-sounding speech in 70+ languages with regional accents
- **API Integrations**: RESTful API with comprehensive documentation for developers

## Deep Dive: Help Scout
Help Scout is purpose-built for customer service teams, offering a shared inbox architecture that centralizes all customer communications across email, chat, and self-service channels. The platform's strength lies in its human-centric design—combining AI-powered drafting tools with collaborative workflows that maintain the personal touch customers expect. Built-in knowledge base and help center features reduce ticket volume by empowering customers to self-serve, while HIPAA compliance makes it suitable for healthcare and regulated industries. The mobile app and 100+ integrations ensure teams can provide support from anywhere without breaking existing toolchains.

### Standout Features of Help Scout
- **Shared Inbox**: Unified team inbox with collision detection and internal notes
- **AI Drafts & Answers**: Smart suggestions that speed up response times without losing authenticity
- **HIPAA Compliance**: Enterprise-grade security for regulated industries

## The Final Verdict
**Choose ElevenLabs if:**
- You need cost-effective voice automation for customer interactions
- Multilingual support is critical for your global audience
- You want to build custom AI voice experiences

**Choose Help Scout if:**
- Your team prioritizes human collaboration over pure automation
- You need a complete support stack with knowledge base and live chat
- HIPAA compliance or regulated industry requirements are essential
