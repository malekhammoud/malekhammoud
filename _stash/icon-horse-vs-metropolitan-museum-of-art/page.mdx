## Deep Dive: Icon Horse
Icon Horse is a specialized favicon retrieval service that provides on-demand access to website icons with intelligent fallback systems. Built for developers who need reliable icon fetching without the complexity of building their own solution, it offers private mode operation and priority support for enterprise needs. The service operates without cookies or tracking, making it privacy-compliant by default.

### Standout Features of Icon Horse
- **Automated Favicon Workflows**: Handles icon discovery and retrieval automatically with intelligent fallback chains
- **Customizable Fallbacks**: Configurable fallback logic ensures you always get an icon, even if the primary source fails
- **Private Mode Operation**: Works without cookies or tracking for privacy-compliant applications
- **Priority Support**: Enterprise-grade support for critical applications and uptime requirements

## Deep Dive: Metropolitan Museum of Art
The Metropolitan Museum of Art provides open access to one of the world's largest art collections through RESTful JSON APIs. With over 470,000 artworks available for unrestricted commercial and noncommercial use, it's a treasure trove for developers building art-related applications, educational tools, or cultural projects. The service requires no registration or API keys, making it instantly accessible.

### Standout Features of Metropolitan Museum of Art
