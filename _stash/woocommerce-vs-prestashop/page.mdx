# WooCommerce vs PrestaShop: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** **WooCommerce wins for WordPress-native stores with tiered pricing needs; PrestaShop dominates for scalable, self-hosted e-commerce with full data ownership.**

## Deep Dive: WooCommerce
WooCommerce is a WordPress-native e-commerce solution built for merchants who want seamless integration with their existing WordPress sites. Its open-source architecture allows for deep customization, and its tiered pricing features (rules-based, editable labels) make it ideal for businesses with complex pricing strategies. WooCommerce’s plugin ecosystem and developer-friendly API ensure scalability for small to medium-sized stores.

### Standout Features of WooCommerce
- **Rules-Based Tier Pricing**: Automate discounts based on cart value or quantity.
- **Editable Tier Pricing Labels**: Customize pricing tiers to match your brand voice.
- **WordPress Integration**: Native compatibility with WordPress themes and plugins.

## Deep Dive: PrestaShop
PrestaShop is a self-hosted e-commerce platform designed for businesses that prioritize full control over their online store and data. Its modular architecture supports thousands of themes and modules, enabling highly customizable storefronts. PrestaShop’s scalability makes it suitable for businesses growing from hundreds to millions in revenue, with a dedicated support team and extensive resources to guide merchants.

### Standout Features of PrestaShop
- **Full Data Ownership**: Maintain complete control over your store’s data.
- **Thousands of Modules**: Extend functionality with a vast library of add-ons.
- **Scalable Architecture**: Handle growth from small to enterprise-level revenue.

## The Final Verdict
Choose **WooCommerce if** you’re running a WordPress site and need advanced tiered pricing features.  
Choose **PrestaShop if** you want full data ownership, scalability, and a self-hosted solution for long-term growth.
