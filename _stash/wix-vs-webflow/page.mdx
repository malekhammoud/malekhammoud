## Deep Dive: Wix
Wix is an AI-powered website builder designed for non-technical users who want to launch quickly without coding. Its drag-and-drop interface, 2000+ templates, and built-in business tools make it ideal for small businesses, portfolios, and online stores. The platform handles hosting, security, and SEO out of the box, letting users focus on content rather than infrastructure.

Wix's core strength lies in its all-in-one approach—combining website design, eCommerce, appointment scheduling, CRM, and marketing tools in a single platform. The AI website builder can generate complete sites in minutes, while the mobile app lets owners manage their business on the go. It's built for speed of deployment rather than architectural flexibility.

### Standout Features of Wix
- **AI Website Builder**: Generates complete sites from prompts in minutes
- **Integrated Business Suite**: CRM, payments, scheduling, and marketing in one platform
- **Enterprise Security**: Built-in SSL, DDoS protection, and PCI compliance

## Deep Dive: Webflow
Webflow is a visual web development platform that bridges the gap between design and code, offering designers and developers unprecedented control over their websites. Unlike traditional builders, Webflow generates clean, semantic HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript code that's exportable and customizable. The platform combines a powerful CMS, hosting infrastructure, and design tools in one environment.

Webflow's architecture is built for scalability and collaboration, with features like Figma to Webflow conversion, shared libraries, and localization support. It's designed for teams who need pixel-perfect designs, complex interactions, and custom code integration. The visual CSS grid and flexbox controls, combined with custom code capabilities, make it a favorite among professional designers.

### Standout Features of Webflow
- **Visual Code Generation**: Produces clean, exportable HTML5/CSS3/JS code
- **Figma to Webflow**: Direct import of Figma designs with automatic conversion
- **Advanced Interactions**: Custom animations and micro-interactions without coding

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Wix if:**
- You're a beginner or small business owner
- You need an all-in-one solution with business tools
- You want to launch quickly without technical knowledge

**Choose Webflow if:**
- You're a designer or developer needing granular control
- You require custom code or complex interactions
- You want to maintain design consistency across large sites
- You need exportable, clean code for further development
