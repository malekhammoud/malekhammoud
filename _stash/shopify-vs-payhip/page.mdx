# Shopify vs Payhip: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Shopify dominates for complex e-commerce businesses; Payhip wins for digital creators selling courses and downloads.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Shopify** | **Payhip** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $40.00 | $5.95 |
| **Best For** | Full-scale e-commerce | Digital creators |
| **Core Strength** | Enterprise features | Simplicity |

## Deep Dive: Shopify
Shopify is a comprehensive e-commerce platform built for scaling businesses. Its architecture includes a robust API, customizable storefronts, and integrated POS systems. With features like Shopify Flow for automation, custom APIs for developers, and the Shopify Fulfillment Network, it's designed for businesses that need advanced inventory management, multichannel integration, and international sales tools. Shopify's checkout is optimized for conversions, and its app store extends functionality for virtually any use case.

### Standout Features of Shopify
- **Shopify Flow**: Automate workflows and business processes
- **Shopify POS**: Integrated in-person sales system
- **Shopify Fulfillment Network**: End-to-end logistics and shipping

## Deep Dive: Payhip
Payhip is a lightweight platform focused on digital products and courses. Its architecture is designed for simplicity, allowing creators to launch stores without technical skills. Payhip supports digital downloads, memberships, and coaching, with built-in VAT & taxes handling. The platform integrates with payment gateways and offers email marketing tools, making it ideal for solo creators or small businesses selling digital content. Payhip's free tier and low-cost plans make it accessible for those just starting out.

### Standout Features of Payhip
- **Digital Downloads**: Sell files, courses, and memberships easily
- **VAT & Taxes**: Automatic tax calculation and compliance
- **Free Tier**: Start selling without upfront costs

## The Final Verdict
Choose Shopify if you need a scalable, feature-rich platform for physical products, complex inventory, or multi-channel sales.

Choose Payhip if you're a creator selling digital products, courses, or memberships and want a simple, affordable solution.
