## Deep Dive: AppsFlyer
AppsFlyer is built as a Modern Marketing Cloud with a foundation in mobile attribution that has expanded into a comprehensive measurement ecosystem. Its architecture centers on breaking down silos across measurement, deep linking, data collaboration, and autonomous AI workflows. The platform excels at tracking users across mobile apps, CTV, PC, and console platforms with its Measurement Suite, while the Deep Linking Suite handles sophisticated routing from web-to-app, QR-to-app, and social channels. AppsFlyer's Agentic AI Suite and Data Clean Room enable privacy-safe data collaboration and autonomous marketing workflows that scale across channels.

### Standout Features of AppsFlyer
- **Cross-Platform Attribution**: Tracks users across mobile, CTV, PC, and console with unified measurement
- **Deferred Deep Linking**: Routes users to specific app content even if the app isn't installed
- **Data Clean Room**: Privacy-safe environment for data collaboration without raw data sharing

## Deep Dive: Amplitude
Amplitude is architected as a digital analytics platform focused on understanding user behavior and driving product decisions through data. Its core Product Analytics engine tracks events and user journeys across web and mobile, while Feature Experimentation and Feature Management enable controlled rollouts and A/B testing. The platform's Session Replay captures user interactions for qualitative analysis, complemented by Guides and Surveys for in-app user engagement. Amplitude's AI Agents and AI Visibility provide automated insights and anomaly detection, while the Amplitude MCP (Model Context Protocol) extends the platform's capabilities through integrations.

### Standout Features of Amplitude
- **Product Analytics**: Deep behavioral insights with funnels, retention, and cohort analysis
- **Feature Experimentation**: Controlled A/B testing with statistical rigor for product changes
- **Session Replay**: Visual recording of user sessions to understand friction points

## The Final Verdict
Choose AppsFlyer if you need comprehensive mobile attribution, cross-platform measurement, and marketing analytics across channels with strong fraud protection and deep linking capabilities.

Choose Amplitude if you're focused on product analytics, experimentation, and understanding user behavior within your application to drive product decisions and optimization.
