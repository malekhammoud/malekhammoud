## Deep Dive: Zoho
Zoho Creator is a low-code platform built for rapid application development, offering modular architecture with tiered scalability from 10 to 200 modules. Its structured data model supports 10-300 fields per module, unlimited dashboards at higher tiers, and 2,500 workflow rules per organization. The platform integrates seamlessly with Zoho's ecosystem including Analytics, Campaigns, and Mail 360, making it ideal for businesses needing custom CRM solutions without extensive coding.

### Standout Features of Zoho
- **Tiered Scalability**: Starter to Scale plans accommodate growing data needs
- **Workflow Automation**: 2,500 org-level rules with granular email notification controls
- **Ecosystem Integration**: Native connections to Zoho's full suite of business tools

## Deep Dive: ACD Systems
ACDSee specializes in photo and video editing software with advanced AI-powered tools for digital asset management. Features include AI keywords, sky replacement, selection tools, facial recognition, and RAW photo editing. The platform excels in creative workflows for photographers and media professionals, offering layered editing, portrait retouching, and comprehensive organization capabilities. However, ACDSee lacks any CRM functionality, focusing entirely on creative content management.

### Standout Features of ACD Systems
- **AI-Powered Tools**: Automated keyword tagging and intelligent sky replacement
- **RAW Photo Editing**: Professional-grade editing for high-resolution images
- **Facial Recognition**: Advanced organization through facial detection

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Zoho if...** you need a CRM platform with custom app development capabilities, workflow automation, and integration with business tools.

**Choose ACD Systems if...** you're a photographer or media professional needing advanced photo/video editing and digital asset management—but not for CRM purposes.
