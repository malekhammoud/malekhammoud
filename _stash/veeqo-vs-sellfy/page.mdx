# Veeqo vs Sellfy: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Veeqo wins for high-volume e-commerce operations with its free, feature-rich inventory and shipping management, while Sellfy excels for creators selling digital products with its simple, code-free store builder.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Veeqo** | **Sellfy** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | FREE | $22/mo |
| **Best For** | Multi-channel e-commerce | Digital product sales |
| **Core Strength** | Inventory & shipping automation | Creator storefront simplicity |

## Deep Dive: Veeqo
Veeqo is a comprehensive e-commerce operations platform designed for businesses managing inventory and shipping across multiple sales channels. Its architecture integrates with major marketplaces, shopping carts, and carriers, providing real-time inventory updates and automated order routing. The platform's strength lies in its ability to centralize operations, offering pre-negotiated shipping rates, barcode scanning for accuracy, and advanced reporting for data-driven decisions. Ideal for scaling e-commerce businesses, Veeqo eliminates the complexity of juggling multiple systems.

### Standout Features of Veeqo
- **Multi-carrier shipping:** Pre-negotiated rates with major carriers, including Amazon Shipping, with label printing in seconds.
- **Inventory automation:** Real-time syncing across all sales channels, with barcode scanning and automated low-stock alerts.
- **Amazon MCF integration:** Direct access to Amazon's Multi-Channel Fulfillment for streamlined order processing.

## Deep Dive: Sellfy
Sellfy is a lightweight, creator-focused e-commerce platform that prioritizes simplicity and speed for selling digital products, subscriptions, and print-on-demand goods. Built for non-technical users, it offers customizable storefronts without any coding, with built-in marketing tools like email campaigns and discount codes. Sellfy's architecture is optimized for fast store setup and secure payments, making it ideal for artists, educators, and influencers who want to monetize content quickly. The platform's 24/7 support and all-in-one dashboard cater to solo entrepreneurs.

### Standout Features of Sellfy
- **Digital product sales:** Sell downloads, subscriptions, and print-on-demand items with no inventory management.
- **Built-in marketing:** Email marketing, discount codes, and upselling tools to boost conversions.
- **Customizable storefronts:** Drag-and-drop store builder with no coding required, optimized for mobile.

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Veeqo if:**
- You run a multi-channel e-commerce business needing inventory and shipping automation.
- You want to save on shipping costs with pre-negotiated carrier rates.
- You need advanced reporting and Amazon MCF integration.

**Choose Sellfy if:**
- You're a creator selling digital products, courses, or print-on-demand items.
- You want a simple, code-free store builder with built-in marketing tools.
- You prioritize speed and ease of use over complex operations.
