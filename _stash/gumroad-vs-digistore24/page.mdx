## Deep Dive: Gumroad
Gumroad is a minimalist e-commerce platform built for creators who want to sell directly to their audience without friction. Its architecture is lightweight, focusing on speed and ease of use over complex features. The platform handles payments, digital delivery, and basic analytics out of the box, making it ideal for solo entrepreneurs who prioritize rapid deployment over customization. Gumroad's API is straightforward but limited, designed for basic integrations rather than complex workflows.

### Standout Features of Gumroad
- **Pay What You Want**: Flexible pricing models for creative work
- **Instant Payouts**: Get paid immediately after a sale
- **Built-in Analytics**: Track sales and audience growth without third-party tools

## Deep Dive: Digistore24
