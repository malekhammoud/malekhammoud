## Deep Dive: Intelli-CTi
Intelli-CTi is a specialized Computer Telephony Integration solution engineered for enterprises running Infor CRM. Its architecture seamlessly bridges CRM and CTI-enabled telephone systems, delivering real-time caller identification and click-to-dial functionality. Built with a flexible, extendable framework, it supports multi-site deployments and Citrix/Terminal Server environments. The solution integrates directly into desktop environments with dynamic sidebar displays and on-screen call handling, making it ideal for high-volume call centers and customer service teams requiring rapid implementation and multi-call support.

### Standout Features of Intelli-CTi
- **One-click dialing** from CRM records with automatic call logging and statistics tracking
- **Multi-site deployment** supporting multiple telephone systems simultaneously
- **Chrome browser support** with desktop integration and active sidebar display
- **In-call note capture** capability for post-call wrap-up activities

## Deep Dive: Jobber
Jobber is a comprehensive field service management platform designed for home and commercial service businesses. Its architecture combines CRM, scheduling, invoicing, and client communication into a unified system. The platform features a drag-and-drop calendar, client hub, and AI-powered tools for quote generation and job management. With integrated payment processing, online booking capabilities, and marketing tools, Jobber serves as an all-in-one solution for service businesses looking to streamline operations from initial contact through payment collection.

### Standout Features of Jobber
- **Complete job lifecycle management** from quoting through invoicing and payment
- **Client Hub portal** enabling customer self-service and communication
- **AI-powered tools** for intelligent job scheduling and optimization
- **Integrated payment processing** with Jobber Capital financing options

## The Final Verdict
Choose Intelli-CTi if you need enterprise-grade CRM-telephony integration with advanced call handling features and multi-site support for Infor CRM environments.

Choose Jobber if you run a field service business requiring comprehensive job management, scheduling, and client communication tools in a single platform.
