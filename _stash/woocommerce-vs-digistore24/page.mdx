## Deep Dive: WooCommerce
WooCommerce is a WordPress-native, open-source e-commerce powerhouse built for developers who demand complete control. Its plugin-based architecture allows deep customization through PHP, hooks, and filters, making it ideal for merchants who want to own their codebase and scale without platform lock-in. With tier pricing rules and editable labels, it's tailored for B2B merchants running complex pricing models on WordPress.

### Standout Features of WooCommerce
- **Rules-Based Tier Pricing**: Configure volume discounts with granular conditions
- **Editable Tier Pricing Labels**: Customize pricing display for B2B clarity
- **WordPress Integration**: Native hooks and filters for deep customization

## Deep Dive: Digistore24
Digistore24 is a European affiliate marketing juggernaut designed for digital product creators who need built-in affiliate management and compliance. Its all-in-one platform handles everything from order forms to tax calculation, with S2S postback tracking and lifetime attribution baked in. The multi-upsell and cross-sell engine, combined with automated affiliate payouts, makes it a conversion-optimized machine for info-product and software sellers.

### Standout Features of Digistore24
- **Lifetime Affiliate Attribution**: Track and reward affiliates across customer lifetime
- **S2S Postback Tracking**: Server-side conversion tracking for ad platforms
- **Multi-Upsell Engine**: Sequential one-click upsells post-purchase
