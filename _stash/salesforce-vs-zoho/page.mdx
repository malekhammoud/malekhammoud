# Salesforce vs Zoho: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Salesforce dominates enterprise AI CRM with unmatched scalability, while Zoho wins for SMBs needing affordable, customizable workflows.

## Deep Dive: Salesforce

Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM, built for Agentic Enterprises where humans and AI agents collaborate through a unified platform. Its architecture centers on Customer 360, integrating AI, data, and pricing into a single ecosystem. Ideal for enterprises needing enterprise-grade pricing management, Salesforce offers Price Designer for complex pricebooks, Pricing Rules for volume/tiered discounts, and Pricing Waterfall for transparent discount tracking. Integrations with Quoting and Order Capture make it a powerhouse for large-scale sales operations.

### Standout Features of Salesforce
- **Pricing Designer**: Create complex pricebooks with dynamic entries
- **Pricing Waterfall**: Transparent, rule-based discount application
- **Unified Platform**: Seamlessly integrates AI, data, and pricing

## Deep Dive: Zoho

Zoho Creator is a low-code platform designed for SMBs to build custom applications quickly. Its modular architecture scales from Starter (10 modules, 10 fields each) to Scale (200 modules, 300 fields each), with unlimited dashboards and custom reports at higher tiers. Workflow Rules (2,500/org) and tiered email notifications (5 to 500/day) make it ideal for automating business processes. Integrations with Zoho Analytics, Campaigns, and Mail 360 enhance its ecosystem for SMBs.

### Standout Features of Zoho
- **Modular Scalability**: Grow from 10 to 200 modules as needed
- **Unlimited Dashboards**: Visualize data without restrictions
- **Low-Code Customization**: Build apps without deep technical expertise

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Salesforce if:**
- You need enterprise-grade AI CRM with advanced pricing tools
- Your team requires unified data + AI + pricing workflows
- You’re scaling beyond SMB needs

**Choose Zoho if:**
- You’re an SMB needing affordable, customizable workflows
- You want to build apps without deep coding expertise
- You need scalable modules and unlimited dashboards
