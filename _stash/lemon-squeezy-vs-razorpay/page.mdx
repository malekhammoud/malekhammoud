## Deep Dive: Lemon Squeezy
Lemon Squeezy is engineered specifically for SaaS businesses operating globally. Its architecture centers around automated tax compliance across 100+ jurisdictions, making it ideal for companies selling software internationally. The platform handles VAT, GST, and sales tax collection automatically, filing returns and remitting payments without manual intervention. Built on a modern REST API with webhook support, it integrates seamlessly with existing tech stacks while providing a no-code checkout option for non-technical teams.

The platform's core strength lies in its A.I.-driven fraud prevention and failed payment recovery systems, which work together to maximize revenue retention. With multi-currency support and localized checkout experiences, Lemon Squeezy eliminates the complexity of cross-border transactions. Its subscription management includes license key generation, making it particularly valuable for software companies that need to control access alongside payments.

### Standout Features of Lemon Squeezy
- **Global tax automation**: Automatically calculates, collects, and remits taxes across 100+ jurisdictions
- **A.I. fraud prevention**: Machine learning algorithms detect and prevent fraudulent transactions in real-time
- **Failed payment recovery**: Smart retry logic and dunning management to recover lost revenue automatically

## Deep Dive: Razorpay
Razorpay is built as an all-in-one financial operating system for businesses in India and emerging markets. Its architecture integrates payment processing, payroll automation, and business banking into a single platform, eliminating the need for multiple financial service providers. The platform supports 100+ payment methods including UPI, net banking, and wallets that are dominant in the Indian market, with settlement times as fast as 24 hours.

The core strength of Razorpay lies in its deep integration with the Indian financial ecosystem. Beyond payments, it offers automated payroll compliance with Indian tax regulations, vendor payouts, and business accounts with zero balance requirements. The unified dashboard provides real-time insights across all financial operations, while the developer-friendly API supports instant deployment with comprehensive documentation and SDKs.

### Standout Features of Razorpay
- **Indian payment methods**: Supports UPI, wallets, net banking, and cards popular in India
- **Automated payroll compliance**: Handles PF, ESIC, and TDS calculations automatically
- **Integrated business banking**: Zero-balance accounts with instant settlements and payouts

## The Final Verdict
Choose Lemon Squeezy if you're running a SaaS business selling globally and need automated tax compliance across multiple countries without the headache of manual filings.

Choose Razorpay if you're operating primarily in India and want a unified platform that handles payments, payroll, and banking within the local financial ecosystem.
