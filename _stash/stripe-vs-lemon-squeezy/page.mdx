> **TL;DR:** Stripe dominates for enterprise-scale complexity and custom billing, while Lemon Squeezy wins for indie SaaS simplicity and rapid deployment.

## Deep Dive: Stripe

Stripe is a financial services platform built for developers who need granular control over payment flows and billing logic. Its API-first architecture supports everything from simple card payments to complex usage-based billing models with tax automation and revenue recognition. Stripe's strength lies in its extensibility—you can build custom billing workflows, integrate with financial accounts, and leverage global payouts across 100+ countries. The platform is ideal for SaaS companies with dedicated engineering teams who need to scale payment infrastructure alongside product complexity.

The platform's core features include Sigma for analytics, Data Pipeline for ETL, Connect for marketplace payouts, and Issuing for virtual cards. Stripe's tax automation handles multi-jurisdiction compliance automatically, while revenue recognition helps SaaS companies manage deferred revenue and ASC 606 compliance. With support for 135+ currencies and 100+ payment methods, Stripe is engineered for global scale but requires significant development investment to implement correctly.

### Standout Features of Stripe
- **Sigma Analytics**: SQL-based ad-hoc analysis of payment data without data export
- **Connect**: Multi-party payouts and marketplace functionality for platforms
- **Revenue Recognition**: Automated deferred revenue calculations and compliance reporting

## Deep Dive: Lemon Squeezy

Lemon Squeezy is an all-in-one SaaS platform designed for indie developers and small teams who want to launch quickly without managing payment infrastructure. The platform bundles payments, subscriptions, tax compliance, and fraud prevention into a single dashboard, eliminating the need for separate services. Lemon Squeezy's no-code checkout forms and license key management make it particularly attractive for digital product creators who lack engineering resources but need professional payment processing.

The platform handles global tax compliance automatically, supports 25+ currencies, and includes AI-powered fraud prevention out of the box. Failed payment recovery and PayPal integration are built-in features that reduce churn without custom development. Lemon Squeezy's pricing model is transparent—5% + 50¢ per transaction covers all fees, including payment processing, so there are no hidden costs or separate merchant account requirements.

### Standout Features of Lemon Squeezy
- **AI Fraud Protection**: Automated fraud detection and prevention without manual configuration
- **License Key Management**: Built-in software license generation and validation for digital products
- **No-Code Checkout**: Customizable checkout forms that work without development effort

## The Final Verdict

Choose Stripe if you need enterprise-grade billing flexibility, have engineering resources for API integration, require custom revenue recognition, or operate a marketplace/platform model.

Choose Lemon Squeezy if you're an indie developer, want to launch in hours instead of weeks, need built-in tax compliance, or prefer predictable pricing without separate merchant accounts.
