## Deep Dive: Zoho
Zoho offers a low-code platform with granular control over CRM architecture. Its tiered structure scales from 10 to 200 modules, supporting 10 to 300 fields per module. Workflow rules cap at 2,500 per org, with email notifications scaling from 5 to 500 daily. Integration with Zoho Analytics and Campaigns enables deep data pipelines, while unlimited dashboards in Growth/Scale tiers support real-time monitoring. Ideal for teams needing custom schemas and complex automation.

### Standout Features of Zoho
- **Modular Architecture:** 10-200 modules with 10-300 fields each
- **Scalable Workflows:** 2,500 rules/org, 2,000 active max
- **Deep Integrations:** Zoho Analytics, Campaigns, Mail 360

## Deep Dive: Podium
Podium leverages AI to streamline lead generation and conversion. Its AI Employee automates responses, while Quick Replies and Conversational Summaries accelerate customer interactions. Review Management and Review Responses enhance reputation, and Text Marketing drives repeat business. Mobile App and Payments enable on-the-go transactions, with Automations and Integrations reducing manual overhead. Best for businesses prioritizing lead capture and AI-driven engagement.

### Standout Features of Podium
- **AI Employee:** Automated lead responses
- **Review Management:** Centralized reputation control
- **Text Marketing:** Direct customer re-engagement
