# Zoho vs Intelli-CTi: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Zoho dominates for scalable custom app development, while Intelli-CTi excels at seamless telephony integration for customer service teams.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Zoho** | **Intelli-CTi** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $5 | £15.00 per user per month (minimum 10 users) |
| **Best For** | Custom app development | CTI-enabled customer service |
| **Core Strength** | Low-code platform flexibility | Real-time caller recognition |

## Deep Dive: Zoho
Zoho Creator is a low-code platform designed for businesses that need custom applications without heavy development overhead. With tiered modules, extensive field limits, and unlimited dashboards on higher plans, it scales from small teams to enterprise deployments. The platform's workflow automation and deep Zoho ecosystem integration make it ideal for businesses seeking operational efficiency and tailored customer experiences.

### Standout Features of Zoho
- **Modular scalability:** 10-200 modules across plans with up to 300 fields per module
- **Workflow automation:** 2,500 org-wide rules with granular email notification controls
- **Ecosystem integration:** Native connectivity with Zoho Analytics, Campaigns, and Mail 360

## Deep Dive: Intelli-CTi
Intelli-CTi is a specialized Computer Telephony Integration (CTI) solution that bridges CRM systems with telephone infrastructure. Its core value lies in real-time caller recognition and one-click dialing, making it a powerful tool for customer service teams handling high call volumes. The solution supports multi-site deployments, Citrix environments, and Chrome browsers, ensuring flexibility across enterprise setups.

### Standout Features of Intelli-CTi
- **Real-time caller ID:** Automatic customer information display on incoming calls
- **Multi-call support:** Handle multiple concurrent calls with on-screen management
- **Post-call automation:** Wrap-up features for activity logging and call statistics tracking

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Zoho if...**
- You need a customizable platform for building internal applications
- Your priority is workflow automation and data management
- You want deep integration with the Zoho ecosystem

**Choose Intelli-CTi if...**
- Your team relies heavily on phone-based customer interactions
- You need seamless CTI integration with existing telephone systems
- Real-time caller recognition and click-to-dial functionality are critical
