Crunchbase delivers a comprehensive database of private company intelligence, focusing on startup funding, financial data, and business insights. The platform excels at providing real-time funding information and company profiles, making it invaluable for investors, researchers, and business development professionals. With access to 4M+ private companies and the ability to export up to 2K accounts monthly, Crunchbase Pro offers scalable solutions for data-driven decision making.

## Deep Dive: LiveRamp
LiveRamp is an enterprise-grade data collaboration platform built around identity resolution and data connectivity. The platform's core strength lies in its ability to safely connect and activate data across multiple channels while maintaining privacy compliance. With its Agentic AI Orchestration and Clean Room Technology, LiveRamp enables sophisticated data strategies for large organizations, focusing on customer experience transformation and business outcome optimization.

### Standout Features of LiveRamp
- **Identity Resolution Engine**: Resolve and translate customer identities across platforms
- **Clean Room Technology**: Secure data collaboration without sharing raw data
- **RampID-powered Connections**: Create persistent identifiers for cross-channel activation
