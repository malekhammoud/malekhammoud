## Deep Dive: Gitter
Gitter is built on Matrix, an open protocol for decentralized, real-time communication. It's engineered for developer communities, offering end-to-end encryption, Markdown/LaTeX support, and unlimited chat history. The platform shines with GitHub integrations, allowing seamless collaboration between code and conversation. Gitter's architecture supports both public and private rooms, making it ideal for open-source projects and internal team discussions.

### Standout Features of Gitter
- **Matrix Protocol**: Decentralized, interoperable architecture for secure messaging
- **GitHub Integration**: Native sync with repositories, issues, and pull requests
- **End-to-End Encryption**: Privacy-first messaging for sensitive team communications

## Deep Dive: Jivo
JivoChat is an omnichannel customer service platform that consolidates live chat, social media, messengers, and phone calls into a single interface. Its free tier includes WhatsApp Business, AI agent, and chatbot capabilities, making it accessible for small businesses. The platform's strength lies in its breadth of integrations—supporting Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, Viber, and Apple Business Chat—and its built-in CRM for managing customer interactions across channels.

### Standout Features of Jivo
- **AI Agent & Assistant**: Automated customer service with intelligent response capabilities
- **Omnichannel Support**: Unified inbox for chat, social media, messengers, and calls
- **Free Tier**: Comprehensive feature set including WhatsApp and chatbots at no cost

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Gitter if:**
- You're building a developer community or open-source project
- You need Matrix-based decentralized communication
- GitHub integration is critical for your workflow

**Choose Jivo if:**
- You need omnichannel customer service across chat, social, and phone
- You want AI-powered automation without upfront costs
- You're a small business needing unified customer communication
