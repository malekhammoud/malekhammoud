# Kinde vs WorkOS: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Choose Kinde for all-in-one SaaS tooling with billing and feature management; choose WorkOS for enterprise-grade SSO and directory sync at scale.

## Deep Dive: Kinde

Kinde is a comprehensive developer platform that combines authentication with billing, feature management, and access control in a single toolkit. Built for startups and growing SaaS companies, Kinde's unified approach eliminates the need to stitch together multiple services. The platform's architecture centers around a single API that handles everything from user sign-up to subscription management, making it ideal for teams that want to move fast without managing complex vendor relationships. With built-in feature flags and billing management, Kinde is particularly strong for product-led growth companies that need to experiment with pricing and functionality.

Kinde's developer experience shines through its intuitive Page Designer for custom auth flows, powerful feature management system, and seamless integration between authentication and monetization. The platform supports passwordless auth, social logins, and enterprise SSO while maintaining a clean, unified dashboard for both technical and non-technical team members. For teams building B2C or SMB-focused SaaS products, Kinde eliminates the operational overhead of managing separate auth, billing, and feature flag services.

### Standout Features of Kinde
- **Unified Billing & Auth**: Single platform for user management and subscription billing
- **Feature Flags**: Built-in experimentation and rollout controls without additional services
- **Page Designer**: Visual tool for customizing authentication flows without frontend work

## Deep Dive: WorkOS

WorkOS is an enterprise-focused developer platform that specializes in making applications enterprise-ready through robust SSO, directory sync, and audit capabilities. The platform's architecture is optimized for handling complex enterprise authentication scenarios, including SAML, OIDC, and SCIM integrations with major identity providers like Azure AD, Okta, and Google Workspace. WorkOS's modular approach allows developers to implement enterprise features incrementally, making it ideal for B2B SaaS companies targeting enterprise customers who need compliance and security features.

WorkOS excels in its directory sync capabilities, allowing seamless user provisioning and deprovisioning across enterprise systems. The platform's audit logging and role-based access control (RBAC) provide the compliance and governance features that enterprise customers demand. With its generous free tier covering up to 1 million monthly active users, WorkOS is particularly attractive for startups and scale-ups that need enterprise features without immediate cost commitment. The platform's API-first design and comprehensive documentation make integration straightforward for engineering teams.

### Standout Features of WorkOS
- **Enterprise SSO**: Native support for SAML, OIDC, and major identity providers
- **Directory Sync**: SCIM-based user provisioning and deprovisioning
- **Audit Logs**: Comprehensive compliance and governance tracking

## The Final Verdict

Choose Kinde if you're building a B2C or SMB-focused SaaS product and want an all-in-one platform that handles authentication, billing, and feature management without stitching together multiple services. Kinde is ideal for teams that prioritize speed-to-market and want unified tooling for both technical and business operations.

Choose WorkOS if you're targeting enterprise customers and need robust SSO, directory sync, and compliance features. WorkOS is the better choice for B2B SaaS companies that require enterprise authentication standards and need to support complex identity provider integrations at scale.
