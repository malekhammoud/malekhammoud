# Stripe vs Braintree: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Stripe dominates for global scale and advanced features, while Braintree excels for PayPal-centric businesses and simpler implementations.

## Deep Dive: Stripe
Stripe is a comprehensive financial platform built for developers who need granular control over their payment infrastructure. Its API-first architecture supports everything from basic card processing to complex billing models with usage-based metering, tax automation, and revenue recognition. The platform shines with its extensive feature set including Stripe Sigma for analytics, Data Pipeline for ETL, and Connect for marketplace functionality. Ideal for startups scaling to enterprise, Stripe handles 135+ currencies and offers advanced fraud prevention through Radar.

### Standout Features of Stripe
- **Revenue Recognition**: Automated accounting compliance for GAAP/IFRS reporting
- **Stripe Sigma**: SQL-based analytics on your transaction data
- **Connect**: Built-in marketplace payouts and multi-party payments

## Deep Dive: Braintree
Braintree, a PayPal service, delivers a streamlined payment solution optimized for businesses already embedded in the PayPal ecosystem. Its SDK-first approach simplifies integration with pre-built UI components and supports multiple payment methods including Venmo, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. The platform handles PCI compliance automatically and includes adaptive fraud management through PayPal's network. With global payouts in 50+ currencies and recurring billing capabilities, Braintree targets SMBs and mid-market companies seeking quick PayPal integration without extensive customization.

### Standout Features of Braintree
- **PayPal Integration**: Native support for PayPal and Venmo payments
- **SDK-First Development**: Pre-built UI components for rapid implementation
- **Adaptive Fraud Management**: Leverages PayPal's extensive fraud detection network

## The Final Verdict
Choose Stripe if you need:
- Advanced billing models and subscription management
- Enterprise-grade analytics and reporting
- Global scale with extensive currency support
- Marketplace functionality with Connect

Choose Braintree if you:
- Already use PayPal and need seamless integration
- Want faster implementation with pre-built SDKs
- Target customers who prefer PayPal/Venmo
- Need a simpler solution without extensive customization
