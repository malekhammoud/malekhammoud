# Clerk vs Ory: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** **Clerk wins for startups and SMBs** with its generous free tier and developer-friendly pricing; **Ory dominates enterprises** with advanced security, compliance, and multi-region deployments.

## At a Glance Comparison

## Deep Dive: Clerk
Clerk delivers a modern, out-of-the-box authentication experience with a focus on developer speed and simplicity. Its generous free tier (50,000 MRU) and per-app limits make it ideal for startups and growing teams. Built for B2C and B2B apps, Clerk offers social sign-on, MFA, magic links, and a polished UI kit that can be deployed in minutes. The platform emphasizes rapid integration with SDKs for React, Vue, and mobile, plus robust webhooks and user management APIs.

### Standout Features of Clerk
- **50K Free MRU**: No-cost entry point for startups scaling to 50K monthly users
- **Social & Magic Auth**: Built-in SSO with Google, GitHub, and magic link support
- **Developer-First UI**: Themeable, no-code auth pages with React/Vue SDKs

## Deep Dive: Ory
Ory is an enterprise-grade, modular IAM platform built for security, compliance, and global scale. It offers advanced features like multi-region deployments, GDPR-compliant EU data storage, and enterprise integrations for legacy systems. Ory's pricing is fixed at $770/year but includes premium support, SLAs, and features like B2B SSO (OIDC), event firehose, and fine-grained permissions. It's designed for organizations needing robust compliance (ISO, SOC2), advanced threat protection, and multi-tenant architectures.

### Standout Features of Ory
- **Enterprise Security**: ISO, SOC2, GDPR compliance with advanced threat protection
- **Multi-Region Deployments**: Data residency and global scaling for enterprise needs
- **Event Firehose**: Real-time analytics and integration with data lakes

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Clerk if...**
- You're a startup or SMB needing a free tier up to 50K users
- You want fast, developer-friendly integration with polished UI components
- Your priority is rapid deployment over enterprise compliance

**Choose Ory if...**
- You require enterprise-grade security, compliance, and multi-region deployments
- You need advanced features like event firehose, B2B SSO, and legacy system integration
- You're willing to pay a premium for 99.99% uptime SLA and premium support
