# Loops vs Brevo: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Loops wins for developer-first transactional email with intelligent deliverability; Brevo wins for all-in-one multichannel marketing automation.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Loops** | **Brevo** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free | $0 |
| **Best For** | Developer-focused email | Multichannel marketing |

## Deep Dive: Loops
Loops is engineered for developers who need transactional email without the marketing fluff. Its intelligent queueing system optimizes deliverability by managing send rates and handling retries automatically. The complete DNS handling eliminates configuration headaches, while the simple integration approach works seamlessly with your existing stack. Loops treats email like code—you craft emails like writing a doc, save reusable themes and components, and build automated customer journeys with developer-friendly tools.

### Standout Features of Loops
- Intelligent queueing for maximum deliverability
- Complete DNS handling out-of-the-box
- Component-based email building for reusable templates
- Simple API integration with minimal configuration

## Deep Dive: Brevo
Brevo positions itself as an all-in-one customer engagement platform rather than just email. Beyond transactional messaging, it offers comprehensive sales management with custom pipelines, multichannel automation across email, SMS, WhatsApp, and chat, plus a full customer loyalty program. The Brevo Data Platform unifies customer data for smarter marketing decisions. With 150+ integrations including Shopify, WordPress, and Stripe, Brevo excels for businesses needing a unified marketing stack rather than just transactional email infrastructure.

### Standout Features of Brevo
- Multichannel automation (email, SMS, WhatsApp, chat)
- Integrated sales pipeline management
- 150+ native integrations with major platforms
- Customer loyalty program built-in

## The Final Verdict
Choose **Loops** if you're a developer-focused team that needs reliable, high-deliverability transactional email without the complexity of multichannel marketing features. Choose **Brevo** if you need an all-in-one platform that combines transactional email with comprehensive marketing automation, sales management, and customer loyalty features.
