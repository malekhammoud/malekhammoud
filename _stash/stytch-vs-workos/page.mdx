# Stytch vs WorkOS: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Stytch wins for startups needing comprehensive fraud prevention; WorkOS dominates for enterprise SSO and directory sync.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Stytch** | **WorkOS** |

## Deep Dive: Stytch
Stytch is a modern identity platform built for developers who need comprehensive authentication with built-in security. Its architecture emphasizes device-aware MFA, intelligent rate limiting, and bot detection as first-class citizens rather than afterthoughts. The platform shines for teams building consumer apps or B2B SaaS that need to balance user experience with fraud prevention. Stytch's SCIM provisioning, just-in-time controls, and pre-built UI components make it particularly strong for multi-tenant SaaS applications.

### Standout Features of Stytch
- **Device-aware multifactor authentication** that adapts security based on device trust levels
- **Intelligent rate limiting and bot detection** built directly into the auth flow
- **Just-in-time provisioning controls** by email domain and SSO connection for automated user management

## Deep Dive: WorkOS
WorkOS positions itself as the enterprise-ready auth platform, with a laser focus on getting B2B SaaS applications enterprise-ready quickly. Its architecture prioritizes Single Sign-On (SSO) with SAML, LDAP, and OAuth providers, making it the go-to choice for teams needing to integrate with enterprise identity providers. WorkOS excels at Directory Sync for automated user provisioning and deprovisioning, and its comprehensive audit logging satisfies enterprise compliance requirements. The platform's AuthKit and Admin Portal provide white-labeled experiences without heavy customization.

### Standout Features of WorkOS
- **Enterprise SSO with 20+ providers** including SAML, LDAP, and OAuth
- **Directory Sync** for automated user provisioning and deprovisioning
- **Comprehensive audit logging** for compliance and security monitoring

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Stytch if:**
- You need built-in fraud prevention and bot detection
- Your application requires device-aware MFA
- You're building a consumer-facing app or multi-tenant SaaS

**Choose WorkOS if:**
- Enterprise SSO integration is your primary requirement
- You need comprehensive directory sync capabilities
- Audit logging and compliance features are critical
