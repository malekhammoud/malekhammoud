# Contentful vs Storyblok: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Contentful wins for enterprise-scale personalization and AI-driven analytics, while Storyblok dominates for startups needing a free, intuitive visual editor.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Contentful** | **Storyblok** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $15/month | Free |
| **Best For** | Enterprise personalization | Visual-first startups |
| **Core Strength** | AI-driven analytics | Visual editing + free tier |

## Deep Dive: Contentful
Contentful is a DXP-first headless CMS built for enterprises that need AI-powered personalization at scale. Its agentic analytics integrate directly into workflows, enabling real-time content performance tracking and audience definition. The platform excels at assembling rich digital experiences visually while automating workflows and leveraging enterprise-grade integrations. With partnerships and scalable solutions, Contentful targets teams requiring sophisticated content operations and data-driven optimization.

### Standout Features of Contentful
- **Agentic Analytics**: Real-time content performance insights embedded in your workflow
- **AI-Powered Personalization**: Define audiences and experiment with confidence using built-in AI
- **Enterprise Scale**: Robust partnerships and solutions for large organizations

## Deep Dive: Storyblok
Storyblok is an intuitive headless CMS designed for developer-marketing collaboration, offering a generous free tier that includes 1 space, 1 user seat, 100GB traffic/month, and 100k AI credits. Its visual editor stands out as a core differentiator, allowing non-technical users to see content changes in real-time. The platform includes essential SEO meta tags, a content delivery API, and integrations with tools like Shopify, making it ideal for teams wanting speed to market without complexity.

### Standout Features of Storyblok
- **Visual Editor**: Real-time content preview that bridges dev/marketing gaps
- **Generous Free Tier**: 100GB traffic and 100k AI credits at no cost
- **Developer-Friendly API**: Clean content delivery API with essential integrations

## The Final Verdict
Choose Contentful if:
- You need enterprise-grade personalization and AI analytics
- Your team requires sophisticated workflow automation
- You're scaling to enterprise-level content operations

Choose Storyblok if:
- You want a free tier with generous limits to start
- Visual editing is crucial for your marketing team
- You need fast implementation without enterprise complexity
