## Deep Dive: Tidio
Tidio's architecture centers around its Lyro AI Agent, which leverages natural language processing to automate up to 67% of customer inquiries without human intervention. Built specifically for e-commerce, it integrates natively with Shopify and WordPress, offering flows that trigger based on cart abandonment, product views, and purchase history. The shared inbox consolidates conversations from live chat, email, and social channels, while the help desk transforms conversations into trackable tickets with SLA management.

Ideal for SMBs and growing e-commerce stores, Tidio's strength lies in its ability to deflect repetitive queries through AI while maintaining human escalation paths. The analytics dashboard provides conversion tracking and agent performance metrics, making it particularly valuable for revenue-focused support teams that need to prove ROI on their customer service investments.

### Standout Features of Tidio
- **Lyro AI Agent**: Automates up to 67% of inquiries using NLP, reducing support costs while maintaining quality
- **E-commerce Flows**: Pre-built automation for cart abandonment, product recommendations, and post-purchase follow-ups
- **Shopify Integration**: Native plugin that syncs customer data, orders, and product catalogs for contextual support

## Deep Dive: Crisp Instant Messaging
Crisp operates as a comprehensive messaging hub designed for SaaS companies and digital-first businesses. Its architecture supports real-time communication across web chat, mobile apps, social media, and email through a single API. The platform's AI Agent handles routine queries while the shared inbox provides a unified view of all customer interactions, complete with conversation history and customer context pulled from the integrated CRM.

The platform shines in its extensibility—offering Chat SDK for custom implementations, status pages for incident communication, and campaign tools for proactive outreach. Crisp's knowledge base integration allows automated responses based on help center content, while the ticketing system ensures complex issues get proper tracking and resolution workflows.

### Standout Features of Crisp Instant Messaging
- **Multichannel Messaging Hub**: Consolidates web chat, email, social media, and mobile app conversations in one unified inbox
- **Chat SDK**: Fully customizable chat widget with native SDKs for iOS, Android, and web frameworks
- **Status Page Integration**: Built-in incident communication tool that keeps customers informed during outages

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Tidio if...**
- You run an e-commerce store on Shopify or WordPress

**Choose Crisp Instant Messaging if...**
- You're a SaaS company needing multichannel support across web, mobile, and social
- You require a customizable chat experience with Chat SDK
