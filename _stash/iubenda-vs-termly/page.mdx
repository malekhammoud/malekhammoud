# Iubenda vs Termly: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Termly dominates for comprehensive compliance automation; Iubenda wins for global accessibility and cookie consent.

## Deep Dive: Iubenda
Iubenda delivers a 360° compliance solution engineered for global data privacy laws. Its architecture integrates cookie consent management, privacy policy generation, and website accessibility tools into a unified platform. The system automatically updates policies for GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, and other regulations, with multi-language support baked in. Iubenda's accessibility features ensure WCAG compliance alongside legal requirements, making it ideal for businesses needing comprehensive regulatory coverage across multiple jurisdictions.

### Standout Features of Iubenda
- **Global Compliance Engine**: Automatic updates for GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, and 10+ regulations
- **Website Accessibility Integration**: Built-in WCAG compliance tools alongside legal features
- **Multi-language Support**: Native policy generation in 10+ languages with automatic updates

## Deep Dive: Termly
Termly operates as a specialized compliance automation platform focused on policy generation and consent management. Its architecture centers on a robust policy generator system that creates Privacy Policies, Terms & Conditions, Cookie Policies, and 8+ additional legal documents. The platform includes a consent management platform with cookie scanning, banner generation, and IAB TCF 2.3 implementation. Termly's DSAR form creation and Google Consent Mode integration make it particularly strong for businesses requiring granular consent tracking and data subject rights management.

### Standout Features of Termly
- **Comprehensive Policy Generation**: 12+ document types including EULA, Return Policy, and Shipping Policy
- **Advanced Consent Management**: IAB TCF 2.3 implementation with Google Consent Mode integration
- **Automated Compliance Updates**: Regular regulation monitoring with automatic policy updates

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Iubenda if:**
- You need integrated website accessibility compliance
- You operate across multiple global jurisdictions
- You want a unified platform for legal and accessibility requirements

**Choose Termly if:**
- You require extensive policy document generation
- You need advanced consent management with IAB compliance
- You want granular control over data subject rights management
