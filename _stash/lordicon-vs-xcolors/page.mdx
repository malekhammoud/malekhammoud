## Deep Dive: Lordicon

Built with developers in mind, it supports multiple export formats including Lottie, GIF, MP4, WebP, PNG, and SVG, ensuring seamless integration across platforms. Ideal for product teams, content creators, and agencies needing motion design assets without the overhead of custom animation work. The platform's guest access feature allows collaboration with up to 5 team members, making it practical for distributed design workflows.
