## Deep Dive: OpenCart
OpenCart is a PHP-based, open-source e-commerce framework designed for developers who need complete control over their store's architecture. With over 13,000 modules and themes available, it offers unparalleled customization potential. The built-in SEO capabilities and support for multiple payment gateways make it ideal for businesses planning to scale aggressively. Being self-hosted means you own your data and can modify every aspect of the platform without vendor lock-in.

### Standout Features of OpenCart
- **13,000+ extensions** for virtually any functionality
- **Built-in SEO** with customizable URLs and meta tags
- **Multi-store support** from a single installation
- **Payment gateway agnostic** - integrate any provider

## Deep Dive: Sellfy
Sellfy is a cloud-based e-commerce platform built specifically for creators who want to sell digital products, subscriptions, and print-on-demand items without touching code. The platform handles hosting, security, and payment processing out of the box, allowing users to launch in minutes. With features like email marketing automation, customizable templates, and 24/7 support, Sellfy eliminates technical barriers while maintaining professional-grade functionality.

### Standout Features of Sellfy
- **Zero-code store builder** with customizable templates
- **Print-on-demand integration** for physical products
- **Built-in email marketing** and automated campaigns
- **Secure payments** with no technical setup required

## The Final Verdict
**Choose OpenCart if:**
- You need complete control over your store's architecture
- You plan to scale beyond basic e-commerce needs
- You have technical resources for setup and maintenance
- You want to avoid recurring SaaS fees

**Choose Sellfy if:**
- You're a creator selling digital products or subscriptions
- You need to launch quickly without technical expertise
- You want an all-in-one solution with built-in marketing
- You prefer predictable monthly pricing over infrastructure costs
