## Deep Dive: Sanity
Sanity is a fully customizable, all-code backend built for AI, automations, and applications. Its Content Lake database and Live CDN provide real-time content delivery with built-in event handlers for content changes. The platform excels at AI-assisted workflows through Canvas (free-form writing) and Content Agent (AI for content operations), making it ideal for teams needing sophisticated content operations with minimal manual intervention.

The platform's strength lies in its developer experience—Sanity Studio offers a headless CMS with customizable schemas, while the App SDK enables rapid content app development. Content Releases allows teams to stack and stage updates, and Insights provides analytics for content performance. With Agent Actions providing built-in, content-aware AI, Sanity creates a seamless pipeline from content creation to distribution.

### Standout Features of Sanity
- **Content Agent**: AI-powered content operations that automate workflows and content management
- **Canvas**: AI-assisted, free-form writing environment for collaborative content creation
- **Live CDN**: Real-time, scalable content delivery with built-in event handling

## Deep Dive: Strapi
Strapi is the leading open-source Headless CMS that gives developers complete freedom over their tech stack. As an open-source solution, it allows teams to self-host on their infrastructure, providing full control over data, security, and customization. The platform focuses on API-first architecture, enabling developers to create robust APIs and manage content through a customizable admin panel.

Strapi's strength lies in its flexibility and extensibility—developers can use their favorite frameworks while editors manage content through an intuitive interface. The platform supports multiple databases, offers extensive plugin ecosystem through Strapi Market, and includes Strapi AI for enhanced functionality. Enterprise Edition provides additional features for large organizations, while Strapi Cloud offers managed hosting options.

### Standout Features of Strapi
- **Open-source Architecture**: Complete control over code, hosting, and customization
- **API-first Design**: Create powerful APIs with built-in REST and GraphQL support
- **Plugin Ecosystem**: Extensive marketplace with pre-built integrations and extensions

## The Final Verdict

Choose Sanity if you need:
- AI-powered content workflows and automation
- Real-time collaboration with built-in analytics
- Seamless integration with modern applications

Choose Strapi if you need:
- Complete control over your CMS infrastructure
- Self-hosting capabilities with open-source flexibility
- Extensive customization and plugin ecosystem
