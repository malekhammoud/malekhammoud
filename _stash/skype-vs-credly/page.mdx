## Deep Dive: Skype
Skype operates as a pay-as-you-go communication platform, offering affordable local and international calling with SMS capabilities. Its credit-based model eliminates subscription fees, making it ideal for occasional users needing flexible communication options. The platform supports emergency calling in select regions, though with limitations.

### Standout Features of Skype
- **Pay-as-you-go model**: No subscriptions, pay only for what you use
- **Global reach**: Affordable international calling rates
- **SMS integration**: Send text messages alongside voice calls

## Deep Dive: Credly
Credly provides an end-to-end digital credential management system designed for enterprise organizations. The platform automates badge issuance, validates achievements, and delivers workforce analytics through a connected credentialing network. Organizations leverage Credly for strategic workforce planning and candidate assessment.

### Standout Features of Credly
- **Automated badge issuance**: Create and distribute verified digital credentials
- **Workforce analytics**: Map employee skills across the organization
- **Strategic planning tools**: Identify future skill requirements and learning paths

## The Final Verdict
Choose Skype if you need affordable, flexible communication without subscription commitments and primarily use calling/SMS services.

Choose Credly if you require enterprise-grade digital credential management, workforce skill mapping, and automated achievement recognition systems.
