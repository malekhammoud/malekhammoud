> **TL;DR:** WooCommerce dominates for full-stack, customizable e-commerce; Oberlo wins for dropshipping simplicity.

## Deep Dive: WooCommerce
WooCommerce is a powerful, open-source e-commerce plugin for WordPress, built for developers and merchants who want complete control over their online store. With tiered pricing rules, editable labels, and deep WordPress integration, it's ideal for businesses that need custom workflows and scalability. Its architecture allows for extensive plugin support, API access, and theme customization, making it a go-to for long-term, self-hosted e-commerce.

### Standout Features of WooCommerce
- **Rules-Based Tier Pricing**: Automate discounts for bulk orders with conditional logic.
- **Editable Tier Pricing Labels**: Customize pricing displays to match your brand voice.
- **Open-Source Flexibility**: Modify code, extend functionality, and integrate with third-party tools.

## Deep Dive: Oberlo
Oberlo is a dropshipping platform designed for entrepreneurs who want to launch an online store without handling inventory, packaging, or shipping. It integrates seamlessly with Shopify and offers business tools like a profit margin calculator, business name generator, and traffic calculator. With access to blogs, ebooks, and podcasts, Oberlo supports users with educational content and pricing strategies tailored for dropshipping success.

### Standout Features of Oberlo
- **Profit Margin Calculator**: Instantly calculate profitability for product pricing.
- **Business Name Generator**: Generate unique, brandable names for your store.
- **Educational Resources**: Access blogs, ebooks, and videos to grow your dropshipping knowledge.

## The Final Verdict
Choose WooCommerce if:
- You want full control over your store's code and design.
- You need advanced pricing rules and WordPress integration.
- You're building a long-term, scalable e-commerce business.

Choose Oberlo if:
- You want to start dropshipping without inventory management.
- You need built-in business tools and educational content.
- You're looking for a quick, low-risk way to launch an online store.
