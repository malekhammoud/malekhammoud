# LogRocket vs Highlight: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** LogRocket wins for AI-powered UX insights, Highlight wins for open-source flexibility and full-stack visibility.

## Deep Dive: LogRocket

LogRocket is a product analytics platform that combines session replay with AI-powered struggle detection to pinpoint where users experience friction. Its architecture captures DOM state, network logs, console errors, and user interactions in real-time, reconstructing sessions with pixel-perfect accuracy. Designed for product managers, UX designers, and developers, LogRocket excels at identifying conversion blockers and optimizing user flows. The platform's AI automatically surfaces patterns in user behavior, making it ideal for teams focused on improving engagement and retention metrics.

### Standout Features of LogRocket
- **AI-powered Struggle Detection**: Automatically identifies where users struggle and surfaces insights without manual analysis
- **Session Replay with DOM State**: Reconstructs exact user sessions including network logs, console errors, and pixel-perfect UI
- **Performance Monitoring**: Tracks Core Web Vitals and performance metrics to optimize load times and responsiveness

## Deep Dive: Highlight

Highlight is an open-source, full-stack monitoring platform built for developers who need complete visibility into their web applications. Its architecture leverages OpenTelemetry for distributed tracing, combining session replay, error monitoring, logging, and custom dashboards in a unified interface. Highlight's self-hosting capability and extensive SDK support make it ideal for engineering teams that require full control over their observability stack. The platform provides granular session search, customizable alerting, and powerful privacy controls, making it suitable for both frontend and backend debugging scenarios.

### Standout Features of Highlight
- **OpenTelemetry Integration**: Native support for OpenTelemetry standards enabling seamless distributed tracing across services
- **Self-Hosting Capability**: Deploy on your own infrastructure with full data control and compliance with privacy regulations
- **Comprehensive Session Search**: Advanced filtering and search capabilities across logs, traces, and session data for rapid debugging

## The Final Verdict

Choose LogRocket if you need AI-driven UX insights and struggle detection to optimize user experience and conversion rates. It's perfect for product teams focused on understanding user behavior and identifying friction points in the customer journey.

Choose Highlight if you require open-source flexibility, full-stack visibility, and distributed tracing capabilities. It's ideal for engineering teams that need complete control over their observability infrastructure and want to leverage OpenTelemetry standards.
