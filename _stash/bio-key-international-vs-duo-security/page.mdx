## Deep Dive: BIO-key International

BIO-key PortalGuard delivers a biometric-first identity and access management platform built for organizations requiring hardware-backed, identity-bound authentication. Its architecture supports over 15 MFA options, including fingerprint, facial, and voice biometrics, integrated directly into the authentication flow. The platform is highly customizable with on-premises, cloud, or hybrid deployment models, making it ideal for regulated industries like healthcare, finance, and government. PortalGuard's identity-bound biometrics ensure credentials are tied to the user, not the device, reducing credential theft risks.

The solution excels in environments where user verification must be irrefutable and tamper-resistant. Its self-service password reset and single sign-on capabilities reduce help desk overhead, while centralized policy management ensures consistent security across distributed teams. BIO-key's focus on biometric integration makes it a strong fit for organizations prioritizing hardware-backed identity assurance over pure software-based MFA.

### Standout Features of BIO-key International
- **Identity-Bound Biometrics**: Credentials tied to the user, not the device, for tamper-resistant authentication.
- **Flexible Deployment**: On-premises, cloud, or hybrid options for regulated industries.
- **Customizable Workflows**: Tailor user interfaces and authentication policies to specific needs.

## Deep Dive: Duo Security

Duo Security is a cloud-native IAM solution designed for modern enterprises prioritizing phishing-resistant MFA and endpoint security. Its architecture integrates seamlessly with existing directories and supports over 150 pre-built integrations, making deployment fast and scalable. Duo's phishing-resistant MFA uses WebAuthn and hardware tokens, while its Trusted Endpoints feature ensures only healthy, compliant devices can access applications. The platform also includes AI-driven risk-based authentication and identity threat detection, providing proactive security against emerging threats.

Duo Essentials is priced at $3/user/month, offering a cost-effective entry point for SMBs and enterprises alike. Its Session Theft Protection and Device Health Checks add layers of security beyond traditional MFA, making it ideal for distributed teams and remote workforces. Duo's focus on simplicity and scalability makes it a go-to choice for organizations needing robust IAM without heavy customization.

### Standout Features of Duo Security
- **Phishing-Resistant MFA**: WebAuthn and hardware tokens protect against credential-based attacks.
- **Trusted Endpoints**: Only healthy, compliant devices can access applications.
- **Risk-Based Authentication**: AI-driven policies adapt to user behavior and threat levels.

## The Final Verdict

Choose BIO-key International if:

Choose Duo Security if:
