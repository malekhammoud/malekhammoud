## Deep Dive: SurveyMonkey
SurveyMonkey delivers enterprise-grade survey infrastructure with AI-powered creation, 200+ integrations, and research panel access. Its flexible admin controls and benchmarking capabilities make it ideal for large organizations conducting market research, employee feedback, and customer satisfaction studies. The platform emphasizes data protection and compliance features, ensuring enterprise security standards.

### Standout Features of SurveyMonkey
- AI-powered survey creation with intelligent question suggestions
- Research panel access for targeted audience sampling
- Multi-survey analysis and benchmarking against industry standards
- 200+ integrations including Salesforce, Slack, and Microsoft Teams

## Deep Dive: JobPotato
JobPotato (Tally) provides a privacy-first form builder with unlimited forms and submissions at zero cost. Built with GDPR compliance and European hosting, it offers conditional logic, calculated fields, and custom CSS without coding requirements. The platform supports payment collection, file uploads, and multi-page forms, making it perfect for businesses prioritizing data privacy and cost efficiency.

### Standout Features of JobPotato
- Unlimited forms and submissions with no hidden costs
- GDPR compliance and European data hosting
- Advanced form logic including conditional fields and calculations
- Payment collection and file upload capabilities

## The Final Verdict

Choose SurveyMonkey if:
- You need AI-powered survey insights and benchmarking
- Enterprise research with 200+ integrations is critical
- You require research panel access for targeted sampling

Choose JobPotato if:
- You want unlimited free forms with no subscription costs
- Data privacy and GDPR compliance are non-negotiable
- You need advanced form logic without coding complexity
