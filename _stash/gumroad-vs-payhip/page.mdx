## Deep Dive: Gumroad
Gumroad's architecture is built for creators who want to monetize instantly without setup friction. Its minimalist approach strips away complexity—no storefront builder, no marketing tools, just a direct path from creation to sale. The platform handles multi-currency payments and supports one-time, recurring, and fixed-length payments out of the box. Ideal for solo creators, artists, and indie developers who prioritize speed over feature bloat.

### Standout Features of Gumroad
- **Sell Anything**: Upload files, products, or services without restrictions
- **Automated Workflows**: Set up payment triggers and delivery sequences instantly
- **Global Currency Support**: Customers pay in their local currency automatically

## Deep Dive: Payhip
Payhip offers a comprehensive e-commerce platform with a built-in storefront builder and extensive feature set. The $5.95 starting price unlocks digital downloads, online courses, memberships, and physical products. Its architecture supports VAT/tax handling, email marketing integrations, and multiple payment gateways. Perfect for creators who need a complete business infrastructure without third-party tools.

### Standout Features of Payhip
- **All-in-One Store Builder**: Create a professional digital storefront without coding
- **VAT & Tax Automation**: Built-in compliance for global sales
- **Email Marketing Tools**: Integrated campaigns and customer nurturing

## The Final Verdict
Choose Gumroad if you need to start selling immediately with zero setup, value simplicity over features, or want to test monetization ideas quickly.

Choose Payhip if you need a complete digital storefront, require VAT/tax handling, or want integrated marketing tools for scaling your business.
