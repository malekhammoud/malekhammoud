# Crowdcast vs Streamlabs: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Crowdcast wins for structured webinar hosting with analytics, while Streamlabs dominates for live streaming production and viewer engagement.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Crowdcast** | **Streamlabs** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $49/mo | $27/mo |
| **Best For** | Webinar event hosting | Live streaming production |
| **Core Strength** | Multi-session events | Multi-platform streaming |

## Deep Dive: Crowdcast
Crowdcast is purpose-built for webinar-style events with its multi-session architecture and RTMP broadcasting mode. The platform handles up to 100+ live attendees with HD recording and session analytics baked in. Its Zapier integration and email export capabilities make it ideal for lead generation workflows. The 10-hour monthly streaming limit works well for scheduled events but may constrain daily content creators.

### Standout Features of Crowdcast
- Multi-session event management for complex webinar schedules
- RTMP Mode for professional broadcasting workflows
- Stripe integration for paid events with analytics tracking
- Export capabilities for attendee data and chat logs

## Deep Dive: Streamlabs
Streamlabs is an all-in-one streaming suite designed for content creators broadcasting to multiple platforms simultaneously. With access to 1000+ overlays and 60+ apps, it's built for visual customization and viewer interaction. The platform supports up to 11 guests/cameras and offers 250GB storage with 1-hour video limits. Pro features include multistreaming, 1080p/60fps output, and custom cloudbot functionality for automated moderation.

### Standout Features of Streamlabs
- Multi-platform streaming to 3+ destinations simultaneously
- 1000+ overlay themes and 60+ app integrations
- Custom tip pages with highest profit margins
- Cloudbot automation with custom commands

## The Final Verdict
Choose Crowdcast if you need structured webinar hosting with analytics, multi-session events, and lead capture capabilities for professional presentations.

Choose Streamlabs if you're a content creator needing multistreaming, visual customization, and viewer engagement tools for platforms like Twitch and YouTube.
