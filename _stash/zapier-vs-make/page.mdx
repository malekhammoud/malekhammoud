# Zapier vs Make: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Zapier wins for simplicity and app breadth, Make dominates for visual workflow power and scalability.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Zapier** | **Make** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $0 /mo (Free plan) | $0 |
| **Best For** | SMBs & quick automation | Enterprise & complex workflows |
| **Core Strength** | 8,000+ app integrations | Visual scenario builder |

## Deep Dive: Zapier

Zapier is built for speed and simplicity. Its linear "Zap" model chains triggers and actions in a straightforward sequence, making it ideal for non-technical users who need to connect apps without wrestling with logic. With 8,000+ apps, Zapier covers nearly every SaaS tool on the market, and its pay-per-task billing means you only pay for what you use. Enterprise features like conditional paths, multi-step Zaps, and advanced admin permissions make it surprisingly capable for mid-market teams, though its linear flow limits complex branching.

### Standout Features of Zapier
- **8,000+ app integrations** - unmatched breadth for niche SaaS connections
- **AI automation** - built-in GPT-powered text generation and categorization
- **Enterprise-grade security** - SOC 2 Type II, GDPR compliance, and SSO

## Deep Dive: Make

Make is a visual-first automation powerhouse. Its drag-and-drop scenario builder lets you create complex, branching workflows with custom variables, functions, and real-time execution monitoring. While it supports "only" 3,000+ apps, each integration is deeply customizable with API-level control. Make's unlimited active scenarios on paid plans and priority execution make it ideal for high-volume, mission-critical automation. The learning curve is steeper, but the payoff is enterprise-grade scalability and control that Zapier can't match.

### Standout Features of Make
- **Visual scenario builder** - drag-and-drop workflow design with infinite branching
- **Custom variables & functions** - programmatic logic without writing code
- **Real-time execution monitoring** - live debugging and performance tracking

## The Final Verdict

Choose Zapier if:
- You need to connect a niche or legacy app Zapier supports
- Your team prioritizes speed over complexity
- You want predictable pay-per-task pricing

Choose Make if:
- You need complex, branching workflows with custom logic
- Your automation volume justifies unlimited scenarios
- You prefer visual programming over linear flows
