## Deep Dive: Podium
Podium is a purpose-built CRM platform leveraging AI to automate lead capture, qualification, and conversion. Its architecture centers around conversational AI that handles customer interactions across SMS, web chat, and reviews. The platform integrates payments, surveys, and marketing automation into a unified communication hub. Ideal for small to medium businesses needing scalable customer engagement without manual intervention.

### Standout Features of Podium
- **AI Employee**: Autonomous conversational AI that qualifies and converts leads 24/7
- **Review Management**: Centralized platform for generating and responding to customer reviews
- **Quick Replies & Summaries**: AI-powered response suggestions and conversation recaps

## Deep Dive: ACD Systems
ACDSee is a legacy digital asset management and creative editing suite—not a CRM. Its feature set includes AI-powered photo organization, RAW editing, and video production tools. While technically sophisticated for media workflows, it lacks any customer relationship management capabilities. The platform serves photographers, videographers, and creative professionals who need robust editing and asset organization, not sales teams or customer service departments.

### Standout Features of ACD Systems
- **AI Keywords & Facial Recognition**: Automated metadata tagging for media assets
- **RAW Photo Editing**: Professional-grade non-destructive image editing
- **Layered Editing & Portrait Retouching**: Advanced creative tools for photographers

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Podium if:** You need a modern CRM with AI-powered lead generation, review management, and customer communication tools.

**Choose ACD Systems if:** You're a creative professional needing photo/video editing and digital asset management (not CRM functionality).
