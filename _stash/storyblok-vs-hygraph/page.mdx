# Storyblok vs Hygraph: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Storyblok wins for marketing teams needing visual editing; Hygraph excels for enterprise-grade content architecture and AI-powered workflows.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Storyblok** | **Hygraph** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free | $0 |
| **Best For** | Visual content creation | Enterprise content modeling |
| **Core Strength** | Visual editor + collaboration | Content architecture + AI |

## Deep Dive: Storyblok
Storyblok delivers a visual-first headless CMS built for marketing teams and developers to collaborate seamlessly. Its visual editor provides real-time content previews, making it ideal for content-heavy websites and e-commerce experiences. With 100GB monthly traffic and 100k AI credits included, it's optimized for teams needing rapid content deployment without complex backend management. The platform shines in scenarios where non-technical users need to create and manage content independently.

### Standout Features of Storyblok
- **Visual Editor**: Real-time content preview with drag-and-drop components
- **E-commerce Ready**: Native Shopify integration and product management
- **Collaboration Tools**: Multi-user editing with role-based permissions and content staging

## Deep Dive: Hygraph
Hygraph positions itself as an AI-powered headless CMS for enterprises requiring sophisticated content modeling and global scalability. Built on GraphQL, it offers advanced content relationships, federated content management, and enterprise-grade security features. The platform excels at handling complex content structures across multiple channels, with built-in AI automation and a robust app marketplace for extending functionality. Its worldwide CDN and audit logs make it suitable for large organizations with strict compliance requirements.

### Standout Features of Hygraph
- **GraphQL API**: Advanced content relationships and flexible querying
- **AI & Automation**: Built-in AI capabilities for content generation and workflow automation
- **Enterprise Security**: SSO integration, audit logs, and role-based permissions

## The Final Verdict
Choose Storyblok if you need a visual, marketing-friendly CMS with easy collaboration tools and e-commerce capabilities. It's perfect for teams where marketers need to create content without developer intervention.

Choose Hygraph if you're building enterprise applications requiring complex content modeling, AI-powered workflows, and advanced GraphQL APIs. It's ideal for organizations needing scalable, secure content infrastructure with sophisticated data relationships.
