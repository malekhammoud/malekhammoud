# Lemon Squeezy vs Mollie: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Lemon Squeezy dominates for SaaS founders with its all-in-one platform and global tax compliance, while Mollie excels for European businesses needing in-person payments and local banking integration.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Lemon Squeezy** | **Mollie** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | 5% + 50¢ per transaction | €24.95 |
| **Best For** | SaaS subscription management | European e-commerce & POS |
| **Core Strength** | All-in-one SaaS platform | European payment ecosystem |

## Deep Dive: Lemon Squeezy
Lemon Squeezy is engineered as a complete SaaS operating system, not just a payment processor. Its architecture handles everything from tax compliance to license key management in one unified platform. The 5% + 50¢ per transaction model eliminates monthly fees, making it ideal for startups and indie developers. Built-in AI fraud prevention and failed payment recovery work automatically, reducing churn without manual intervention. Multi-currency support and global tax compliance mean you can sell worldwide without legal headaches.

### Standout Features of Lemon Squeezy
- **Global Tax Compliance**: Automatic VAT, GST, and sales tax collection across 150+ countries
- **License Key Management**: Built-in software licensing for digital products and SaaS
- **AI-Powered Recovery**: Automated failed payment retries with machine learning optimization

## Deep Dive: Mollie
Mollie targets European businesses with deep banking integrations and local payment method support. The €24.95 starting price positions it as a premium solution for established businesses rather than startups. Its strength lies in the European payment ecosystem—supporting iDEAL, Bancontact, and SEPA Direct Debit alongside credit cards. The iOS app enables contactless payments anywhere, making it perfect for physical retail and events. Mollie's API-first approach appeals to developers who want granular control over payment flows.

### Standout Features of Mollie
- **European Payment Methods**: Native support for region-specific payment options
- **In-Person Payments**: iOS app enables contactless payments on the go
- **Fast Funding**: Next-day payouts to European bank accounts

## The Final Verdict
Choose Lemon Squeezy if you're running a SaaS business that needs subscription management, tax compliance, and an all-in-one platform without monthly fees.

Choose Mollie if you're operating in Europe, need local payment methods, want in-person payment capabilities, or prefer direct banking integrations with faster payouts.
