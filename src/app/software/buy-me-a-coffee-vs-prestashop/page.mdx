## Deep Dive: Buy Me a Coffee
Buy Me a Coffee is a lightweight, creator-focused platform built for rapid deployment and audience monetization. Its architecture emphasizes simplicity—no hosting, no technical setup—making it ideal for artists, writers, and content creators who want to accept support or memberships without managing infrastructure. The platform handles payments, content delivery, and supporter data ownership, with instant payouts and multi-currency support baked in.

### Standout Features of Buy Me a Coffee
- **Instant setup**: Deploy in under a minute without technical expertise
- **Creator ownership**: 100% supporter data ownership, no email spam
- **Shop integration**: Built-in storefront for selling products alongside memberships

## Deep Dive: PrestaShop
PrestaShop is an open-source, self-hosted e-commerce platform designed for businesses requiring full control over their online store. Its modular architecture supports thousands of themes and extensions, enabling deep customization from product catalogs to checkout flows. Built for scalability, PrestaShop handles stores from small catalogs to enterprise-level operations with millions in revenue, backed by dedicated support and extensive documentation.

### Standout Features of PrestaShop
- **Full data ownership**: Complete control over store data and customer information
- **Modular ecosystem**: Thousands of modules and themes for unlimited customization
- **Enterprise scalability**: Handles growth from hundreds to millions in revenue

## The Final Verdict
Choose Buy Me a Coffee if:
- You're a creator needing quick support/membership tools
- You want instant setup without technical overhead
- You prioritize data ownership and simple monetization

Choose PrestaShop if:
- You need a fully customizable online store
- You're building a scalable e-commerce business
- You require complete control over your store's architecture and data
