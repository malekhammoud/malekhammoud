## Deep Dive: Calendly
Calendly is a sophisticated scheduling platform built for B2B workflows, featuring a robust calendar integration layer that syncs with Google, Outlook, and Office 365. Its architecture supports multi-host routing, round-robin distribution, and event type branching—critical for sales teams and customer-facing roles. The platform's API-first design enables webhook automation, custom embeddable widgets, and admin-level team management, making it enterprise-ready for organizations scaling meeting operations.

### Standout Features of Calendly
- **Meeting Routing**: Smart distribution to available team members
- **Event Types**: Multiple meeting templates with custom durations and buffers
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Conversion tracking and team performance metrics

## Deep Dive: World Time Buddy
World Time Buddy operates as a lightweight, browser-based time zone visualization tool with a minimalist API footprint. Its core strength lies in real-time clock rendering across multiple locations, using JavaScript-based time calculations without requiring backend infrastructure. The platform's drag-and-drop interface allows users to overlay meeting times across continents, making it ideal for distributed teams coordinating asynchronously. No authentication layer means instant access, though this limits enterprise control.

### Standout Features of World Time Buddy
- **Multi-Zone Visualization**: Simultaneous display of global times in a single view
- **Quick Scheduling**: Click-to-copy meeting times with timezone offsets
- **No-Login Access**: Immediate functionality without account creation

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Calendly if...**
- You need automated scheduling with calendar sync
- Your team requires meeting analytics and routing
- You want branded scheduling pages and integrations

**Choose World Time Buddy if...**
- You're coordinating across multiple time zones
- You need a free, instant-time comparison tool
- You don't require calendar integration or booking automation
