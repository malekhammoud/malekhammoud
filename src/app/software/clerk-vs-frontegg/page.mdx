# Clerk vs Frontegg: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** **Clerk wins for B2C apps** needing robust user management at scale, while **Frontegg excels for B2B SaaS** requiring tenant-level role management.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Clerk** | **Frontegg** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free for up to 50,000 monthly retained users; Pro plans start at $20/month billed annually. | $0/month |
| **Best For** | B2C user management | B2B SaaS scaling |
| **Core Strength** | Seamless user experience | Multi-tenant architecture |

## Deep Dive: Clerk

Clerk delivers a polished, developer-friendly authentication platform optimized for B2C applications. Its architecture centers on a unified user model with comprehensive profile management, making it ideal for consumer-facing apps that need smooth onboarding and engagement. The platform shines with its generous free tier (50,000 MRU) and enterprise-grade security features like bot protection and account lockout mechanisms.

Clerk's strength lies in its **developer experience**—pre-built UI components, extensive SDKs, and webhook-driven workflows that reduce implementation time. The platform particularly excels at handling high-volume consumer traffic with features like magic links, SMS OTPs, and social authentication that prioritize user convenience without sacrificing security.

### Standout Features of Clerk
- **50,000 free MRU tier** - Massive free tier for scaling consumer apps
- **Magic links & SMS OTPs** - Frictionless authentication options
- **Bot protection & account lockout** - Enterprise-grade security out of the box

## Deep Dive: Frontegg

Frontegg is purpose-built for B2B SaaS applications, offering a low-code CIAM platform with deep multi-tenant capabilities. Its architecture revolves around tenant-level role management and organization structures, making it the go-to solution for SaaS products that need to manage complex customer hierarchies and permissions.

The platform's **multi-tenant-first approach** distinguishes it from competitors. Features like tenant-level roles, in-app roles components, and advanced fraud protection are designed specifically for SaaS businesses managing multiple customer organizations. The $0 starting price makes it particularly attractive for early-stage B2B startups.

### Standout Features of Frontegg
- **Tenant-level roles & permissions** - Granular access control for B2B scenarios
- **In-app roles component** - Seamless integration of role management into your UI
- **Feature flags** - Control feature rollout per tenant

## The Final Verdict

Choose **Clerk if**:
- Building a B2C application with high user volumes
- Need a generous free tier (50,000 MRU)
- Prioritizing seamless user experience and engagement

Choose **Frontegg if**:
- Developing a B2B SaaS product with multiple customers
- Require tenant-level role management and organization structures
- Need a completely free starting point for your auth infrastructure
