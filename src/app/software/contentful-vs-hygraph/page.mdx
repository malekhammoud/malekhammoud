# Contentful vs Hygraph: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Contentful wins for enterprise AI-powered personalization, Hygraph wins for budget-conscious teams needing a solid free tier.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Contentful** | **Hygraph** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $15/month | $0 |
| **Best For** | Enterprise content personalization | Cost-effective headless CMS |
| **Core Strength** | AI-driven DXP platform | GraphQL-first architecture |

## Deep Dive: Contentful
Contentful positions itself as a Digital Experience Platform (DXP) with AI at its core. The platform integrates agentic analytics directly into workflows, enabling real-time content optimization and personalization. Its visual assembly tools let teams create complex experiences without deep technical knowledge, while the $15/month starting price unlocks enterprise-grade features including advanced analytics, A/B testing, and extensive app integrations. Contentful's architecture supports massive scale through partnerships and enterprise solutions.

### Standout Features of Contentful
- **Agentic Analytics**: AI-powered insights delivered directly in your workflow for real-time optimization
- **Visual Experience Builder**: Drag-and-drop assembly of complex digital experiences at scale
- **AI Content Automation**: Eliminate busywork with AI-driven content creation and workflow automation

## Deep Dive: Hygraph
Hygraph takes a GraphQL-first approach to headless CMS, offering a completely free tier that includes core features like worldwide CDN, audit logs, and SSO integration. The platform excels at content architecture with its federated content graph, making it ideal for teams that need complex content relationships without vendor lock-in. Hygraph's app marketplace extends functionality while maintaining developer-friendly tooling and roles & permissions. The GraphQL API provides precise data fetching capabilities that developers love.

### Standout Features of Hygraph
- **GraphQL Native**: Type-safe API queries with precise data fetching and reduced over-fetching
- **Content Federation**: Unified content graph across multiple sources without duplication
- **Free Tier Advantage**: Complete feature set including security, audit logs, and CDN at $0

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Contentful if:**
- You need AI-powered content personalization and analytics
- Enterprise features and scalability are priorities
- Budget isn't a constraint for DXP capabilities

**Choose Hygraph if:**
- You want a free headless CMS with GraphQL
- Developer experience and type safety matter most
- You need content federation across multiple sources
