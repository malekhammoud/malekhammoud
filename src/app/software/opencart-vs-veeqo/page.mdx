# OpenCart vs Veeqo: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** OpenCart wins for full-stack e-commerce stores; Veeqo wins for shipping and inventory automation.

## Deep Dive: OpenCart
OpenCart is a PHP-based, open-source e-commerce platform built for merchants who want full control over their online store. Its modular architecture supports over 13,000 extensions and themes, making it highly customizable for niche or scaling businesses. With built-in SEO tools and multi-store capabilities, it's ideal for developers and entrepreneurs who need a tailored storefront without recurring SaaS fees.

### Standout Features of OpenCart
- **Open-source core**: No licensing costs, full codebase access
- **Marketplace ecosystem**: 13K+ modules for payments, shipping, analytics
- **Built-in SEO**: Optimized URLs, metadata, and sitemap generation

## Deep Dive: Veeqo
Veeqo is a cloud-based inventory and shipping management platform designed for high-volume sellers operating across multiple sales channels. It automates stock syncing, label printing, and order routing while integrating with Amazon MCF and major carriers. With pre-negotiated shipping rates and real-time inventory tracking, it's built for operational efficiency rather than storefront creation.

### Standout Features of Veeqo
- **Multi-carrier shipping**: Pre-negotiated rates, label automation
- **Inventory sync**: Real-time updates across channels
- **Amazon integration**: MCF, account protection, streamlined fulfillment

## The Final Verdict
- **Choose OpenCart if** you need a customizable, self-hosted online store with full control over design and functionality.
- **Choose Veeqo if** you're scaling multichannel sales and need automated shipping and inventory management without building a storefront.
