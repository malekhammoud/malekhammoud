# Clerk vs Kinde: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Clerk wins for startups and developers needing a generous free tier and focused authentication; Kinde wins for SaaS teams wanting an all-in-one platform with billing and feature management.

## Deep Dive: Clerk
Clerk is purpose-built for authentication, offering a generous free tier that supports up to 50,000 monthly retained users. Its architecture is optimized for seamless integration, with pre-built UI components, extensive APIs, and robust security features like multi-factor authentication and bot protection. Clerk's modular design allows developers to pick and choose features, making it ideal for both B2C and B2B applications. The platform's focus on authentication ensures top-tier performance and reliability for user management.

### Standout Features of Clerk
- **50,000 MRU Free Tier**: No cost for up to 50,000 monthly retained users, perfect for scaling startups.
- **Advanced Security**: Multi-factor authentication, account lockout protection, and bot detection out of the box.
- **Customizable UI**: Pre-built components and themes that can be tailored to match your app's design.

## Deep Dive: Kinde
Kinde positions itself as a comprehensive SaaS platform, combining authentication with billing, feature management, and access control. Its architecture is designed to handle the full lifecycle of a SaaS product, from user sign-up to subscription management. Kinde's feature flags and billing integration make it a one-stop shop for teams looking to streamline their tech stack. While its pricing starts at $25/month, the all-in-one approach can reduce the need for multiple third-party tools.

### Standout Features of Kinde
- **Billing Integration**: Built-in billing management to monetize your SaaS product from day one.
- **Feature Flags**: Advanced feature management to control rollouts and experiments.
- **SSO & Enterprise**: Enterprise-grade single sign-on (SSO) for B2B applications.

## The Final Verdict
Choose **Clerk** if you're a startup or developer prioritizing a generous free tier, focused authentication, and seamless integration. Choose **Kinde** if you need an all-in-one platform with billing, feature management, and access control to power your SaaS product.
