# Clerk vs Kinde: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Clerk dominates for startups and scale-ups with its generous free tier and developer-first approach, while Kinde wins for teams needing billing and feature management baked into auth.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Clerk** | **Kinde** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free for up to 50,000 monthly retained users; Pro plans start at $20/month billed annually. | $25 USD per month |
| **Best For** | Developer-first authentication | All-in-one SaaS platform |
| **Core Strength** | Generous free tier & UX polish | Billing + feature management |

## Deep Dive: Clerk
Clerk is built for developers who want authentication that "just works" without breaking the bank. Its generous free tier (50K MRU) and unlimited applications make it ideal for startups and side projects. The platform excels at delivering a polished, customizable UI with features like user impersonation, session management, and bot protection baked in. Clerk's architecture is optimized for B2C and B2B apps, with social sign-on, MFA, and magic links ready to deploy.

### Standout Features of Clerk
- **50K Free MRU**: No-cost authentication for high-growth apps
- **User Impersonation**: Debug and support with admin session takeover
- **Bot Protection**: Built-in defenses against credential stuffing and abuse

## Deep Dive: Kinde
Kinde positions itself as a full-stack SaaS enablement platform, not just an auth provider. It bundles authentication with billing management, feature flags, and access control—making it a one-stop shop for product teams launching paid SaaS products. While its $25/month starting price is steeper, Kinde eliminates the need for multiple vendors by integrating subscription management, lead generation, and feature rollout directly into the auth flow.

### Standout Features of Kinde
- **Billing Management**: Built-in subscription and payment handling
- **Feature Flags**: Roll out features progressively with built-in experimentation
- **Enterprise SSO**: SAML/SSO support for enterprise customers

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Clerk if...**
- You need a generous free tier and developer-friendly auth
- Your focus is on user experience and customization
- You're building B2C or B2B apps without complex billing needs

**Choose Kinde if...**
- You want billing, feature management, and auth in one platform
- You're launching a paid SaaS product quickly
- Enterprise SSO and advanced access control are priorities
