# Help Scout vs Intercom: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Help Scout wins for SMBs needing HIPAA-compliant, human-centric support; Intercom dominates for AI-powered, enterprise-scale customer engagement.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Help Scout** | **Intercom** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $25 per user/mo | N/A |

| **Core Strength** | Human-AI hybrid support | Fin AI Agent, omnichannel |

## Deep Dive: Help Scout
Help Scout delivers a balanced stack of AI and human-powered tools, optimized for teams that need to scale without losing the personal touch. Its shared inbox architecture centralizes communications, while HIPAA compliance makes it ideal for healthcare, finance, and regulated industries. The platform's strength lies in its workflow automation, customer profiles, and 100+ integrations that plug into existing CRMs and helpdesks.

### Standout Features of Help Scout
- **AI Drafts & Answers**: AI-generated responses that maintain brand voice and accuracy
- **HIPAA Compliance**: Enterprise-grade security for regulated industries
- **Multi-Channel Inbox**: Unified view of email, chat, and social interactions

## Deep Dive: Intercom
Intercom's Fin AI Agent is purpose-built for handling complex queries across any helpdesk or channel. Its architecture is designed for high-volume, enterprise environments where speed and automation are critical. The platform consolidates customer service into a single view, reducing agent workload and improving response times through deep AI integration.

### Standout Features of Intercom
- **Fin AI Agent**: Industry-leading AI that resolves complex queries autonomously
- **Omnichannel Support**: Seamless customer experience across web, mobile, and social
- **Unified Customer View**: Single pane of glass for all customer interactions

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Help Scout if...**
- You need HIPAA-compliant support for healthcare or finance
- Your team values human-AI collaboration over full automation
- You're an SMB looking for predictable per-user pricing

**Choose Intercom if...**
- You require enterprise-grade AI automation for complex queries
- Your business operates at high volume across multiple channels
- You want a single platform to consolidate all customer interactions
