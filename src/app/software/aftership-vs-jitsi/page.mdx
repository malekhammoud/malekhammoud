> **TL;DR:** AfterShip dominates post-purchase logistics while Jitsi crushes real-time video conferencing—choose based on your workflow, not buzzwords.

## Deep Dive: AfterShip
AfterShip is a post-purchase automation platform built for eCommerce brands needing carrier-grade tracking and returns management. Its architecture centers on 1,282 carrier integrations and real-time webhook delivery, making it ideal for high-volume merchants. The platform's AI-powered delivery predictions and automated workflows reduce customer service overhead while branded tracking pages maintain consistent UX.

### Standout Features of AfterShip
- **1,282 carrier integrations** with real-time tracking updates
- **AI delivery predictions** that reduce WISMO (Where Is My Order) tickets
- **Automated returns workflows** with fraud prevention and printerless QR codes

## Deep Dive: Jitsi
Jitsi is a WebRTC-native video conferencing stack designed for developers building custom communication solutions. Its architecture leverages Jitsi Videobridge for SFU (Selective Forwarding Unit) routing, enabling scalable multi-party video without mixing audio/video streams. The open-source model supports self-hosting on Ubuntu/Debian, making it perfect for enterprises needing HIPAA or GDPR compliance without vendor lock-in.

### Standout Features of Jitsi
- **SFU architecture** with simulcast support for bandwidth optimization
- **Jigasi integration** enabling SIP/H.323 gateways and telephony
- **Jibri recording** for YouTube live streaming and session recording

## The Final Verdict
Choose AfterShip if you need enterprise-grade post-purchase tracking, automated returns, and carrier integrations for your eCommerce store.

Choose Jitsi if you're building custom video conferencing, need self-hosted compliance, or want WebRTC-compatible communication infrastructure.
