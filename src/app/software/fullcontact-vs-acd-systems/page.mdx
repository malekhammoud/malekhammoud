## Deep Dive: FullContact
FullContact is engineered for B2B marketers who need to identify anonymous website visitors and convert them into actionable leads. Its deterministic identity resolution technology maps customer journeys across devices in real-time, providing businesses with professional contact information at the exact moment of purchase intent. The platform's architecture is built for scale, offering browsing analytics and real-time alerts that integrate seamlessly into existing CRM workflows.

### Standout Features of FullContact
- **Website Visitor Identification**: Reveal anonymous visitors with professional contact details
- **Real-Time Journey Mapping**: Track cross-device behavior with deterministic matching
- **Intent-Based Alerts**: Get notified when high-value prospects are actively evaluating

## Deep Dive: ACD Systems
ACD Systems is a powerhouse in creative asset management, offering a comprehensive suite of photo and video editing tools. Built for photographers and creative professionals, the platform combines AI-driven features like Sky Replacement and Selection Tools with robust organization capabilities including facial recognition and RAW editing. Its layered editing and portrait retouching workflows make it a complete digital asset management solution.

### Standout Features of ACD Systems
- **AI-Powered Editing**: Smart selection tools and sky replacement for creative workflows
- **Facial Recognition**: Automated organization of photo libraries by people
- **RAW Photo Editing**: Professional-grade editing with layered capabilities

## The Final Verdict
**Choose FullContact if:**
- You need to identify and convert anonymous B2B website visitors
- Real-time lead alerts and journey mapping are critical
- Deterministic identity resolution across devices is required

**Choose ACD Systems if:**
- You're managing large libraries of digital photos and videos
- AI-powered editing and facial recognition are priorities
- You need professional RAW photo editing capabilities
