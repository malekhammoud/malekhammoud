> **TL;DR:** Mollie wins for European SMBs needing simple, transparent pricing; Razorpay dominates for Indian startups requiring comprehensive financial solutions.

## Deep Dive: Mollie
Mollie offers a streamlined payment gateway focused on European markets with transparent, per-transaction pricing. Built for SMBs, it provides essential payment processing features without complex enterprise contracts. The platform emphasizes developer-friendly APIs and fast payouts, making it ideal for businesses that need reliable, straightforward payment processing without unnecessary complexity.

### Standout Features of Mollie
- **Optimized Checkout**: Mobile-first payment flows with minimal friction
- **Fast Funding**: Next-day payouts to improve cash flow
- **Developer Resources**: Comprehensive API documentation and community support

## Deep Dive: Razorpay
Razorpay positions itself as an all-in-one financial platform beyond just payments, targeting the Indian market with a comprehensive suite of business financial tools. Beyond standard payment processing, it offers payroll automation, business banking, and compliance management. The platform supports 100+ payment methods and provides integrated financial services designed to scale with growing businesses from startup to enterprise.

### Standout Features of Razorpay
- **Automated Payroll**: Built-in payroll processing with compliance automation
- **Business Banking**: Integrated accounts and financial management tools
- **100+ Payment Methods**: Extensive local payment method support for Indian market

## The Final Verdict
Choose Mollie if you're a European SMB needing transparent pricing, fast payouts, and simple payment processing without enterprise contracts.

Choose Razorpay if you're an Indian startup requiring comprehensive financial solutions including payroll, banking, and compliance alongside payment processing.
