## Deep Dive: Braintree

Braintree, a PayPal company, delivers enterprise-grade payment infrastructure with native PayPal and Venmo support. Its SDKs provide seamless mobile checkout experiences with single-touch payments, while PCI compliance and adaptive fraud tools handle security at scale. The platform processes in 50+ currencies with global payouts, making it ideal for businesses expanding internationally.

Developers benefit from comprehensive REST APIs and platform-specific SDKs (iOS, Android, React Native) that abstract complex payment flows. The recurring billing engine supports sophisticated subscription models with add-ons and tiered pricing, while real-time reporting gives finance teams granular visibility into transaction data. Braintree's architecture excels at high-volume, cross-border e-commerce where PayPal's network effects matter.

### Standout Features of Braintree
- **PayPal/Venmo Integration**: Direct access to PayPal's 400M+ user network
- **Adaptive Fraud Tools**: Machine learning-based risk scoring with customizable rules
- **Global Currency Support**: 50+ currencies with local acquiring in key markets

## Deep Dive: Razorpay

Razorpay positions itself as India's financial operating system, bundling payment processing with payroll, lending, and business banking. The platform supports 100+ payment methods including UPI, wallets, and international cards, catering to India's diverse payment landscape. Its unified dashboard provides end-to-end visibility from collections to settlements, with instant payouts for cash flow management.

The API-first architecture enables rapid integration with comprehensive documentation and sandbox environments. Razorpay's payroll automation handles statutory compliance (PF, ESI, TDS) while the business banking suite offers current accounts with built-in payment acceptance. This multi-product approach reduces vendor sprawl for Indian businesses seeking consolidated financial operations.

### Standout Features of Razorpay
- **100+ Payment Methods**: UPI, wallets, cards, and netbanking support
- **Automated Payroll**: Compliances and statutory filings handled automatically
- **Instant Settlements**: Real-time fund transfers for improved cash flow

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Braintree if...**
- You need PayPal/Venmo integration for global reach
- Your business operates across multiple countries
- You prioritize enterprise-grade fraud prevention

**Choose Razorpay if...**
- You're building in the Indian market
- You want consolidated financial products (payments + payroll + banking)
- You need support for India-specific payment methods like UPI
