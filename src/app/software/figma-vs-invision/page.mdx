# Figma vs InVision: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Figma dominates collaborative design workflows with real-time editing and AI credits, while InVision offers a broader enterprise platform for product development and team alignment.

## Deep Dive: Figma

Figma is a cloud-native design platform built for speed and collaboration. Its architecture enables real-time multiplayer editing, making it ideal for distributed teams. With unlimited designs in the free tier, 150 AI credits/day, and advanced Dev Mode inspection, Figma streamlines the design-to-development handoff. The platform's responsive design tools and interactive prototypes empower designers to create adaptive, user-centric interfaces. Figma's team libraries and MCP server integration ensure consistency across projects, while its lightweight, browser-based approach eliminates installation overhead.

### Standout Features of Figma
- **Unlimited Designs**: Free tier offers unlimited design files without storage caps
- **AI Credits**: 150/day for free users, 3,000/month for Full license
- **Real-time Collaboration**: Multiplayer editing with comments and live cursors
- **Dev Mode**: Advanced inspection with MCP server for seamless developer handoff

## Deep Dive: InVision

InVision positions itself as an AI-powered visual platform for product development, extending beyond traditional design tools. Its Intelligent Canvas and AI Workflows integrate ideation, prototyping, and delivery into a unified workspace. Features like Roadmaps, Kanban boards, and Timelines cater to cross-functional teams, while Blueprints and Diagrams support technical design and planning. InVision's Enterprise Guard and security features make it suitable for large organizations with strict compliance needs. The platform's breadth—from TalkTrack to Developer Platform—aims to break tool silos and accelerate product launches.

### Standout Features of InVision
- **AI Innovation Workspace**: Central hub for AI-driven product workflows
- **Intelligent Canvas**: Visual workspace for ideation, planning, and delivery
- **Enterprise Guard**: Security and compliance for large organizations
- **Cross-functional Tools**: Roadmaps, Kanban, and Timelines for team alignment

## The Final Verdict

Choose Figma if you need a fast, collaborative design tool with real-time editing and AI-powered features for UI/UX workflows. Opt for InVision if you require an enterprise-grade platform that integrates design, development, and product management into a single AI-powered ecosystem.
