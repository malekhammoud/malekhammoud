## Deep Dive: Freshworks
Freshworks delivers enterprise-grade customer service software with Freddy AI at its core. The platform handles multichannel support across email, chat, phone, and social media with advanced ticket routing and automation. Built for scalability, it includes incident/problem/change management workflows, service catalog, and SLA management—making it ideal for support teams handling high ticket volumes. The analytics dashboards and custom reporting provide real-time insights into team performance and customer satisfaction metrics.

### Standout Features of Freshworks
- **Freddy AI**: Predictive support with automated ticket categorization and response suggestions
- **Advanced Routing**: Intelligent ticket distribution based on agent skills, workload, and priority
- **Service Catalog**: Self-service portal for employees to request IT services and track fulfillment

## Deep Dive: ACD Systems
ACDSee is a digital asset management and creative editing platform, not a CRM. It specializes in photo and video editing with AI-powered tools like sky replacement, facial recognition, and keyword generation. The software excels at RAW photo editing, layered workflows, and batch processing for creative professionals. With organization features including searching and facial recognition, it's designed for photographers and videographers managing large media libraries—completely outside the customer relationship management space.

### Standout Features of ACD Systems
- **AI Keywords**: Automatic image tagging and metadata generation
- **RAW Photo Editing**: Professional-grade editing for unprocessed camera files
- **Facial Recognition**: Smart organization of people across your photo library

## The Final Verdict
Choose Freshworks if you need enterprise customer service software with AI automation, ticket management, and multichannel support for your support team.

Choose ACD Systems if you're a photographer or videographer needing digital asset management and professional editing tools—but don't expect any CRM functionality.
