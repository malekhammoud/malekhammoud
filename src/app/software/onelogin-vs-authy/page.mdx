> **TL;DR:** OneLogin dominates enterprise SSO and IAM; Authy wins for simple, secure 2FA across personal and small business accounts.

## Deep Dive: OneLogin
OneLogin is a full-stack identity and access management platform built for enterprises that need to secure both human and machine identities across workforce, customer, and partner ecosystems. Its extensible IAM architecture supports SSO, advanced directory integration, and granular lifecycle management, making it ideal for organizations with complex identity governance requirements. With features like SmartFactor Authentication, Vigilance AI, and PAM Essentials, OneLogin goes beyond basic MFA to provide adaptive, policy-driven access control.

The platform's strength lies in its ability to unify authentication, authorization, and user provisioning into a single pane of glass. OneLogin Desktop and Virtual LDAP extend identity policies to endpoints and legacy systems, while APIs and Smart Hooks enable deep integration with custom applications. It's engineered for scale, compliance, and resilience—perfect for enterprises that demand zero-trust security without sacrificing user experience.

### Standout Features of OneLogin
- **SmartFactor Authentication**: Risk-based MFA that adapts to user behavior and context
- **Vigilance AI**: Machine learning-driven anomaly detection for real-time threat prevention
- **Identity Lifecycle Management**: Automated provisioning/deprovisioning across connected apps

## Deep Dive: Authy
Authy is a lightweight, user-friendly two-factor authentication app designed to add an extra layer of security to online accounts. Unlike traditional hardware tokens or SMS-based MFA, Authy stores encrypted 2FA tokens in the cloud and syncs them across multiple devices, ensuring users never lose access—even if they lose their phone. Its offline capability and biometric protections (TouchID, PIN, password) make it both secure and convenient for everyday use.

Authy's simplicity is its superpower. It supports a wide range of services, from social media to banking, and offers easy recovery processes that prevent account lockouts. For developers, Authy provides a cost-effective API that scales with usage, making it a popular choice for startups and SMBs that need reliable 2FA without the complexity of enterprise IAM suites.

### Standout Features of Authy
- **Multi-device Sync**: Securely sync 2FA tokens across phones, tablets, and desktops
- **Encrypted Cloud Backups**: Protect tokens with AES-256 encryption and allow seamless recovery
- **Offline Access**: Generate 2FA codes without an internet connection

## The Final Verdict
Choose OneLogin if you're an enterprise needing comprehensive identity governance, SSO, and adaptive MFA across complex ecosystems.

Choose Authy if you want simple, secure 2FA with cloud sync for personal accounts or small business applications.
