## Deep Dive: Adyen
Adyen delivers enterprise-grade payment infrastructure with 99.999% historical uptime, built for businesses processing millions in transactions globally. Its single API architecture supports multiple use cases across 180+ countries, optimizing conversion rates through built-in fraud prevention and payment routing intelligence. The platform excels at handling complex payment flows, multi-currency settlements, and high-volume transaction processing with minimal latency.

### Standout Features of Adyen
- **99.999% uptime SLA** ensuring mission-critical payment reliability
- **Smart payment routing** automatically selects optimal acquiring banks
- **Built-in fraud detection** with machine learning models trained on global data

## Deep Dive: Razorpay
Razorpay positions itself as an all-in-one finance platform tailored for Indian businesses, combining payment processing with payroll automation and business banking. The platform supports 100+ payment methods including UPI, cards, and wallets, with quick integration through REST APIs and SDKs. Its unified dashboard provides real-time analytics and reconciliation, while automated payroll compliance reduces administrative overhead for growing teams.

### Standout Features of Razorpay
- **100+ payment methods** including UPI, cards, and digital wallets
- **Automated payroll & compliance** streamlining HR financial operations
- **Instant settlements** improving cash flow for businesses

## The Final Verdict
Choose Adyen if you need enterprise-scale reliability, global payment reach, and mission-critical uptime for high-volume transactions across multiple countries.

Choose Razorpay if you're an Indian startup or SMB needing comprehensive finance automation, local payment method support, and integrated payroll solutions.
