# Intelli-CTi vs FullContact: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Intelli-CTi dominates for call center CRM integration, while FullContact wins for B2B lead identification and visitor tracking.

## Deep Dive: Intelli-CTi
Intelli-CTi is a specialized Computer Telephony Integration solution engineered for seamless CRM-telephony convergence. Built specifically for Infor CRM environments, it delivers real-time caller identification, one-click dialing, and automatic call logging. The architecture supports multi-site deployments with Citrix/Terminal Server compatibility, making it ideal for enterprise call centers requiring robust telephony integration and rapid implementation.

The solution's dynamic active sidebar and on-screen call display provide agents with immediate customer context during interactions. With multi-call support and in-call note capture capabilities, Intelli-CTi streamlines the entire customer communication workflow while automatically logging call statistics for performance analysis.

### Standout Features of Intelli-CTi
- **CTI Telephony Integration**: Real-time caller ID display with customer data pop-up
- **One-Click Dialing**: Click-to-call functionality from CRM records
- **Multi-Site Support**: Enterprise-ready deployment across multiple locations
- **Chrome Compatibility**: Modern browser support for contemporary workflows

## Deep Dive: FullContact
FullContact is a B2B identity resolution platform focused on website visitor identification and lead intelligence. The platform uses deterministic identity resolution to map customer journeys and provide professional contact information for anonymous visitors. With real-time alerts and cross-device recognition capabilities, FullContact transforms anonymous website traffic into actionable sales intelligence.

The architecture is designed for marketing teams and sales development representatives who need to identify high-intent leads at the moment of evaluation. FullContact's browsing analytics and journey mapping capabilities provide deep insights into prospect behavior, enabling timely and personalized outreach strategies.

### Standout Features of FullContact
- **Website Visitor Identification**: Reveal anonymous B2B website visitors
- **Cross-Device Recognition**: Track users across multiple devices and sessions
- **Journey Mapping**: Visualize complete customer paths and intent signals
- **Real-Time Alerts**: Instant notifications when target accounts visit your site

## The Final Verdict
Choose Intelli-CTi if:
- You need deep CRM-telephony integration for call center operations
- Your team requires one-click dialing and automatic call logging
- You're using Infor CRM and need enterprise-grade CTI capabilities
- Multi-site deployment with Citrix support is essential

Choose FullContact if:
- You need to identify anonymous website visitors for B2B lead generation
- Real-time intent signals and journey mapping are priorities
- Cross-device tracking and professional contact enrichment matter
- Your focus is on marketing intelligence rather than telephony integration
