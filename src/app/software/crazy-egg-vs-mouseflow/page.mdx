## Deep Dive: Crazy Egg
Crazy Egg's architecture centers on conversion optimization with built-in A/B testing and survey capabilities. The platform integrates heatmaps, session recordings, and conversion funnels into a unified dashboard, making it ideal for marketers focused on CRO. Its instant heatmaps and popup CTAs provide immediate visual feedback, while the A/B testing suite allows for rapid experimentation without external tools.

### Standout Features of Crazy Egg
- **A/B Testing Suite** - Built-in experimentation platform with statistical significance tracking
- **Conversion Funnels** - Visual drop-off analysis with detailed step-by-step breakdowns
- **Surveys & Feedback** - Native survey tools for direct user feedback collection

## Deep Dive: Mouseflow
Mouseflow's architecture leverages AI at its core, with Mina AI providing automated insights and friction detection. The platform processes session replays through machine learning to identify UX bottlenecks automatically. Its journey analytics and form analytics modules create comprehensive user behavior maps, while the free forever tier makes it accessible for teams of any size.

### Standout Features of Mouseflow
- **Mina AI** - Automated UX insights and friction point detection
- **Journey Analytics** - Cross-page behavior mapping with drop-off visualization
- **Form Analytics** - Field-level interaction tracking with abandonment analysis

## The Final Verdict
Choose Crazy Egg if:

Choose Mouseflow if:
