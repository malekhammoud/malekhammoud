# Clerk vs Stytch: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** **Clerk wins for rapid B2C integration** with generous free tier, while **Stytch dominates enterprise-grade B2B** with advanced RBAC and SCIM.

## Deep Dive: Clerk

Clerk delivers a **developer-first authentication platform** optimized for speed and user experience. Its architecture centers on pre-built UI components and comprehensive SDKs that enable teams to ship authentication in hours, not weeks. The platform excels at B2C scenarios where conversion rates matter, offering magic links, social auth, and passwordless flows out of the box. With 50,000 free MRUs and unlimited applications, it's particularly attractive for startups scaling their consumer products.

### Standout Features of Clerk

- **50,000 free monthly retained users** with unlimited applications—ideal for consumer apps
- **Pre-built, customizable UI components** that reduce frontend development time by 80%
- **Advanced security stack** including bot protection, account lockout, and device-aware MFA

## Deep Dive: Stytch

Stytch positions itself as an **enterprise-grade identity platform** built for complex organizational needs. Its architecture emphasizes granular access control with full RBAC implementation, SCIM provisioning, and domain-based JIT provisioning—features critical for B2B SaaS serving enterprise customers. The platform's multi-tenancy support and organization-level authentication policies make it the go-to choice for applications requiring sophisticated user management across multiple clients or departments.

### Standout Features of Stytch

- **Enterprise RBAC & SCIM provisioning** for complex organizational hierarchies
- **Domain-based JIT provisioning** enabling automated user creation from enterprise SSO
- **Device-aware MFA with intelligent rate limiting** for enterprise security compliance

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Clerk if:**
- You're building a B2C application and need to ship authentication fast
- Your primary concern is user experience and conversion optimization
- You want generous free tier limits (50,000 MRUs) while scaling

**Choose Stytch if:**
- You're building B2B SaaS for enterprise customers requiring advanced RBAC
- SCIM provisioning and compliance features are non-negotiable
- You need sophisticated multi-tenancy and domain-based user management
