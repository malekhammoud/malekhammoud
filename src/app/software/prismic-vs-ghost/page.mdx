# Prismic vs Ghost: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Choose Prismic for AI-powered visual page building and developer-first workflows, Ghost for content-driven subscription businesses.

## Deep Dive: Prismic
Prismic is a headless CMS built for marketers and developers who need to ship landing pages fast without sacrificing brand consistency. Its slice-based architecture lets teams create reusable content components with visual editing, while automatic TypeScript generation and one-click sync streamline developer workflows. The platform shines for teams building high-converting websites with complex layouts and localization needs.

The visual page builder, combined with AI features and preview capabilities, makes Prismic ideal for content teams who want design freedom without touching code. Its shared slices and slice libraries enable enterprise-grade content reuse across multiple projects, while the local development tool ensures developers can work offline and maintain full control over the build process.

### Standout Features of Prismic
- **Slice Variations**: Create multiple versions of content components for different use cases
- **Auto-Generated Component Structure**: TypeScript types generated automatically from your content models
- **Shared Slices**: Build once, reuse everywhere across projects and teams

## Deep Dive: Ghost
Ghost is a publishing platform designed specifically for professional content creators and subscription businesses. It combines a clean writing interface with built-in membership management, newsletter delivery, and analytics—all optimized for growing a direct-to-audience business. The platform handles everything from content creation to payment processing, making it a complete solution for independent publishers.

With its focus on speed and simplicity, Ghost provides advanced creator tools like dynamic cards, rich media support, and native analytics without the complexity of traditional CMS platforms. The integrated newsletter system and membership features eliminate the need for third-party tools, while the $9/month starter plan makes it accessible for solo creators and small teams.

### Standout Features of Ghost
- **Built-in Newsletters**: Send emails directly from your Ghost publication without external services
- **Membership Management**: Handle subscriptions, payments, and member-only content natively
- **Native Analytics**: Track reader engagement and revenue metrics without additional tools

## The Final Verdict
Choose Prismic if you need a visual, developer-friendly CMS for building landing pages and marketing websites with complex layouts and localization requirements.

Choose Ghost if you're a content creator or publisher focused on building a subscription-based business with integrated newsletters and membership management.
