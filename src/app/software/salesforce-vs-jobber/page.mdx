## Deep Dive: Salesforce
Salesforce is the #1 AI CRM built for enterprise organizations that need unified pricing, price management, and pricing execution. Its Pricing Designer creates pricebooks with volume, tiered, and bundle discounts, while Pricing Waterfall ensures transparent discount application. The platform integrates seamlessly with Quoting and Order Capture components for end-to-end sales automation. Ideal for companies with complex pricing strategies and compliance requirements.

Salesforce's Agentic Enterprise model combines AI, data, and Customer 360 to create intelligent pricing systems that adapt to business rules and market conditions. The unified pricing platform ensures consistency across all sales channels, making it perfect for organizations with multiple product lines and regional pricing variations.

### Standout Features of Salesforce
- **Pricing Designer**: Creates sophisticated pricebooks with complex discount structures
- **Pricing Rules**: Automated volume, tiered, and bundle discount calculations
- **Pricing Waterfall**: Transparent, rule-based discount application across all pricing tiers

## Deep Dive: Jobber
Jobber is purpose-built for home and commercial service businesses that need to quote, schedule, invoice, and get paid in one streamlined platform. Its drag-and-drop calendar makes job scheduling intuitive, while the Client Hub provides customers with self-service portals for booking and payments. The platform includes AI Tools for automation and Jobber Capital for business financing, making it a complete operational solution for field service professionals.

The mobile-first design ensures teams can manage jobs from anywhere, with credit card processing built directly into the workflow. Client Communication tools keep customers informed throughout the service lifecycle, while Marketing Tools help businesses grow their customer base. Perfect for plumbers, electricians, landscapers, and other service professionals who need simple, reliable business management software.

### Standout Features of Jobber
- **Drag-and-drop Calendar**: Visual job scheduling with easy drag-and-drop interface
- **Client Hub**: Self-service customer portal for bookings and payments
- **AI Tools**: Automated scheduling, quoting, and customer communication

## The Final Verdict
Choose Salesforce if you need enterprise-grade CRM with AI-powered pricing automation, complex discount structures, and compliance features for large organizations with sophisticated sales processes.

Choose Jobber if you're a small to medium service business that needs simple scheduling, invoicing, and customer management without the complexity of enterprise CRM systems.
