## Deep Dive: Jitsi
Jitsi is a suite of open-source projects centered around Jitsi Videobridge and Jitsi Meet, delivering WebRTC-compatible video conferencing with advanced routing support for simulcast, bandwidth estimation, and scalable video coding. Its architecture enables direct peer-to-peer video/audio streaming, bypassing the need for server-side mixing, which translates to lower latency and higher quality. With Ubuntu/Debian packages, mobile apps, and features like screen sharing, YouTube streaming, and dial-in/out via Jigasi, Jitsi is ideal for developers seeking a self-hosted, customizable conferencing solution.

### Standout Features of Jitsi
- **Scalable video routing:** Simulcast and bandwidth estimation for optimal performance
- **Open-source flexibility:** Deploy on your own infrastructure with WebRTC standards
- **Advanced integrations:** Dial-in/out, YouTube streaming, and shared document editing

## Deep Dive: Claris
Claris is a unified tech-building platform designed for enterprise teams to create custom applications, automate workflows, and deliver rich web experiences. Built on FileMaker Pro's legacy, it offers database functionality, interface design, reporting, and robust security. Claris Connect enables seamless integration with third-party services, while Claris Studio provides web-based app development. With mobile and network sharing, it's tailored for organizations needing secure, scalable app ecosystems without extensive coding.

### Standout Features of Claris
- **Custom app development:** Build tailored solutions with FileMaker-based tools
- **Workflow automation:** Connect apps and services via Claris Connect
- **Enterprise-grade security:** Built-in data protection and admin controls

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Jitsi if:**
- You need a free, open-source video conferencing solution
- You prioritize WebRTC standards and low-latency streaming
- You want full control over deployment and customization

**Choose Claris if:**
- You require custom business applications with minimal coding
- You need workflow automation and third-party integrations
- You prefer a unified platform with enterprise security and support
