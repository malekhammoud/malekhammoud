## Deep Dive: BigCommerce
BigCommerce is a headless-capable, enterprise-grade commerce platform built for scaling across brands, regions, and channels. Its API-first architecture supports complex B2B workflows, multi-store management, and payment freedom with lower fees. The platform excels at omnichannel selling with built-in integrations for marketplaces, social commerce, and POS systems.

Ideal for mid-market to enterprise businesses, BigCommerce offers headless commerce flexibility alongside traditional hosted solutions. Its robust API ecosystem, advanced B2B features, and global expansion capabilities make it suitable for businesses managing multiple brands or geographic regions from a single backend.

### Standout Features of BigCommerce
- **Headless Commerce Ready**: Full API coverage for custom frontend builds
- **Multi-Store Management**: Centralize operations across brands and regions
- **Advanced B2B Features**: Custom pricing, bulk ordering, and quote management

## Deep Dive: Payhip
Payhip is a streamlined, creator-focused platform designed specifically for selling digital products, courses, and memberships. Its intuitive store builder requires zero technical skills, making it accessible for solo entrepreneurs and content creators. The platform handles VAT & taxes automatically and includes built-in marketing tools and email marketing functionality.

Payhip's strength lies in its simplicity and all-in-one approach to digital commerce. With support for digital downloads, online courses, coaching, memberships, and even physical products, it provides a complete solution for creators who want to monetize their knowledge and content without dealing with complex e-commerce infrastructure.

### Standout Features of Payhip
- **Digital-First Architecture**: Optimized specifically for digital products and courses
- **Built-in Marketing Tools**: Email marketing and promotional features included
- **Tax Automation**: Automatic VAT calculation and compliance handling

## The Final Verdict
Choose BigCommerce if you need enterprise-grade scalability, multi-channel selling, or complex B2B functionality with headless commerce capabilities.

Choose Payhip if you're a creator, coach, or educator who needs a simple, affordable platform to sell digital products, courses, and memberships without technical overhead.
