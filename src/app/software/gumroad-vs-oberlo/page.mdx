## Deep Dive: Gumroad
Gumroad is a minimalist e-commerce platform built for creators who want to sell digital products directly to their audience. Its architecture focuses on simplicity—upload your product, set your price, and share your link. The platform handles payments in multiple currencies and supports one-time, recurring, or fixed-length payments. With automated workflows and a "make your own road" philosophy, Gumroad empowers creators to maintain full control over their sales process without complex infrastructure.

### Standout Features of Gumroad
- **Multi-currency payments**: Customers pay in their local currency automatically
- **Flexible payment models**: One-time, subscriptions, or fixed-duration access
- **Automated workflows**: Built-in email sequences and delivery systems
- **Direct audience connection**: No marketplace mediation between creator and buyer

## Deep Dive: Oberlo
Oberlo is a dropshipping platform designed for entrepreneurs who want to run online stores without handling physical inventory. It integrates with Shopify to source products from suppliers worldwide, automatically syncing inventory and pricing. The platform provides business tools like profit margin calculators and traffic estimators to optimize store performance. Oberlo's strength lies in its ability to scale dropshipping operations—once products are imported, the system handles order fulfillment, packaging, and shipping logistics.

### Standout Features of Oberlo
- **Supplier integration**: Direct connection to AliExpress and other suppliers
- **Inventory automation**: Real-time stock level updates across products
- **Business calculators**: Profit margin, traffic, and pricing optimization tools
- **Educational resources**: Blogs, ebooks, and podcasts for ecommerce success

## The Final Verdict
Choose Gumroad if you're a creator selling digital products, courses, or memberships and want direct control over your audience relationships without marketplace fees.

Choose Oberlo if you're building a dropshipping business focused on physical products and need automated inventory management and fulfillment at scale.
