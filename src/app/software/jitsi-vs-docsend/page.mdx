## Deep Dive: Jitsi
Jitsi is a robust, open-source video conferencing platform built on WebRTC standards. Its architecture centers around Jitsi Videobridge, which routes video and audio streams directly between participants without server-side mixing, resulting in lower latency and better scalability. The platform supports advanced video routing features like simulcast, bandwidth estimation, and scalable video coding, making it ideal for self-hosted deployments that require high-quality, real-time communication. With Ubuntu/Debian packages and mobile apps, Jitsi offers flexibility for developers and organizations seeking customizable video solutions.

### Standout Features of Jitsi
- **Direct P2P routing** via Jitsi Videobridge eliminates server-side mixing for lower latency
- **Advanced video routing** with simulcast, bandwidth estimation, and scalable video coding
- **Full WebRTC compatibility** enabling browser-based video conferencing without plugins

## Deep Dive: DocSend
DocSend is a secure document sharing platform designed for deal management, investor relations, and sensitive project collaboration. Its architecture focuses on providing granular control over document access with features like dynamic watermarking, data rooms, and detailed analytics tracking. The platform excels at monitoring document engagement through comprehensive analytics, including video analytics and participant interaction tracking. With eSignature integration and non-disclosure agreement support, DocSend provides enterprise-grade security for confidential business communications and deal workflows.

### Standout Features of DocSend
- **Dynamic watermarking** automatically protects documents with visible security marks
- **Comprehensive analytics** tracks viewer engagement, time spent, and interaction patterns
- **Integrated eSignature** streamlines document workflows with built-in signing capabilities

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Jitsi if...** you need open-source, customizable video conferencing with direct peer-to-peer routing and WebRTC compatibility for real-time collaboration.

**Choose DocSend if...** you require secure document sharing with advanced analytics, watermarking, and enterprise-grade security for sensitive business communications.
