# Evernote vs Notion: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Evernote dominates for individual note-taking and archival, while Notion wins for collaborative workspaces and knowledge management.

| Feature/Spec | **Evernote** | **Notion** |

## Deep Dive: Evernote

Evernote is a battle-tested note-taking platform built for individual users who need robust document capture and long-term archival. Its Web Clipper and document scanning features create a seamless pipeline from physical to digital, while advanced search capabilities make finding archived content effortless. The platform excels at personal productivity workflows with integrated tasks, calendar sync, and AI-powered organization.

### Standout Features of Evernote
- **Web Clipper**: One-click capture of web pages, articles, and PDFs with full-text search
- **Document Scanning**: High-quality OCR for converting physical documents into searchable digital notes
- **Advanced Search**: Powerful filtering with tags, notebooks, and content-based search across all note types

## Deep Dive: Notion

Notion is a flexible AI workspace designed for teams that need to centralize knowledge and automate workflows. Its Custom Agents work 24/7 to capture information, answer questions, and keep projects moving without manual intervention. The platform's modular approach allows teams to build custom databases, wikis, and project boards that scale with organizational complexity.

### Standout Features of Notion
- **Custom Agents**: AI-powered automation that captures knowledge and answers questions autonomously
- **Enterprise Search**: Unified search across all connected tools and databases
- **Knowledge Base**: Centralized documentation with powerful linking and relational database capabilities

## The Final Verdict

Choose Evernote if you need:
- Individual note-taking with superior document capture
- Long-term archival of physical and digital content
- Advanced search across personal archives

Choose Notion if you need:
- Collaborative team workspaces with shared knowledge
- AI-powered automation for repetitive tasks
- Customizable databases and project management systems
