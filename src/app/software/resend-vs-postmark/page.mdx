# Resend vs Postmark: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Resend wins for startups and developers seeking a free, modern email API with built-in marketing features, while Postmark excels at enterprise-grade deliverability and expert support.

## Deep Dive: Resend
Resend is a developer-first email platform built for the modern web. With a generous free tier and pay-as-you-go pricing, it's perfect for startups and indie hackers who need to send both transactional and marketing emails without breaking the bank. The platform offers a clean, RESTful API, SMTP relay, and built-in audience management tools that make it easy to scale from MVP to production.

Resend's architecture emphasizes simplicity and speed, with features like webhooks for real-time event tracking, template management, and inbound email processing. The platform also includes broadcasts and audience segmentation, making it a compelling all-in-one solution for teams that want to avoid juggling multiple email services.

### Standout Features of Resend
- **Free Tier**: $0/month with generous limits, ideal for testing and small projects
- **Built-in Audiences**: Manage subscribers and send marketing emails without extra tools
- **Modern API**: RESTful, well-documented, and developer-friendly with webhooks

## Deep Dive: Postmark
Postmark is a battle-tested transactional email service trusted by enterprises for its rock-solid deliverability and expert support. Designed with developers in mind, it offers powerful features like message streams for organizing emails by purpose, detailed analytics, and 45 days of message retention for debugging and compliance.

Postmark's architecture is built for reliability, with dedicated infrastructure to ensure emails land in the inbox every time. The platform includes advanced features like inbound email processing, integrations with popular tools, and a team of email experts who can help troubleshoot deliverability issues—making it a top choice for mission-critical applications.

### Standout Features of Postmark
- **Enterprise Reliability**: Proven deliverability with dedicated infrastructure
- **Message Streams**: Organize transactional and marketing emails by purpose
- **Expert Support**: Access to email deliverability experts for troubleshooting

## The Final Verdict
Choose Resend if:
- You're a startup or indie developer on a budget
- You need both transactional and marketing emails in one platform
- You want a modern, easy-to-use API with a generous free tier

Choose Postmark if:
- You need guaranteed deliverability for mission-critical emails
- You want expert support and advanced analytics
- You're sending high volumes and need enterprise-grade reliability
