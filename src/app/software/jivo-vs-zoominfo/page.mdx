# Jivo vs ZoomInfo: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Jivo wins for SMBs and startups needing omnichannel customer engagement; ZoomInfo dominates for enterprise B2B sales teams requiring deep market intelligence.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Jivo** | **ZoomInfo** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $0 | $15,000 |
| **Best For** | SMB customer service | Enterprise B2B sales |
| **Core Strength** | Omnichannel engagement | Market intelligence & intent |

## Deep Dive: Jivo
JivoChat delivers a unified omnichannel communication platform built for SMBs and startups. Its architecture supports real-time messaging across 10+ channels (WhatsApp, Facebook, Telegram, etc.) through a single dashboard. The free tier includes core features like live chat, chatbots, and basic telephony, making it accessible for businesses with limited budgets. Jivo's API enables custom integrations, while its mobile SDKs allow in-app chat functionality. The platform emphasizes speed-to-market with minimal setup overhead.

### Standout Features of Jivo
- **AI Agent & Assistant**: Automated customer interactions with machine learning
- **Jivo Marketing**: Built-in campaign tools for lead generation
- **CRM Integration**: Native sync with popular CRMs and email systems

## Deep Dive: ZoomInfo
ZoomInfo operates as a comprehensive Go-to-Market intelligence platform designed for enterprise sales teams. Its core value lies in proprietary B2B contact databases (over 200M+ profiles) enriched with real-time intent signals and predictive analytics. The platform integrates with sales workflows through automation, conversation intelligence, and buyer journey tracking. ZoomInfo's API supports bulk data enrichment and custom integrations, while its enterprise-grade security meets SOC 2 compliance. The $15,000 entry price reflects its depth of data and enterprise features.

### Standout Features of ZoomInfo
- **Buyer Intent Data**: Real-time signals showing purchase readiness
- **Conversation Intelligence**: AI-powered call analytics and coaching
- **Predictive Modeling**: AI-driven lead scoring and opportunity identification

## The Final Verdict
Choose Jivo if you're an SMB needing affordable omnichannel customer engagement with minimal setup complexity.

Choose ZoomInfo if you're an enterprise B2B sales team requiring deep market intelligence, intent data, and advanced sales automation tools.
