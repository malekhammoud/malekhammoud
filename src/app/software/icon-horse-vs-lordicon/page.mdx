# Icon Horse vs Lordicon: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Icon Horse dominates for automated favicon management with its zero-cost, privacy-first approach, while Lordicon wins for animated icon libraries with extensive customization and commercial licensing.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Icon Horse** | **Lordicon** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free | $8 / month, billed annually |
| **Best For** | Automated favicon workflows | Animated icon libraries |
| **Core Strength** | Privacy-first favicon automation | 41,100+ animated icons |

## Deep Dive: Icon Horse

Icon Horse is a specialized tool designed for developers who need reliable, on-demand favicon retrieval and management. Built with privacy as a core principle, it operates without cookies or tracking, making it ideal for privacy-conscious applications. The platform excels at automating favicon workflows, providing customizable fallbacks when icons aren't available, and offering priority support for critical implementations. Its private mode ensures sensitive projects remain secure, while early access to new features keeps developers ahead of the curve. The fallback icon system guarantees your application always displays appropriate visual indicators, even when source sites lack proper favicons.

### Standout Features of Icon Horse
- **Zero-cost operation** - Completely free with no hidden fees or usage limits
- **Privacy-first architecture** - No cookies or tracking mechanisms implemented
- **Customizable fallbacks** - Intelligent system for handling missing favicons
- **Priority support** - Direct access to developers for critical issues
- **Early feature access** - Beta testing opportunities for cutting-edge functionality

## Deep Dive: Lordicon

Lordicon positions itself as a comprehensive animated icon solution for designers and developers seeking premium visual assets. With a library of 41,100+ animated icons, it caters to web, mobile, and content design projects requiring dynamic visual elements. The platform offers extensive format support including Lottie, GIF, MP4, WebP, PNG, and SVG, ensuring compatibility across all modern platforms. Commercial licensing without attribution requirements makes it particularly attractive for professional projects. Users benefit from unlimited icon collections, collection sharing with up to 5 guests, and unlimited exports. The keep-forever policy means purchased icons remain accessible even after subscription cancellation, providing long-term value for ongoing projects.

### Standout Features of Lordicon
- **41,100+ animated icons** - Massive library covering diverse design needs
- **Multi-format exports** - Lottie, GIF, MP4, WebP, PNG, SVG support
- **Commercial license included** - No attribution required for professional use
- **Unlimited collections & exports** - No restrictions on organization or usage
- **Keep icons forever** - Permanent access after subscription ends

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Icon Horse if:**
- You need automated favicon management for websites or applications
- Privacy compliance is a critical requirement
- Budget constraints demand a completely free solution
- You want priority support for technical implementations

**Choose Lordicon if:**
- Your project requires animated icons with extensive customization
- You need commercial licensing without attribution requirements
- Multi-format export capabilities are essential for your workflow
- You prefer a subscription model with permanent access to purchased assets
