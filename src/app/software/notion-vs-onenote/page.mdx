> **TL;DR:** Notion wins for enterprise teams needing AI automation and centralized knowledge; OneNote dominates in classroom collaboration and educational workflows.

## Deep Dive: Notion
Notion is a comprehensive AI workspace built for modern teams, featuring Custom Agents that automate repetitive tasks, capture institutional knowledge, and provide instant answers. Its architecture combines docs, projects, and enterprise search into a unified platform, with robust integrations and security features for scaling organizations. The $5/month starting price includes AI meeting notes, knowledge base management, and 24/7 automation capabilities that keep workflows moving without manual intervention.

### Standout Features of Notion
- **Custom Agents**: AI-powered assistants that automate tasks and answer questions around the clock
- **AI Meeting Notes**: Automatic transcription and summarization of meetings with action item extraction
- **Enterprise Search**: Full-text search across all connected documents and databases
- **Knowledge Base**: Centralized documentation with version control and collaborative editing
- **Security**: Enterprise-grade permissions, SSO, and data encryption

## Deep Dive: OneNote
OneNote is Microsoft's digital notebook solution optimized for educational environments, featuring Class Notebooks with dedicated personal workspaces for students, content libraries for handouts, and collaboration spaces for group activities. Its architecture mirrors physical notebooks with flexible pages and sections, making it intuitive for teachers and students to organize course materials, create interactive lessons, and provide real-time feedback. The free tier makes it accessible for educational institutions while maintaining deep integration with Microsoft's ecosystem.

### Standout Features of OneNote
- **Class Notebooks**: Dedicated structure with personal, content, and collaboration sections for education
- **Interactive Lessons**: Support for multimedia content, handwriting recognition, and inking tools
- **Real-time Collaboration**: Simultaneous editing and feedback capabilities for classroom activities
- **Microsoft Integration**: Seamless connection with Teams, Outlook, and other Microsoft tools
- **Free Access**: No cost barrier for educational institutions and students

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Notion if...**
- Your team needs AI automation and custom agents for productivity
- You require enterprise search across multiple knowledge sources
- You want a unified workspace for docs, projects, and databases
- Budget allows for paid subscription with advanced features

**Choose OneNote if...**
- You're in education and need classroom-specific features
- Your workflow depends on Microsoft ecosystem integration
- You want free access with robust collaboration tools
- You need flexible digital notebooks for students and teachers
