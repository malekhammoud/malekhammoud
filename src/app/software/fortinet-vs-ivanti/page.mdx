## Deep Dive: Fortinet
Fortinet delivers enterprise-grade network and endpoint security with a focus on threat prevention and secure access. Its architecture centers on FortiClient integration, providing IPsec and SSL VPN capabilities, web filtering through FortiGuard, and zero-trust network access. The platform excels at malware protection with cloud-based detection and scheduled scanning, while FortiAnalyzer support enables centralized logging and compliance reporting.

### Standout Features of Fortinet
- **ZTNA HTTPS Proxy**: Secure remote access with client certificate authentication
- **FortiClient EMS Integration**: Centralized endpoint management and policy enforcement
- **FortiGuard Web Filtering**: Real-time threat intelligence and category-based web control

## Deep Dive: Ivanti
Ivanti offers a unified platform that breaks down IT silos by combining service management, endpoint management, and security into a single AI-powered solution. Its architecture supports autonomous endpoint management with generative AI for ITSM, risk-based patch management, and comprehensive exposure management. The platform extends beyond traditional security to include digital experience management and specialized medical device management capabilities.

### Standout Features of Ivanti
- **Autonomous Endpoint Management**: AI-driven automation for endpoint lifecycle
- **Generative AI for ITSM**: Intelligent service desk automation and resolution
- **Risk-Based Patch Management**: Prioritized patching based on vulnerability exposure

## The Final Verdict
Choose Fortinet if you need robust network security, advanced threat protection, and secure remote access with zero-trust capabilities.

Choose Ivanti if you want unified IT service management with AI-powered endpoint automation and comprehensive exposure management across your entire IT infrastructure.
