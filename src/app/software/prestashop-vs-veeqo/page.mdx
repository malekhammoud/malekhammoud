> **TL;DR:** **PrestaShop** dominates for customizable, scalable online stores; **Veeqo** wins for streamlined shipping and inventory management.

## Deep Dive: PrestaShop
PrestaShop is a self-hosted, open-source e-commerce platform designed for businesses seeking complete control over their online store. Its modular architecture supports thousands of themes and extensions, enabling deep customization for unique branding and functionality. Ideal for developers and growing businesses, PrestaShop scales seamlessly from small catalogs to enterprise-level operations, with full data ownership and a robust API for integrations.

### Standout Features of PrestaShop
- **Modular architecture**: Thousands of modules and themes for endless customization.
- **Scalable infrastructure**: Handles growth from hundreds to millions in revenue.
- **Full data ownership**: Complete control over store data and customer information.

## Deep Dive: Veeqo
Veeqo is a cloud-based shipping and inventory management platform tailored for e-commerce businesses managing multiple sales channels. Its all-in-one software automates order fulfillment, tracks inventory in real-time, and integrates with major carriers and marketplaces like Amazon. Veeqo’s pre-negotiated shipping rates and multi-channel fulfillment tools reduce operational costs and improve efficiency, making it a top choice for logistics-heavy operations.

### Standout Features of Veeqo
- **Multi-channel fulfillment**: Syncs inventory and orders across Amazon, eBay, and more.
- **Automated shipping**: Pre-negotiated rates and label printing in seconds.
- **Real-time inventory tracking**: Data-driven forecasting and reconciliation.

## The Final Verdict
**Choose PrestaShop if…**
- You need a fully customizable, self-hosted e-commerce platform.
- Scalability and full data ownership are critical for your business.
- You have development resources to leverage its modular ecosystem.

**Choose Veeqo if…**
- Shipping and inventory management are your top priorities.
- You operate across multiple sales channels and need centralized control.
- You want to reduce shipping costs with pre-negotiated rates and automation.
