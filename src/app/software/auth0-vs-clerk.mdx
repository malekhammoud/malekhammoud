# Auth0 vs Clerk: Which is the Best Authentication?

Choosing between Auth0 and Clerk can be challenging, as both offer robust authentication and authorization solutions tailored to different needs. In this comparison, we break down their core differences, pricing, and features to help you decide.

## Key Differences

- **Pricing Model**: Auth0 offers a free plan with limited features, while Clerk starts free for 50,000 monthly retained users and scales with premium tiers.
- **Target Audience**: Auth0 is enterprise-focused with broad customization; Clerk is developer-first and streamlined for quick integration.
- **Enterprise Features**: Auth0 includes advanced capabilities like Actions and Machine-to-Machine (M2M) flows; Clerk emphasizes simplicity and built-in security defaults.

## Pricing Comparison

### Auth0
- **Free Plan**: $0/month
  - Limited to core features
  - Good for basic needs or testing

### Clerk
- **Free Plan**: Up to 50,000 monthly retained users
- **Pro Plans**: Starting at $20/month (billed annually)
  - Unlimited applications
  - Enhanced features and support

## Feature Comparison

### Auth0
- ✅ Universal Login
- ✅ Single Sign-On (SSO)
- ✅ Multifactor Authentication (MFA)
- ✅ Actions (customizable workflows)
- ✅ Machine-to-Machine (M2M) tokens
- ✅ Passwordless authentication
- ✅ Breached password detection
- ✅ Token Vault
- ✅ Enterprise-grade security

### Clerk
- ✅ Unlimited applications
- ✅ 50,000 MRU limit per app (free tier)
- ✅ Dashboard seats with user impersonation
- ✅ Complete APIs for sign-up/sign-in flows
- ✅ MFA, social sign-on, and custom session lifetime
- ✅ SMS authentication, email/SMS OTP, magic links
- ✅ Bot protection, account lockout, webhooks
- ✅ User metadata management

## Which Should You Choose?

### **Choose Auth0 if:**
- You need enterprise-level customization and scalability.
- Your workflows require advanced features like Actions or M2M integrations.
- Budget is less of a constraint and you want a broad feature set.

### **Choose Clerk if:**
- You want a quick, developer-friendly setup with built-in best practices.
- You expect high user growth and want a generous free tier.
- Your app benefits from features like magic links, SMS authentication, and out-of-the-box security defaults.

Both platforms excel in their own right—Auth0 is powerful for complex, enterprise use cases, while Clerk is optimized for ease of use and rapid development. Evaluate your exact requirements to make the best choice for your application.
