# Mailgun vs Brevo: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Mailgun dominates for developer-first transactional email with superior deliverability tools, while Brevo wins for SMBs wanting an all-in-one marketing suite.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Mailgun** | **Brevo** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $0/month | $0 |
| **Best For** | Developer-focused email delivery | All-in-one SMB marketing |
| **Core Strength** | Deliverability & analytics | Multi-channel automation |

## Deep Dive: Mailgun

Mailgun is built for developers who need rock-solid email deliverability at scale. Its core strength lies in the email API and SMTP services that give you granular control over sending, tracking, and analytics. With features like email validation, inbox placement testing, and spam trap monitoring, Mailgun ensures your transactional emails actually land in inboxes rather than spam folders. The platform offers dedicated IPs, custom sending domains, and webhook support for real-time event handling—essential for high-volume transactional workflows. SAML SSO and multi-user access with role-based permissions make it enterprise-ready for teams that need security and control.

### Standout Features of Mailgun
- **Email Validation API** - Real-time syntax and deliverability checking before sending
- **Inbox Placement Testing** - Preview how emails render across 50+ providers
- **Dedicated IPs & Domain Authentication** - Full control over sender reputation

## Deep Dive: Brevo

Brevo positions itself as an all-in-one customer engagement platform that happens to include transactional email. Beyond SMTP relay and API sending, it bundles email marketing, SMS campaigns, WhatsApp messaging, live chat, and a built-in CRM. This makes it ideal for small to medium businesses wanting to manage customer relationships across multiple channels from a single dashboard. The Brevo Data Platform unifies customer data for smarter segmentation, while sales automation tools and a loyalty program help convert one-time buyers into repeat customers. With 150+ integrations including Shopify and WordPress, it's designed for businesses that want marketing and transactional messaging under one roof.

### Standout Features of Brevo
- **Multi-Channel Campaigns** - Coordinate email, SMS, and WhatsApp in unified workflows
- **Built-in CRM & Sales Pipelines** - Manage leads and deals alongside messaging
- **Customer Loyalty Integration** - Turn transactions into long-term relationships

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Mailgun if:**
- You need maximum deliverability for transactional emails
- Your team is developer-centric and wants API control
- You require advanced analytics and inbox placement testing

**Choose Brevo if:**
- You want email + SMS + WhatsApp + chat in one platform
- You're an SMB needing marketing automation and CRM
- You prefer an all-in-one solution over specialized tools
