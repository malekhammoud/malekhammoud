# Gumroad vs Sellfy: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Gumroad wins for solo creators needing simplicity; Sellfy dominates for multi-product sellers requiring marketing automation.

## Deep Dive: Gumroad
Gumroad strips e-commerce to its essentials—upload, price, share. Its architecture favors creators who want to sell without store-building overhead. The platform handles payments, VAT, and currency conversion automatically, letting you focus on creation. With no monthly fees, Gumroad takes a percentage per sale, making it ideal for low-volume sellers testing the market.

### Standout Features of Gumroad
- **Pay-what-you-want pricing** - Let customers choose their price
- **One-click checkout** - Frictionless purchasing experience
- **Built-in audience** - Marketplace discovery for new creators
- **Flexible product types** - Digital, physical, subscriptions, and bundles

## Deep Dive: Sellfy
Sellfy builds full-featured stores with marketing tools baked in. Its architecture supports unlimited products, email campaigns, and print-on-demand integrations. The platform's strength lies in scaling sales through automation and analytics. Unlike Gumroad's marketplace approach, Sellfy gives you a standalone store you fully control, complete with customizable templates and abandoned cart recovery.

### Standout Features of Sellfy
- **Unlimited products** - No caps on catalog size
- **Email marketing** - Built-in campaigns and automation
- **Print-on-demand** - Merch integration for physical goods
- **24/7 support** - Priority help for paid plans

## The Final Verdict
Choose **Gumroad if** you're a solo creator testing ideas, want marketplace exposure, or prefer percentage-based pricing over monthly fees.

Choose **Sellfy if** you need a full-featured store, plan to sell multiple products, or require marketing automation and analytics.
