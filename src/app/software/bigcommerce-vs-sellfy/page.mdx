## Deep Dive: BigCommerce
BigCommerce is a headless-capable, enterprise-grade e-commerce platform built for businesses scaling across brands, regions, and channels. Its architecture supports hosted storefronts, headless deployments, or hybrid setups, giving developers flexibility to integrate with custom frontends via robust APIs. The platform emphasizes payment freedom with lower processing fees, improved conversion rates, and streamlined operations to reduce costs and risk. Ideal for mid-market to enterprise teams needing centralized control over complex, multi-region operations.

### Standout Features of BigCommerce
- **Headless & Hosted Flexibility**: Deploy as traditional SaaS or decoupled architecture
- **Multi-Channel Commerce**: Sync inventory and orders across marketplaces, social, and POS
- **Enterprise-Grade APIs**: RESTful and GraphQL APIs with extensive webhook support

## Deep Dive: Sellfy
Sellfy is a lightweight, code-free e-commerce builder tailored for creators, artists, and small businesses selling digital products, subscriptions, or print-on-demand goods. Its intuitive interface allows store setup in minutes with customizable templates, built-in email marketing, and automated workflows. The platform handles secure payments and offers 24/7 support, making it ideal for non-technical users who want to launch quickly without infrastructure overhead. Sellfy’s unlimited product listings and core marketing tools cater to solo entrepreneurs and small teams.

### Standout Features of Sellfy
- **Digital-First Commerce**: Sell downloads, subscriptions, and physical goods seamlessly
- **No-Code Store Builder**: Drag-and-drop customization with zero development required
- **Built-In Marketing**: Email campaigns and automation tools included in all plans

## The Final Verdict
**Choose BigCommerce if:**
- You need enterprise scalability and multi-channel operations
- You require headless commerce or complex API integrations
- You’re managing multiple brands or international expansion

**Choose Sellfy if:**
- You’re a creator selling digital products or subscriptions
- You want to launch a store without coding or technical expertise
- You need a simple, affordable solution with built-in marketing tools
