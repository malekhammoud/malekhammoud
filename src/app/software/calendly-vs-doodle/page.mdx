# Calendly vs Doodle: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Calendly dominates for professional teams needing advanced automation, while Doodle wins for casual group scheduling and simplicity.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Calendly** | **Doodle** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $10/seat/mo | $8.95 per user/month, billed annually |
| **Best For** | Professional meeting automation | Group poll scheduling |
| **Core Strength** | Enterprise workflow integration | Quick consensus building |

## Deep Dive: Calendly
Calendly is engineered as a professional scheduling automation platform with a robust API, webhook system, and deep integration ecosystem. Its architecture supports complex workflows including meeting routing, custom event types, and admin management—making it ideal for sales teams, recruiters, and enterprise environments where scheduling is a critical business process. The platform's mobile apps and browser extensions extend functionality across devices, while analytics provide visibility into scheduling patterns and team performance.

### Standout Features of Calendly
- **Meeting Routing**: Automatically directs meetings to the right team member based on rules and availability
- **Event Types**: Create multiple scheduling scenarios with different durations, buffers, and questions
- **Admin Management**: Centralized control over team scheduling, permissions, and analytics

## Deep Dive: Doodle
Doodle takes a minimalist approach focused on the core problem of finding mutual availability. Its architecture centers around lightweight poll-based scheduling that works without requiring participants to have accounts. The platform excels at group decision-making with features like sign-up sheets and payment collection, making it perfect for casual meetings, event planning, and scenarios where you need quick consensus from multiple parties. Doodle's simplicity reduces friction for one-off scheduling needs.

### Standout Features of Doodle
- **Group Polls**: Create multiple time options and let participants vote on what works
- **Payment Collection**: Accept payments directly through scheduling forms for paid appointments
- **No-Account Required**: Participants can respond without creating accounts or logins

## The Final Verdict
Choose Calendly if you need professional-grade scheduling automation, team management features, and deep integrations with your existing business tools. It's the superior choice for sales teams, customer success, and any scenario where scheduling is a core business function.

Choose Doodle if you're organizing casual meetings, events, or need to quickly find a time that works for a group. Its poll-based approach and simplicity make it ideal for one-off scheduling where you don't need the overhead of professional scheduling workflows.
