# Demand Sage vs Sellfy: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** **Demand Sage wins for scaling enterprises** with advanced analytics and 24/7 support, while **Sellfy dominates for creators** needing a code-free, multi-product platform.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Demand Sage** | **Sellfy** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | $39/month | $22/mo |
| **Best For** | Scaling enterprises | Creators & small sellers |
| **Core Strength** | Advanced analytics & support | Multi-product flexibility |

## Deep Dive: Demand Sage
Demand Sage is built for scaling businesses that need enterprise-grade e-commerce infrastructure. Its tiered pricing unlocks progressively powerful features—from basic analytics in the $39/month plan to advanced reporting, shipping discounts, and priority support at $399/month. The platform shines with its abandoned cart recovery, SSL security, and 24/7 technical support, making it ideal for businesses transitioning from SMB to enterprise operations. Demand Sage's architecture prioritizes reliability and scalability, with robust API integrations for inventory management and real-time analytics dashboards.

### Standout Features of Demand Sage
- **Advanced Analytics**: Real-time reporting and business intelligence dashboards
- **Enterprise Support**: 24/7 priority support with dedicated account managers
- **Scalability**: Three-tier plans designed for growth from startup to enterprise

## Deep Dive: Sellfy
Sellfy targets creators and small businesses who need to launch fast without technical overhead. Its code-free store builder lets users sell digital products, physical goods, and subscriptions from a single dashboard. The platform's strength lies in its flexibility—supporting unlimited products, print-on-demand integration, and built-in email marketing tools. Sellfy's architecture is optimized for simplicity, with one-click payment integrations (PayPal, Stripe) and customizable templates that require zero development time. Perfect for artists, educators, and entrepreneurs who value speed over enterprise features.

### Standout Features of Sellfy
- **Multi-Product Support**: Sell digital, physical, and subscription products simultaneously
- **Print-on-Demand Integration**: Built-in POD capabilities for merchandise
- **Email Marketing**: Core marketing tools included in all plans

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Demand Sage if...**
- You're scaling beyond $100K annual revenue
- You need advanced analytics and reporting
- 24/7 enterprise support is non-negotiable

**Choose Sellfy if...**
- You're a creator or small business owner
- You need to sell multiple product types (digital + physical)
- You want to launch in minutes without coding
