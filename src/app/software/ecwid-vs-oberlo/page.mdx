# Ecwid vs Oberlo: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** **Ecwid wins for multi-platform e-commerce with POS integration; Oberlo wins for dropshipping automation and business planning tools.**

## Deep Dive: Ecwid
Ecwid is a comprehensive e-commerce platform designed for businesses seeking to sell across multiple channels. Its architecture supports seamless integration with websites, social media, and marketplaces, making it ideal for merchants who want a unified sales presence. With built-in POS capabilities, Ecwid caters to both online and offline retail, offering inventory and order management, automated taxes, and shipping solutions. The platform's customizable themes and marketing tools empower users to build a brand without technical expertise, while the App Market extends functionality through third-party integrations.

### Standout Features of Ecwid
- **Multi-platform selling**: Sell on websites, social media, and marketplaces from one dashboard.
- **POS integration**: Manage in-store and online sales with unified inventory.
- **App Market**: Extend capabilities with integrations for accounting, CRM, and more.

## Deep Dive: Oberlo
Oberlo is tailored for entrepreneurs entering the dropshipping space, providing a streamlined platform to launch and manage online stores without handling inventory. Its core value lies in business planning tools, such as the Profit Margin Calculator and Business Name Generator, which guide users through the early stages of e-commerce. Oberlo also offers educational resources like blogs, ebooks, and podcasts, making it a strong choice for those new to dropshipping. While it lacks the multi-channel selling and POS features of Ecwid, Oberlo excels in simplifying product sourcing and order fulfillment through supplier integrations.

### Standout Features of Oberlo
- **Business planning tools**: Profit Margin Calculator, Business Name Generator, and more.
- **Educational resources**: Access to blogs, ebooks, videos, and podcasts for dropshipping success.
- **Supplier integration**: Automate product sourcing and order fulfillment.

## The Final Verdict
- **Choose Ecwid if**: You need multi-platform selling, POS integration, and advanced e-commerce features for both online and offline sales.
- **Choose Oberlo if**: You're focused on dropshipping, want business planning tools, and prefer a simplified platform for product sourcing and fulfillment.
