# Lemon Squeezy vs Braintree: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Lemon Squeezy wins for indie SaaS founders needing an all-in-one platform, while Braintree dominates for enterprise-scale merchants requiring deep customization and global reach.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Lemon Squeezy** | **Braintree** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | 5% + 50¢ per transaction | 1.9% + 0.30 EUR |

## Deep Dive: Lemon Squeezy
Lemon Squeezy is built as a comprehensive SaaS operating system rather than just a payment processor. The platform bundles payment processing, tax compliance, fraud prevention, and subscription management into a single dashboard. Its AI-powered fraud protection and failed payment recovery work out of the box without developer configuration. The no-code checkout forms and built-in tax collection make it ideal for solo founders and small teams who want to launch quickly without managing multiple vendor relationships.

### Standout Features of Lemon Squeezy
- **Global tax compliance** automatically handles VAT, GST, and sales tax across 100+ jurisdictions
- **AI fraud prevention** blocks suspicious transactions before they hit your revenue
- **Failed payment recovery** automatically retries declined charges and notifies customers

## Deep Dive: Braintree
Braintree is PayPal's developer-first payment gateway engineered for high-volume, complex payment scenarios. The platform excels at supporting 130+ currencies and 130+ payment methods including Venmo, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Its PCI-compliant infrastructure and adaptive fraud tools give enterprise teams granular control over risk management. The comprehensive SDKs for iOS, Android, and web platforms enable custom checkout experiences that match brand requirements. Braintree's reporting tools provide real-time analytics for finance teams tracking global transactions.

### Standout Features of Braintree
- **130+ payment methods** including regional options like iDEAL and Alipay
- **Developer SDKs** for iOS, Android, and web with extensive customization options
- **Global payouts** in 50+ currencies with same-day settlement available

## The Final Verdict
Choose Lemon Squeezy if:
- You're an indie developer or small team launching a SaaS product
- You want tax compliance and fraud protection handled automatically
- You prefer an all-in-one platform over piecing together multiple services

Choose Braintree if:
- You're processing high-volume transactions across multiple countries
- You need support for niche payment methods specific to your market
- You have development resources to customize the checkout experience
