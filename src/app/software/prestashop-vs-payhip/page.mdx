## Deep Dive: PrestaShop
PrestaShop is a self-hosted, open-source e-commerce platform built for businesses that need complete control over their online store. With thousands of modules and themes, it offers unparalleled customization options and can scale from small shops to enterprise-level operations handling millions in revenue. The platform provides full ownership of store data and a dedicated support team, making it ideal for merchants who want to build a long-term, customizable e-commerce presence without vendor lock-in.

The modular architecture allows developers to extend functionality through PHP-based modules, while the responsive design ensures compatibility across devices. PrestaShop's marketplace offers thousands of add-ons for payment processing, shipping, marketing, and inventory management. The platform includes built-in SEO features, multi-store management, and advanced analytics, making it suitable for businesses that need sophisticated e-commerce capabilities and plan to grow significantly.

### Standout Features of PrestaShop
- **Full data ownership** - Complete control over customer data and store information
- **Thousands of modules** - Extensive marketplace for extending functionality
- **Scalable architecture** - Handles growth from hundreds to millions in revenue
- **Dedicated support** - Professional assistance and extensive documentation

## Deep Dive: Payhip
Payhip is a hosted e-commerce solution designed specifically for creators, authors, and small businesses selling digital products. The platform eliminates technical barriers with its intuitive store builder, allowing users to launch in minutes without coding knowledge. Payhip handles VAT and taxes automatically, integrates with multiple payment gateways, and includes built-in marketing tools and email marketing capabilities. The all-in-one approach makes it perfect for individuals who want to focus on creating content rather than managing infrastructure.

The platform's strength lies in its simplicity and creator-focused features. Payhip supports digital downloads, online courses, coaching services, memberships, and even physical products through a unified dashboard. The $5.95 starting price includes hosting, security, and payment processing, making it cost-effective for small-scale operations. Payhip's built-in audience features and affiliate program help creators expand their reach without additional marketing tools.

### Standout Features of Payhip
- **Digital-first design** - Optimized specifically for selling digital products and courses
- **All-in-one dashboard** - Unified interface for products, customers, and marketing
- **Automatic tax handling** - Built-in VAT and tax calculations for global sales
- **Built-in marketing tools** - Email marketing and affiliate program included

## The Final Verdict
**Choose PrestaShop if:**
- You need a fully customizable e-commerce platform
- You plan to scale beyond basic digital product sales
- You want complete ownership and control of your store data
- You have technical resources or budget for development

**Choose Payhip if:**
- You're primarily selling digital products or courses
- You want to launch quickly without technical setup
- You need automatic tax handling and built-in marketing tools
- You prefer predictable pricing with minimal maintenance
