# Bitrix24 vs Freshworks: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Bitrix24 dominates for SMBs needing an all-in-one platform, while Freshworks excels for enterprises requiring AI-powered customer service automation.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Bitrix24** | **Freshworks** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free forever for unlimited users | $19/agent/month, billed annually |
| **Best For** | SMBs & all-in-one teams | Enterprise customer service |
| **Core Strength** | Unified platform with unlimited users | AI-powered automation & analytics |

## Deep Dive: Bitrix24
Bitrix24 delivers a unified platform combining CRM, project management, communication, and automation in a single interface. Its architecture supports unlimited users at no cost, making it ideal for growing teams that need to scale without per-user licensing fees. The platform integrates sales, marketing, HR, and collaboration tools with a built-in website builder and online store capabilities. Bitrix24's modular design allows teams to activate only the features they need while maintaining data consistency across departments.

### Standout Features of Bitrix24
- **Unlimited User Model**: Scale your team without per-user costs
- **Built-in Website Builder**: Create and host websites within the platform
- **Social Selling Integration**: Connect sales efforts across social networks
- **HR Automation Suite**: Manage employee lifecycle and performance tracking

## Deep Dive: Freshworks
Freshworks specializes in AI-powered customer service with enterprise-grade automation capabilities. The platform leverages Freddy AI for intelligent ticket routing, predictive analytics, and automated responses. Its architecture is built for high-volume support environments with advanced SLA management, multi-channel support, and custom reporting. Freshworks' microservices-based design enables rapid deployment of new features and seamless third-party integrations through its extensive marketplace.

### Standout Features of Freshworks
- **Freddy AI Engine**: Intelligent automation and predictive support
- **Advanced Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time insights and custom reporting
- **Multi-channel Support**: Unified inbox for email, chat, phone, and social
- **SLA Management**: Automated compliance tracking and escalation

## The Final Verdict
**Choose Bitrix24 if:**
- You need an all-in-one platform with unlimited users
- Budget constraints require a free starting option
- Your team needs integrated project management and collaboration tools

**Choose Freshworks if:**
- You require AI-powered customer service automation
- Enterprise-level SLA management is critical
- Your focus is on high-volume, multi-channel customer support
