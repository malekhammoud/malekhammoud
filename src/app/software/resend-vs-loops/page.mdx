# Resend vs Loops: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Resend dominates for pure transactional email delivery with superior deliverability, while Loops wins for integrated marketing + transactional workflows.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Resend** | **Loops** |

## Deep Dive: Resend
Resend is engineered for developers who need bulletproof transactional email delivery. Built on a modern infrastructure with intelligent queueing and automated DNS handling, it focuses on getting emails into inboxes, not spam folders. The platform excels at high-volume transactional sends with its robust Email API, SMTP support, and webhook integrations. Resend's architecture prioritizes speed and reliability, making it ideal for SaaS applications, e-commerce order confirmations, and password resets where deliverability is mission-critical.

### Standout Features of Resend
- **Email API & SMTP**: Dual delivery options for maximum flexibility
- **Intelligent Queueing**: Smart delivery optimization for peak performance
- **Automated DNS Handling**: Zero-config setup with DKIM/SPF/DMARC management

## Deep Dive: Loops
Loops positions itself as a comprehensive email platform that unifies transactional, marketing, and product emails under one roof. Its doc-like email crafting interface and reusable components make it accessible to both developers and marketers. The platform shines with its automated customer journey building and complete DNS handling, while intelligent queueing ensures deliverability. Loops is perfect for teams wanting to consolidate their email stack—handling everything from transactional notifications to complex marketing campaigns without juggling multiple tools.

### Standout Features of Loops
- **Unified Interface**: Single platform for all email types
- **Journey Builder**: Automated customer journey creation
- **Reusable Components**: Save and scale email themes effortlessly

## The Final Verdict
Choose **Resend** if you need:
- Pure transactional email delivery with maximum deliverability
- Developer-first tooling with robust APIs and webhooks
- Simple, focused solution without marketing bells and whistles

Choose **Loops** if you need:
- All-in-one platform for transactional + marketing emails
- Visual journey building and automated workflows
- Unified interface for both technical and non-technical team members
