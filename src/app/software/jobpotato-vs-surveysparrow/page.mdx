# JobPotato vs SurveySparrow: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** JobPotato dominates for developers and privacy-conscious teams needing unlimited forms at $0, while SurveySparrow excels at enterprise-grade feedback orchestration with AI-powered insights.

## Deep Dive: JobPotato
JobPotato (Tally.so) delivers a developer-friendly, no-code form builder with a laser focus on privacy and simplicity. Built with GDPR compliance and European hosting, it's engineered for teams that can't compromise on data sovereignty. The platform's unlimited forms and submissions at zero cost makes it a standout for bootstrapped startups and individual creators. Its technical stack supports calculated fields, conditional logic, and custom CSS, giving developers the flexibility to build sophisticated forms without leaving the browser. File uploads, payment collection, and multi-page forms round out a feature set that punches above its weight class.

### Standout Features of JobPotato
- **Unlimited forms & submissions**: No caps or paywalls, even at $0
- **Calculated fields & conditional logic**: Build dynamic forms without code
- **GDPR compliance & EU hosting**: Enterprise-grade privacy baked in

## Deep Dive: SurveySparrow
SurveySparrow positions itself as a Voice-of-Customer (VOC) platform, not just a form builder. Its architecture is designed for enterprises that need to capture feedback across WhatsApp, web, mobile apps, and email—then synthesize it with AI for actionable insights. The platform's response rate optimization (up to 40% higher) and reputation management tools make it ideal for brands serious about customer experience. SurveySparrow's ticketing integration and multi-channel feedback loops create a closed-loop system for issue resolution and brand trust building. While pricing isn't transparent, the feature depth suggests an enterprise-tier positioning.

### Standout Features of SurveySparrow
- **AI-powered insights**: Extract actionable patterns from feedback at scale
- **Multi-channel capture**: WhatsApp, web, mobile apps, email in one platform
- **Reputation management**: Proactively build brand trust with review responses

## The Final Verdict
**Choose JobPotato if...**
- You need unlimited forms and submissions at $0
- Privacy compliance (GDPR) is non-negotiable
- You want a simple, developer-friendly form builder

**Choose SurveySparrow if...**
- You're building an enterprise VOC program
- Multi-channel feedback collection is critical
- You need AI-powered insights and reputation management
