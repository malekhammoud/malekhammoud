## Deep Dive: PrestaShop
PrestaShop is a self-hosted, open-source e-commerce platform built for merchants who want full ownership of their online store's data, design, and infrastructure. Its modular architecture allows developers to customize every aspect—from checkout flows to inventory management—using thousands of available modules and themes. Scalable from hundreds to millions in revenue, PrestaShop supports complex product catalogs, multi-store setups, and integrations with ERP systems. The dedicated support team and extensive documentation make it a developer-first choice for long-term e-commerce growth.

### Standout Features of PrestaShop
- **Full Data Ownership**: No vendor lock-in; export and migrate your store anytime
- **Modular Architecture**: Extend functionality with 5,000+ modules and themes
- **Enterprise Scalability**: Built to handle high-traffic, high-volume stores
- **Developer-Friendly**: Open-source codebase with REST API and webhooks

## Deep Dive: Oberlo
Oberlo is a dropshipping platform designed for entrepreneurs who want to launch an online store without managing inventory, packaging, or shipping logistics. It integrates seamlessly with Shopify, allowing users to import products directly from suppliers and automate order fulfillment. Beyond its core dropshipping tools, Oberlo offers business calculators (profit margin, traffic, pricing), content resources (blogs, ebooks, podcasts), and naming tools to help users optimize their store's performance. Ideal for beginners, it removes the operational overhead of traditional e-commerce.

### Standout Features of Oberlo
- **Automated Fulfillment**: Orders sync directly with suppliers for hands-off shipping
- **Product Import Tools**: One-click import from AliExpress and other marketplaces
- **Business Calculators**: Profit Margin, Traffic, and Pricing Strategy tools built-in
- **Content Library**: Access to blogs, ebooks, videos, and podcasts for growth

## The Final Verdict
**Choose PrestaShop if...**
- You need complete control over your store's code and data
- You plan to scale to enterprise-level operations
- You want to avoid subscription fees and vendor lock-in

**Choose Oberlo if...**
- You're starting a dropshipping business with minimal upfront investment
- You want to avoid inventory management and fulfillment headaches
- You're comfortable with Shopify's ecosystem and pricing model
