# Clerk vs WorkOS: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Clerk dominates for B2C apps with generous free tiers, while WorkOS is the enterprise SSO powerhouse for B2B SaaS.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Clerk** | **WorkOS** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free for up to 50,000 monthly retained users; Pro plans start at $20/month billed annually. | Free for the first 1 million active users; $2500/month for each additional million. |
| **Best For** | B2C apps & startups | Enterprise B2B SaaS |
| **Core Strength** | User management & B2C features | Enterprise SSO & Directory Sync |

## Deep Dive: Clerk

Clerk is a developer-first authentication platform built for rapid B2C deployment. Its architecture centers on a complete user management system with built-in UI components, making it ideal for consumer-facing applications. The generous 50K MRU free tier and $20/month Pro plan provide exceptional value for startups and growing apps. Clerk's strength lies in its comprehensive feature set for user engagement: magic links, social authentication, SMS OTP, and bot protection are all standard. The platform excels at reducing time-to-market with pre-built flows and components that handle everything from sign-up to profile management.

### Standout Features of Clerk
- **50K MRU Free Tier**: Massive free allowance for startups and growing apps
- **Built-in UI Components**: Pre-built sign-in, sign-up, and profile management flows
- **Advanced Security**: Bot protection, account lockout, and multi-factor authentication out of the box

## Deep Dive: WorkOS

WorkOS is engineered specifically for enterprise B2B SaaS, focusing on making applications enterprise-ready with minimal integration effort. Its architecture prioritizes enterprise features like SSO, Directory Sync, and Audit Logging, with pricing structured for high-volume B2B applications. The platform's strength is its deep enterprise integrations—supporting SAML, OAuth, and OIDC for SSO across major identity providers. WorkOS's pricing model (free for 1M active users) is designed for B2B SaaS where a single enterprise customer might have hundreds or thousands of users. The platform also includes advanced features like Role-Based Access Control and Fine-Grained Authorization, essential for enterprise security compliance.

### Standout Features of WorkOS
- **Enterprise SSO**: SAML, OAuth, and OIDC support for all major identity providers
- **Directory Sync**: Automated user provisioning and deprovisioning
- **Audit Logging**: Comprehensive compliance-ready logging for enterprise requirements

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Clerk if:**
- You're building a B2C application or consumer-facing app
- You need generous free tiers and want to minimize authentication costs
- You want pre-built UI components and user management features

**Choose WorkOS if:**
- You're targeting enterprise B2B customers who require SSO
- You need Directory Sync and Audit Logging for compliance
- You expect high user volumes from enterprise accounts
