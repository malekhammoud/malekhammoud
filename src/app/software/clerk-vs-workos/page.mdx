# Clerk vs WorkOS: The Ultimate Comparison

> **TL;DR:** Clerk wins for startups and SMBs needing comprehensive auth with generous free tier; WorkOS dominates for enterprise-ready apps requiring SSO and directory sync.

## At a Glance Comparison

| Feature/Spec | **Clerk** | **WorkOS** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting Price** | Free for up to 50,000 monthly retained users; Pro plans start at $20/month billed annually. | Free for the first 1 million active users; $2500/month for each additional million. |
| **Best For** | B2C & SMB apps | Enterprise & B2B apps |
| **Core Strength** | All-in-one auth platform | Enterprise SSO & directory sync |

## Deep Dive: Clerk
Clerk delivers a comprehensive authentication platform built for developers who need complete user management without enterprise complexity. Its architecture centers around a unified dashboard with built-in user impersonation, session management, and security features like bot protection and account lockout. The generous 50K MRU free tier makes it ideal for consumer-facing apps and growing startups that need robust auth without enterprise pricing.

### Standout Features of Clerk
- **50K free MRU tier** - Most generous free tier in the category
- **Built-in user impersonation** - Debug production issues without staging accounts
- **Multi-channel auth** - Email/SMS OTP, magic links, social auth, and MFA
- **Bot protection & account lockout** - Enterprise-grade security without enterprise cost

## Deep Dive: WorkOS
WorkOS positions itself as the enterprise-ready auth layer, specializing in Single Sign-On (SSO) and directory synchronization. Its architecture is built around making applications enterprise-ready through SAML/SSO integration, SCIM directory sync, and audit logging. The platform shines when you need to integrate with corporate identity providers like Okta, Azure AD, or Google Workspace, making it the go-to choice for B2B SaaS targeting enterprise customers.

### Standout Features of WorkOS
- **Enterprise SSO (SAML)** - Seamless integration with corporate identity providers
- **Directory Sync (SCIM)** - Automatic user provisioning and deprovisioning
- **Audit Logging** - Comprehensive compliance and security tracking
- **Role-Based Access Control** - Fine-grained authorization for enterprise use cases

## The Final Verdict

**Choose Clerk if:**
- You're building B2C or SMB apps needing comprehensive auth
- Budget matters and you want enterprise features at startup prices
- You need built-in user management without additional tooling

**Choose WorkOS if:**
- You're targeting enterprise customers requiring SSO
- Directory sync and SCIM provisioning are must-haves
- Audit logging and compliance are non-negotiable requirements
